Questions Virginia shoppers ask

Where do Virginia residents sign up for Marketplace coverage in 2027?

Virginia operates its own state-based exchange, Virginia's Insurance Marketplace (marketplace.virginia.gov), instead of HealthCare.gov. Federal advance premium tax credits, cost-sharing reductions, and Virginia Premium Savings state financial assistance apply only to qualified health plans bought through Virginia's Insurance Marketplace.

What are the 2027 open enrollment dates in Virginia?

Open Enrollment on Virginia's Insurance Marketplace for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 through January 29, 2027. Complete your enrollment or plan update by December 31 for coverage starting January 1, 2027.

What is Virginia Premium Savings for 2027?

Virginia Premium Savings is a state-funded affordability program for 2027 that layers on top of federal premium tax credits for eligible Virginia residents earning between 138% and 250% of the federal poverty level. You must complete or update your Marketplace.Virginia.gov application during Open Enrollment because state savings are not applied automatically.

Can an agent in Lakeland, Florida help me with a Virginia plan?

Yes, provided the agent holds an active Virginia producer license and Virginia Insurance Marketplace certification. I hold a Virginia nonresident license #1136622. Reviews happen by phone and screen share from Lakeland, Florida, and you keep full control of your account login.

What does a plan review cost?

There is no separate agent fee for the plan-review assistance described on this site. If you enroll in a plan, the insurer may pay me a commission. I compare plans from the insurers I'm appointed with in Virginia, which may not include every insurer on Virginia's Insurance Marketplace.

How do Cardinal Care Medicaid and Virginia's Insurance Marketplace work together?

Virginia expanded Medicaid under Cardinal Care for eligible adults ages 19 to 64 with household incomes at or below 138% of the federal poverty level ($22,025 single / $45,540 family of four in 2026). Applications run year-round through coverva.dmas.virginia.gov and commonhelp.virginia.gov, and your state Medicaid agency makes the final eligibility decision.
