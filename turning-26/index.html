Age-off coverage review

Turning 26 is a coverage deadline, not a plan recommendation.

David can help review when parent-plan coverage ends, whether employer coverage is available, student status if relevant, ZIP code, projected income, and tax-household questions that may need confirmation with a tax professional.

Start a turning-26 reviewCall 863-640-3102

What to gather

Coverage termination date, employer coverage availability, ZIP code, projected income, student status, and household or tax-dependency questions.

Losing eligibility under a parent's plan may create a Special Enrollment Period. Confirm the actual coverage-end date and application deadline before choosing an effective date.

How to compare

Compare employer coverage and ACA Marketplace options by provider network, prescriptions, premium, deductible, and maximum out-of-pocket—not premium alone.

Marketplace household and tax questions can affect eligibility. David can flag the issue, but tax treatment should be confirmed with a tax professional.

Employer coverage first

Ask the employer for the enrollment deadline, employee contribution, dependent contribution, deductible, network, and coverage effective date. An offer of affordable employer coverage can affect Marketplace financial-help eligibility, so compare the actual offer before applying.

Confirm the handoff

Do not cancel or assume the parent plan ended until the plan administrator confirms the termination date. After selecting replacement coverage, keep the enrollment confirmation, initial premium record, carrier ID information, and any Marketplace document request together.

Before enrollment, use the provider and prescription check. If the parent plan is ending, the coverage-loss guide explains the documents and dates to gather.

Dental and vision coverage may also end with the parent plan. Review those benefits separately; they do not determine eligibility for major-medical coverage or Marketplace financial assistance.

Common paths to compare

  • Employer coverage if available.
  • ACA Marketplace plans and subsidy eligibility.
  • Short-term options only when appropriate.
  • Dental, vision, or supplemental needs.

Plan availability, eligibility, and effective dates vary by circumstance and ZIP code.
