Frequently Asked Questions

How does a CHOICE Arrangement (ICHRA) eliminate small-group participation minimums?

Traditional small-group health plans in Florida typically require 70% eligible employee participation and lock every worker into one carrier network. An Individual Coverage HRA (ICHRA) has zero minimum participation threshold, letting businesses with 2 to 50 W-2 employees set a fixed monthly pre-tax allowance per employee class while each worker selects their own individual plan.

Can 1099 subcontractors participate in a W-2 CHOICE Arrangement (ICHRA)?

No. Federal IRS ICHRA rules apply to eligible W-2 employees. However, Lakeland Health Insurance provides a clean, separate 1099 subcontractor individual referral lane so independent contractors can review ACA Marketplace or individual plans directly without sharing personal medical or tax data with the hiring business.

How do optional Accident and Hospital Indemnity add-ons work for field crews?

Optional Accident and Hospital Indemnity supplemental policies pay scheduled cash benefits directly to the insured for covered injuries or inpatient admissions to help offset major-medical deductibles. Fixed-indemnity and supplemental cash-benefit policies are not comprehensive health insurance or ACA minimum essential coverage.
