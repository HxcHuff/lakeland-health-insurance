Florida coverage transition

Use the Medicaid end date—not the letter date—to build the next coverage timeline.

A person who loses or will soon lose Medicaid or CHIP may qualify for a Marketplace Special Enrollment Period. The Marketplace application and eligibility notice determine the available enrollment window and any document requirements.

Direct answer: Keep the state notice, record the last day of Medicaid or CHIP, update or create the HealthCare.gov application promptly, enter current household and income facts, and follow every eligibility-notice deadline. Do not wait for outreach before applying.

Applicability

  • Florida residents losing or denied Medicaid or CHIP.
  • 2026 coverage and planning for 2027 coverage.
  • Enrollment timing and eligibility come from the current Marketplace notice.

A missing-document closure, denial, and eligibility loss can follow different workflows. This page does not decide which applies.

Documents to keep

  • State Medicaid or CHIP decision and coverage-end date.
  • Marketplace eligibility notice and document request.
  • Current household and projected annual income support.
  • Exact doctors, prescriptions, pharmacies, and preferred facilities.

Coverage-gap checklist

  1. Apply before coverage ends when possible.
  2. Use the application’s Medicaid/CHIP questions accurately.
  3. Select a plan only after checking network and formulary details.
  4. Pay the first premium and confirm effectuation with the carrier.

Do not treat an application as active coverage

An application, eligibility result, plan selection, or payment receipt can be a separate checkpoint. Confirm the effective date and active status with the carrier. Marketplace savings depend on the final application facts; they are not guaranteed by a preliminary estimate.
