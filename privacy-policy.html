Section 01

Who we are and what this policy covers

Lakeland Health Insurance is the public-facing business name used by David Huff, a licensed Florida insurance broker (FL License #W371813; NPN 18213932), through HealthMarkets. Lakeland Health Insurance is not an insurance carrier, a government agency, or the federal Health Insurance Marketplace.

We help individuals, families, and small businesses evaluate insurance options, including ACA Marketplace, Medicare Advantage, Medicare Part D, Medicare Supplement, fixed-indemnity, dental, vision, life, and other ancillary insurance products.

This policy applies to information handled through this website, requested phone or video conversations, text messages, email, in-person meetings, enrollment support, and third-party platforms used to provide those services. David provides local assistance in Lakeland and Polk County and remote assistance across Florida. Product and plan availability still depends on ZIP code, county, eligibility, appointment or certification where applicable, and plan year.

Important distinction: General website inquiries are not secure enrollment channels. Do not send a Social Security number, Medicare ID, financial account number, or detailed medical records through a general contact form, ordinary email, or text message unless you are specifically directed to an approved secure workflow.

Medicare and TPMO notice

We do not offer every plan available in your area. Currently we represent 10 organizations which offer 73 products in your area. Please contact Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE, or your local State Health Insurance Program to get information on all of your options.

Company inventory note: HealthMarkets/Connecture displayed 10 organizations and 73 products for the selected 2026 Lakeland/Polk County service area on August 17, 2026. Those figures are not CMS counts or statewide Florida totals. Counts and available products vary by ZIP code, service area, plan year, and current company authorization. Confirm the ZIP code and current approved platform inventory before relying on these figures. Plan availability, benefits, networks, formularies, pharmacies, and costs are subject to the applicable plan documents and service area.

For personal marketing appointments involving Medicare Advantage, Medicare prescription drug, or Medicare cost plan products, the plan, agent, or broker must agree upon and record the Scope of Appointment at least 48 hours before the scheduled appointment. Through September 30, 2026, the 48-hour waiting period has two CMS exceptions: a Scope of Appointment completed during the last four days of a valid election period for the beneficiary, or an unscheduled in-person meeting initiated by the beneficiary. An inbound telephone call is not one of these exceptions. The Scope of Appointment records the product types the beneficiary agrees to discuss and does not require enrollment.

Section 02

Information we collect

The information collected depends on how you interact with us and which service you request.

Contact and identity Name, address, email address, telephone number, date of birth, household details, and identity-verification information.

Section 03

How information is used

We may use information to:

  • Respond to an inquiry and provide requested insurance information.
  • Compare plan availability, benefits, provider networks, formularies, eligibility, and estimated costs.
  • Support applications, enrollment, renewals, policy service, and coverage changes.
  • Verify identity, authorization, eligibility, and required consent.
  • Schedule appointments and communicate about an active request or existing coverage.
  • Maintain compliance records, prevent fraud, protect systems, and meet legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Measure website performance, diagnose technical issues, and improve user experience.
  • Send marketing communications through channels for which the required permission exists; each channel remains subject to its own opt-out process.
Section 04

How information is shared

We disclose information only when reasonably necessary for an authorized service, business operation, or legal obligation.

Insurance and enrollment organizations

Information may be provided to insurance carriers, Medicare plan sponsors, ACA Marketplace issuers, enrollment platforms, and related organizations to obtain plan information, process an authorized application, confirm enrollment, or service coverage.

HealthMarkets

HealthMarkets may receive information for carrier appointments, compliance oversight, enrollment support, commission administration, quality review, and related field marketing organization functions.

Service providers

Vendors may process information for website hosting, analytics, customer relationship management, scheduling, email delivery, SMS delivery, call recording, fraud prevention, data security, and other operational functions. These providers are expected to use information for the contracted purpose and protect it appropriately.

Government, legal, and safety purposes

Information may be disclosed to CMS, HealthCare.gov, state insurance regulators, law enforcement, courts, or other authorities when authorized by you, required by law, necessary to protect legal rights or safety, or needed to respond to valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers or unrelated marketers. We do not share Medicare beneficiary data with another TPMO for marketing without the authorization required by applicable CMS rules. Access to insurance information is not conditioned on agreeing to third-party data sales.

Section 05

Phone, text, and email communications

Phone calls

When you request a call or separately agree to telephone contact, we may call about that request or the insurance products and services covered by your permission. Consent to marketing contact is not a condition of purchasing an insurance product. You may ask us not to call, and channel-specific consent may be revoked using the contact methods below.

Medicare call recording

CMS requires TPMOs to record Medicare marketing, sales, and enrollment calls, including the audio portion of web-based calls, in their entirety. Calls that do not involve marketing, sales, or enrollment are not subject to that CMS recording requirement. A recording disclosure is provided when applicable. Covered recordings are retained as required by CMS, the applicable plan sponsor, or other governing requirements.

Text and SMS messages

If you separately opt in to SMS, messages may concern an insurance request, appointment reminders, policy information, or other subjects described at opt-in. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile information is not shared for third-party marketing: We do not sell, rent, or transfer a mobile number, SMS opt-in status, or text-messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for their own promotional or marketing purposes. Providers that help deliver or administer the SMS program may process mobile information only for those operational purposes.

Review the complete SMS Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for supported keywords, opt-out instructions, carrier disclaimers, and program-specific data practices.

Email

Commercial email includes sender identification, a valid postal address, and an unsubscribe method as required by the CAN-SPAM Act. Requests to stop marketing email are processed within the legally required period. Transactional or relationship messages about an application, enrollment, policy, or requested service may continue when needed.

Section 06

Cookies, analytics, and advertising technology

Production pages may use first-party storage, Google Analytics, Google advertising tags, Meta technologies, and similar tools to understand traffic, measure interactions, attribute campaigns, maintain functionality, and evaluate advertising performance. Depending on the tool and configuration, these technologies may process IP address, device and browser data, page activity, approximate location, referral data, and conversion events.

For United States visitors, these measurement and advertising technologies may initialize without a separate cookie-consent banner where permitted by applicable law. This does not authorize analytics collection of form contact details, health information, policy details, provider or prescription information, household income, or free-text responses.

You can limit cookies using browser controls, use available platform advertising settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can also review Meta ad preferences. Blocking cookies or scripts may affect some website functions.

Browser privacy signals and opt-out mechanisms are handled where required by applicable law and supported by the relevant technology. Because requirements and technical standards change, contact us if you want to confirm how a specific request applies to your information.

Section 07

Security practices

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed for the nature of the information handled. These measures include encrypted HTTPS transmission for this website, access restrictions, secure service providers and enrollment platforms, authentication controls where available, and procedures for handling sensitive records.

No transmission, platform, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a breach involving Florida residents triggers notice obligations under the Florida Information Protection Act, notice will be provided as expeditiously as practicable and within the period required by law, subject to legally permitted delays or exceptions.

Insurance and health-related information is protected under the laws and contractual requirements that apply to the specific organization, transaction, and data. This policy does not represent that every record handled by Lakeland Health Insurance is protected health information subject to HIPAA.

Section 08

Data retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, contract, carrier, and legal requirements. Retention periods vary by record type:

  • Medicare records: Medicare marketing, sales, and enrollment call recordings, Scope of Appointment records, permission-to-contact documentation, and other Medicare records are retained for the period required by CMS, the applicable plan sponsor, HealthMarkets or carrier requirements, and other governing law. Under the CMS requirements in effect through September 30, 2026, recorded calls that pertain to sales or enrollment are subject to a 10-year retention requirement; other record types may have different retention periods.
  • Enrollment and policy records: Retained according to carrier, regulatory, contractual, and policy-service requirements.
  • Communication consent and opt-out records: Retained as needed to document permission, honor preferences, resolve disputes, and meet applicable requirements.
  • Inquiry and prospect records: Retained while an inquiry is active and for a reasonable follow-up and compliance period, unless a longer period is required or a valid deletion request applies.
  • Website and analytics data: Retained according to configured provider settings and legitimate measurement, security, and operational needs.

When information no longer needs to be retained, it is deleted, de-identified, or disposed of using measures appropriate to the record and system.
