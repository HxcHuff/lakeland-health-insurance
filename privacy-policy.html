Information we collect

Depending on what you choose to submit, a first-party form may collect your name, contact details, Florida ZIP code or county, coverage type, household or eligibility context, requested contact channel, and optional provider or prescription information. Required and optional fields are identified on the form.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, Medicare numbers, payment-card information, full medical records, account passwords, or carrier-portal credentials through a general website form. Enrollment or policy servicing may require a separate secure carrier, Marketplace, or authorized system.

The site may also receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device details, page path, referrer, timestamp, campaign parameters, and cookie or platform identifiers.

How information is used

  • Respond to the insurance-help request you submitted.
  • Route a request to an appropriate first-party workflow and maintain necessary service records.
  • Review Florida coverage options and follow up through channels you authorized.
  • Protect the site, investigate errors or abuse, and measure page and conversion performance.
  • Meet applicable insurance, Marketplace, Medicare, tax, recordkeeping, legal, and compliance obligations.

Submitting a request does not enroll you, confirm eligibility, guarantee savings, or guarantee that a provider, prescription, carrier, or plan is available.

Consent and communications

The Get Help form records the consent text version, page, and submission time. A request to receive assistance is separate from optional authorization for phone calls, SMS, service email, or marketing email. Selecting one channel does not authorize another, and email permission is not copied into SMS permission.

  • Calls and SMS: only when separately selected and a matching number is provided. Message and data rates may apply. Frequency varies.
  • Service email: operational follow-up about the submitted request.
  • Marketing email: optional and separate. New subscriptions use a confirmation step before marketing email is activated.

Consent is not a condition of purchasing insurance. You may withdraw channel consent by replying STOP to an SMS, using an email unsubscribe link, or contacting David. A withdrawal applies prospectively and may not eliminate communications required to complete or document an existing request.

Mobile information and SMS consent are not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Forms, analytics, and advertising measurement

First-party forms may be processed through Netlify services and delivered to business systems used to respond to the request. Marketing-email details are sent to the email platform only when the separate marketing-email authorization is selected.

The site uses analytics and advertising measurement services to understand visits and non-identifying conversion events. The implementation is designed not to place raw form values such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, ZIP codes, provider names, prescriptions, income, or coverage details into analytics or advertising event parameters. Providers may still process technical data such as IP address, browser details, cookies, and platform identifiers under their own terms.

You can limit cookies through browser controls and applicable platform settings. Blocking measurement technologies may reduce attribution accuracy but should not prevent you from using the primary contact methods.

When information may be disclosed

Information may be disclosed to service providers that support hosting, form processing, security, communications, customer relationship management, scheduling, analytics, or required compliance work; to carriers, marketplaces, or government systems when you direct or authorize the related insurance activity; or when required to protect rights, prevent fraud, respond to lawful process, or meet legal obligations.

Service providers receive only the information reasonably necessary for the task and are subject to their own contractual and legal obligations. Lakeland Health Insurance does not promise that every website vendor is a HIPAA business associate; use the designated secure channel when protected health information is required.

Medicare communications

Medicare marketing, sales, and enrollment communications are handled under current CMS requirements. For plan year 2027, CMS removed the prior 48-hour waiting period for a Scope of Appointment but still requires the Scope of Appointment before a personal marketing appointment; in-person appointments require written documentation, and applicable marketing, sales, and enrollment calls must be recorded. Requirements can change, so the current CMS guidance controls.

Security and retention

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed secure. Records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected and for applicable insurance, marketplace, carrier, legal, security, tax, and compliance requirements. Retention periods vary by record type and governing requirement.

If you believe information was sent through the wrong channel or may be at risk, contact David promptly at 863-640-3102.

Your choices and policy changes

You may ask what contact information is associated with your website request, request a correction, or ask that information be deleted when retention is not required. Identity verification may be necessary before completing a request. Legal or operational requirements may limit deletion.

This policy may be updated when practices or requirements change. The effective date on this page identifies the current posted version.
