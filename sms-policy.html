Plain English: We text you about your insurance — quotes, appointments, policy updates. We don't spam. You can stop messages any time by replying STOP. Agreeing to texts is never required to work with us.

1. Program Description

Lakeland Health Insurance (operated by David Huff, FL License #W371813, NPN 18213932, through HealthMarkets) operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with clients and prospects regarding insurance-related matters. Messages sent through this program may include:

  • Insurance quotes and plan comparison information
  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Policy updates, renewal reminders, and coverage changes
  • Open enrollment deadlines and special enrollment period alerts
  • Application status updates
  • Responses to inquiries you initiate via text
  • Informational content about ACA, Medicare, and supplemental coverage options

Not a condition of purchase: Consent to receive SMS messages is never required to obtain insurance quotes, enroll in a plan, or receive services from Lakeland Health Insurance. You may opt out at any time without affecting your coverage or our ability to serve you.

2. How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from us in the following ways:

  • Checking an SMS consent box on any form on our website
  • Texting a keyword to our business number
  • Verbally or in writing requesting to communicate by text during a consultation
  • Providing your mobile number during the enrollment process and indicating you wish to receive text updates

By opting in, you confirm that you are the authorized user of the mobile number provided and that you are at least 18 years of age.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your relationship with us and your coverage needs. You may receive:

  • 1–3 messages per month during routine service periods
  • More frequent messages during open enrollment (November–January) or if you initiate a conversation
  • Transactional messages (appointment reminders, application updates) as needed

We do not send bulk promotional blasts. Every message we send is relevant to your insurance situation or initiated by your inquiry.

4. Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply to messages you send and receive. These charges are billed by your mobile carrier and are separate from any costs associated with your insurance coverage. Lakeland Health Insurance does not charge any fee for participation in this SMS program.

If you are unsure whether your carrier plan includes unlimited texting, contact your carrier directly before opting in.

5. Supported Keywords

The following keywords are supported at any time by replying to any message from our number:

STOP Unsubscribe from all messages immediately
HELP Receive assistance and contact information
INFO Receive program information and contact details
CANCEL Alternative opt-out command (same as STOP)
QUIT Alternative opt-out command (same as STOP)
END Alternative opt-out command (same as STOP)

After replying STOP: You will receive one final confirmation message and then no further messages from our program. Your opt-out will be honored immediately. You may re-subscribe at any time by texting START or by contacting us directly.

6. How We Use Your Mobile Number

Your mobile phone number and message history are used exclusively for:

  • Delivering messages within this SMS program
  • Maintaining records of your consent (date, time, method of opt-in)
  • Responding to opt-out and help requests
  • Internal compliance recordkeeping

We maintain records of SMS consent including the date, time, and method of your opt-in, as required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and applicable carrier requirements.

7. Information We Do NOT Share

Your mobile number and SMS opt-in data are never:

  • Sold to third parties, data brokers, or lead aggregators
  • Shared with other Third-Party Marketing Organizations (TPMOs) without your prior written consent
  • Used for purposes unrelated to your insurance consultation and enrollment
  • Shared with carriers for marketing purposes without your knowledge

Medicare-specific notice: Your mobile number and Medicare-related communication data will not be shared with other TPMOs without your prior express written consent, consistent with CMS marketing regulations (42 CFR 422.2274 and 423.2274).

8. Wireless Carrier Disclaimer

Lakeland Health Insurance is not responsible for any delays or failures in the receipt of any SMS messages. Delivery of SMS messages is subject to effective transmission from your mobile carrier network. Message delivery is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

This service is available on most major U.S. wireless carriers. If you experience issues receiving messages, contact us directly.

9. Privacy of Message Content

Because insurance-related communications may contain personal health and financial information, please be aware of the following:

  • Do not share your phone with others if you are receiving insurance-related text messages
  • We will never ask you to provide your Social Security number, full Medicare ID, or bank account information via SMS
  • If you receive a suspicious message claiming to be from us, do not respond — call us directly at (863) 640-3102

10. Data Security & Retention

Mobile numbers and SMS consent records are stored securely within our CRM and communication systems. Access is restricted to authorized staff only. Consent records are retained for the duration of the business relationship plus a minimum of four (4) years, consistent with TCPA requirements. All data security practices described in our full Privacy Policy apply to SMS data.

11. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • Opt out of SMS communications (reply STOP or contact us directly)
  • Request confirmation of whether we have your mobile number on file
  • Request correction of an incorrect number in our records
  • Request deletion of your mobile number from our marketing records

These rights do not affect any transactional communications required to service an active insurance policy. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

12. Changes to These Terms

We may update these SMS Terms of Service from time to time to reflect changes in carrier requirements, applicable law, or our practices. When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting your consent may result in a new opt-in request. Continued participation in the SMS program following an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
