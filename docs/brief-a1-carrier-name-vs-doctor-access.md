## 4. Questions to Answer (Primary & Must-Address)

1. **Why isn't a carrier brand name enough to prove doctor access?**  
   Explain that insurers sell multiple networks (HMO, PPO, POS, D-SNP, C-SNP, ACA individual exchange) under a single umbrella brand. A provider may accept one contract while remaining strictly out-of-network for another.
2. **What does "accepted" really mean at the clinic front desk?**  
   Clarify the legal and financial difference between a provider billing an out-of-network policy ("we accept all insurance") versus being an in-network participating provider under contract.
3. **How do Watson Clinic doctor contracts differ from the new Orlando Health hospital?**  
   Explain that the facility contracts for Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital (4000 Lakeland Highlands Rd) are negotiated separately from the Watson Clinic physician practice group. A hospital can be in-network while an attending specialist is out-of-network.
4. **What is an HMO "specialty-only" contract?**  
   Detail how certain Medicare Advantage HMOs in Polk County contract with Watson Clinic exclusively for specialty care referrals, while barring members from selecting a Watson Clinic physician as their primary care physician (PCP).
5. **How does an enrollee verify their doctor by CMS Plan ID?**  
   Step-by-step breakdown: locate the 5-digit contract number and 3-digit plan benefit package (e.g., `H1036-270` or `H5216-393`) on the Summary of Benefits or ANOC, cross-reference the carrier's official directory, and confirm with the clinic's billing coordinator.
