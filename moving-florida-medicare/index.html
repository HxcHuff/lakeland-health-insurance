Florida Medicare move review

A Medicare move can change the service area, plan choices, network, pharmacy, and enrollment window.

Moving outside a Medicare Advantage or drug plan’s service area—or moving where new plan options are available—may create a Special Enrollment Period. The exact action and timing depend on the move and current coverage.

Direct answer: Tell the current plan about the move, confirm whether the new address is inside its service area, check the exact Special Enrollment Period, and re-run provider, prescription, pharmacy, and plan-ID verification for the destination ZIP and plan year.

Applicability

  • People with Medicare moving into, within, or out of Florida.
  • 2026 coverage and planning for contract year 2027.
  • Plan availability depends on destination county/ZIP and eligibility.

Not every move creates the same enrollment right. Medicare and the current plan are the controlling sources.

Before the move

  • Current address, destination address, and move date.
  • Medicare number kept private; do not send it through analytics or a URL.
  • Exact current plan name, plan ID, and coverage type.
  • Doctors, facilities, prescriptions, dosage/form, and pharmacy.

Before selecting a plan

  1. Confirm the enrollment window and permitted changes.
  2. Compare only plans available at the destination address.
  3. Verify providers by exact location and plan ID.
  4. Verify formulary tier, restrictions, and pharmacy status.
  5. Confirm effective date before relying on new coverage.

Original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, Medigap, and Part D are separate checks

A move can affect each coverage component differently. Do not assume that a Medicare Advantage network follows you, that a drug plan is available in the new area, or that Medigap enrollment rights are identical to Medicare Advantage and Part D rights. Confirm the current rule and underwriting or guaranteed-issue status where applicable before ending coverage.
