What Is This Place?

Look, you've probably visited our professional blog and thought, "Wow, this is comprehensive and authoritative and... really, really dry." You're not wrong. That's intentional. We need to establish ourselves as the expert in the room.

But here's the thing: health insurance is objectively stupid and infuriating, and we're tired of pretending it isn't. So Dude's Corner is where we tell you what we're really thinking while you're stressing about deductibles and subsidy cliffs.

Everything you read here is factually accurate. We're just not wearing pants while we explain it.

Why This Exists

Health insurance is designed by actuaries, written in legalese, and sold by commission-driven brokers. The result? Information that's technically correct but emotionally dishonest.

We wanted a space where we could say:

  • "Yeah, you read that right—you could lose your subsidy because you got a bonus at work."
  • "No, a fixed-indemnity plan won't save you when you actually get sick."
  • "Insurance companies really DO profit by confusing you."
  • "Your deductible is probably higher than your mortgage payment, and that's not a coincidence."

That's Dude's Corner. All the facts. None of the BS.
