FAQ

Five things to know before you apply.

Is Health ProtectorGuard real insurance?

Yes. Health ProtectorGuard is fixed-indemnity insurance underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company, a UnitedHealthcare company. It pays limited, scheduled amounts for covered services. It is not major medical insurance and is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA.

Is Health ProtectorGuard comprehensive health insurance?

No. Health ProtectorGuard is fixed-indemnity insurance that provides limited benefits. It can supplement other health coverage, but it does not replace major medical insurance or Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA.

Will Health ProtectorGuard pay my full medical bill?

Not necessarily. The plan pays the set amount listed for a covered service, not a percentage of your total bill. If the bill is higher than the benefit, you may owe the remaining balance.

Does it cover pre-existing conditions?

Current product materials describe a 12-month lookback and a pre-existing-condition limitation that can apply for up to 12 months after coverage begins. The issued policy controls the exact terms.
