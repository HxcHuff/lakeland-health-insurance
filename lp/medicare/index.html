Quick questions

Common Medicare review questions

How are Medicare brokers compensated?

Insurance carriers generally compensate appointed brokers when an enrollment is completed. Compensation and plan availability vary by carrier and product.

Can you compare both Medicare Advantage and Supplement?

Yes. The review can compare Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement, and Part D options so you can see the tradeoffs clearly.

Should I switch plans every year?

Not automatically. You should review your plan every year because networks, drug lists, and benefits can change. Switching only makes sense if the new plan is a better fit.
