Guide to Common Coverage and Cost Mistakes

Five checks to complete before choosing or changing health coverage
Compare costs, networks, prescriptions, plan limits, and enrollment timing.
Use the exact plan, not a general carrier description. Coverage details can change by plan ID, network, ZIP code, county, and plan year. Verify current documents before enrolling or scheduling non-emergency care.

1. Compare Yearly Cost, Not Only the Premium

Review the annual premium together with the deductible, copays, coinsurance, prescription costs, and in-network out-of-pocket limit. Build both a routine-use estimate and a higher-use scenario.

  • Confirm which services apply before the deductible.
  • Check whether prescriptions have a separate deductible.
  • Identify costs that do not count toward the out-of-pocket limit.
  • Set a realistic reserve for early-year care.

2. Use Accurate Household Information

Marketplace eligibility and premium-tax-credit estimates depend on the application. Use current household, tax-filing, income, and employer-coverage information. Report material changes through the official Marketplace process.

Eligibility and savings are determined by the Marketplace. An estimate is not an eligibility decision or a guarantee of a particular premium.

3. Verify Providers and Facilities

Check each doctor, facility, laboratory, imaging center, and other involved provider against the exact plan and plan year. Confirm directly with the provider when practical and keep a record of the verification.

Provider directories can change and do not guarantee claim payment. Also review referral, service-area, and prior-authorization rules.

4. Check Every Prescription

Use the exact medication name, dosage, form, frequency, and pharmacy. Review formulary tier, prior authorization, step therapy, quantity limits, preferred pharmacies, and current estimated cost.

5. Read Exclusions, Limits, and Enrollment Rules

Review the Summary of Benefits and Coverage, Evidence of Coverage or policy, formulary, and provider network. Confirm exclusions, benefit limits, waiting periods, underwriting where applicable, and the effective date.

Do not end current coverage until replacement coverage is approved and its effective date is confirmed.
