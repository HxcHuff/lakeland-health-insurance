The 3 Florida Medigap enrollment rules: 6-month OEP, 63-day Guaranteed Issue & underwriting

Medicare's fall Annual Election Period (October 15 – December 7) applies to Medicare Advantage and Part D drug plans—it is not an automatic medical-underwriting waiver for Medigap in Florida. Check which of these three enrollment situations applies to you first:

1. Your 6-Month Medigap Open Enrollment Period

Federal law gives you a one-time 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period starting the first month you are both 65 or older and enrolled in Medicare Part B. During these 6 months, any Medigap insurer selling in your Florida ZIP code must issue your policy without medical underwriting or pre-existing condition rate surcharges. If you delayed Part B while covered by active employer group insurance, this 6-month clock starts when your Part B takes effect—see retiring and Medicare in Lakeland.

2. The 63-Day Guaranteed-Issue Window (2027 Plan Non-Renewal, Move, or Trial Right)

Outside your initial 6-month window, federal and Florida rules grant guaranteed-issue (GI) rights—meaning no health questions—under specific documented triggers, including:

  • Medicare Advantage Plan Non-Renewal: Your 2026 Medicare Advantage plan notifies you that it is leaving your Polk County service area or discontinuing your contract on December 31, 2026. Keep the official carrier non-renewal notice—your guaranteed-issue window generally runs from the notice date until 63 calendar days after coverage ends. Read our detailed 2027 non-renewal Medigap Plan G guide.
  • Permanent Move Out of Service Area: You permanently move outside your current Medicare Advantage plan's county service area.
  • Loss of Qualifying Employer/Union Secondary Coverage: Employer or union coverage that supplemented Original Medicare involuntarily ends (verify exact coordination rules before dropping retiree benefits—see our City of Lakeland retiree guide).
  • 12-Month Medicare Advantage Trial Right: You joined a Medicare Advantage plan for the first time at 65 (or dropped a Medigap policy to try Medicare Advantage for the first time) and return to Original Medicare within the first 12 months.

3. Switching with Florida Medical Underwriting

If neither the 6-month Part B window nor a guaranteed-issue event applies, you can apply for a Florida Medigap policy at any time of year, but insurers generally review your health history, height/weight, and prescription records. Critical protection rule: Never cancel an existing Medicare Advantage plan until (a) the Medigap carrier issues written underwriting approval AND (b) you have a valid Medicare election window (such as AEP Oct 15 – Dec 7 or MA OEP Jan 1 – Mar 31) to disenroll back to Original Medicare and enroll in a stand-alone Part D drug plan.

How Florida regulates Medigap age rating

Under Florida Office of Insurance Regulation rules, Florida Medigap policies use issue-age rating (rather than attained-age birthday step-ups alone). Your entry rate tier is based on your age when the policy is issued; however, premiums can still increase over time due to medical inflation, Part A/B deductible adjustments, and block-of-business claims experience. Comparing carrier rate-stability history alongside the initial monthly rate is part of a thorough review—see Medicare Supplement cost factors in Lakeland.

Adding 2027 Stand-Alone Part D ($2,400 Cap & $700 Deductible)

For 2027, covered formulary prescriptions carry a $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 standard deductible. Common questions

Does Original Medicare with Medigap Plan G or Plan N work at Watson Clinic, LRH, and Moffitt Cancer Center?

Yes, as long as the physician or hospital accepts Original Medicare. Standardized Medigap policies do not use private HMO or PPO networks, so beneficiaries can see participating Original Medicare providers at Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands, BayCare, AdventHealth, Moffitt Cancer Center, and Mayo Clinic without primary care referrals.

What is the difference between Medigap Plan G and Plan N in Florida for 2027?

Both standardized Plan G and Plan N cover the Part A hospital deductible and 20% Part B coinsurance after you meet the annual Medicare Part B deductible. With Plan N, you may pay copays of up to $20 for certain office visits and up to $50 for emergency room visits that do not result in an inpatient admission, and Plan N does not cover Part B excess charges if you use a provider who does not accept Medicare assignment. Plan G covers Part B excess charges and has no office or ER copays after the Part B deductible.

When can I enroll in a Florida Medigap plan without medical underwriting?

Your primary window is your 6-month federal Medigap Open Enrollment Period, which begins the month you are both 65 or older and enrolled in Medicare Part B. Outside that window, you need a federal or Florida guaranteed-issue trigger—such as a Medicare Advantage plan non-renewal, moving out of a Medicare Advantage plan's service area, losing qualifying employer group coverage, or a 12-month Medicare Advantage Trial Right (generally extending up to 63 days after prior coverage ends). Without a guaranteed-issue right, Florida insurers generally require medical underwriting.

Does a Medicare Supplement plan include 2027 prescription drug coverage?

No. Standardized Medigap policies sold today do not include outpatient prescription drug coverage. Unless you hold other creditable drug coverage, you pair Original Medicare and Medigap with a stand-alone Medicare Part D plan. For 2027, Part D plans carry a $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 standard deductible on covered formulary medications.

Should I cancel my Medicare Advantage plan before applying for Medigap in Florida?

No. If your application requires medical underwriting, never cancel or disenroll from your current Medicare Advantage plan until the Medigap insurer issues written approval and confirms your effective date, AND you have confirmed a valid Medicare election period to return to Original Medicare and join a stand-alone Part D plan.
