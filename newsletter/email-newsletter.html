Tax Season
Your 1095 Form — Don't File Without It
It's proof you had health coverage. Missing it can cost you your entire subsidy. Here's what each form means.
 1095-A — Marketplace (ACA) plan. Most important. Required for Form 8962. Don't file before this arrives.
 1095-B — Private insurer or Medicaid. Informational only — no action needed.
 1095-C — Employer coverage. Informational unless you also received a subsidy.
What to do with your 1095-A:
1
Log into HealthCare.gov → "My Applications" → Download your 1095-A (available by end of January).
2
Give to your tax preparer or enter when your software prompts for Form 8962.
3
If numbers look wrong, call us — we can request a corrected 1095-A from the Marketplace.
 ⚠ Common mistake: Filing before your 1095-A arrives or using wrong figures triggers an IRS notice and possible repayment of your entire subsidy.
Protect Your Savings
How to Keep Your Subsidy — and Not Pay It Back
Your ACA tax credit is based on estimated income. If your actual income was higher, you may owe money back at tax time. Here's how to stay protected.
1
Got a raise? Log into HealthCare.gov → Report a Life Change → Update income. Your premium adjusts going forward.
2
Side hustle or freelance? Count every dollar. Underreporting is the #1 reason people owe at tax time.
3
Job loss or reduced hours? Update immediately — you may qualify for a larger subsidy or Medicaid.
4
Unpredictable income? Consider electing 0% advance credit — pay full premium now, get full credit as a refund. Zero repayment risk.

Avocado Chicken Bowl
Prep: 5 min | Cook: 10 min | Serves: 2 | High Protein
2 boneless chicken breasts sliced thin • 1 large avocado diced • 1 cup brown rice or quinoa • 1 cup cherry tomatoes halved • Lime juice, olive oil, garlic salt, cumin, cilantro

Prep: 8 min | No Cook | Serves: 4 | Refreshing

🔵 Medicare Corner
On Medicare — or Turning 65?
This section is just for you. Here's what matters right now.
Medicare can feel like a maze — Part A, B, C, D, Medigap, IRMAA surcharges. My job is to cut through all of it and get you into the right plan without overpaying a dollar.
Turning 65? Your Window Is Shorter Than You Think
You have a 7-month Initial Enrollment Period — 3 months before your birthday month, your birthday month, and 3 months after. Miss it and face a permanent 10% per year late penalty on Part B.
 ⚠ Still working at 65 with employer coverage? Different rules apply — don't assume you're exempt. Call me before your birthday month to confirm.
Medicare Advantage vs. Original Medicare + Supplement
Honest answer — it depends on your health, doctors, and budget.
Medicare Advantage (Part C)
✓ Lower or $0 monthly premium
✓ Often includes dental, vision, hearing
✓ OTC allowances & gym benefits
⚠ Network restrictions apply
⚠ Prior authorizations possible
  
Original Medicare + Supplement
✓ Any doctor that accepts Medicare
✓ Predictable out-of-pocket costs
✓ No referrals needed
⚠ Higher monthly premium
⚠ Separate Part D plan needed
Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) — Don't Sleep On It
Every year from October 15 – December 7 you can switch plans, change your Part D, or move back to Original Medicare. Plans change premiums and formularies every year — I review every client annually at no charge.
 
Already On Medicare Advantage? Know Your OEP Rights
Open Enrollment Period: January 1 – March 31  |  One switch. Use it wisely.
If your plan isn't working — wrong doctors, high copays, denied claims — the OEP gives you one chance to make a move before you're locked in for the rest of the year.
What You CAN Do During OEP
1
Switch from one Medicare Advantage plan to another. Different carrier, network, cost structure — your call.
2
Drop Medicare Advantage entirely and return to Original Medicare + a Part D drug plan.
3
Add, drop, or switch a Part D drug plan if you returned to Original Medicare.
 
What You CANNOT Do During OEP
Cannot switch from Original Medicare TO Medicare Advantage — that's AEP (Oct 15–Dec 7).
Cannot enroll in Medigap with guaranteed issue rights — underwriting may apply.
Cannot make more than one plan change — OEP is one switch only.
OEP: Jan 1 – Mar 31   |   AEP: Oct 15 – Dec 7
Miss either window and you're locked in until next year.
