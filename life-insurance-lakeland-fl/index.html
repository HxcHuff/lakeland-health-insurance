Frequently Asked Questions

Can Lakeland seniors ages 50 to 85 buy Final Expense whole life insurance without a medical exam?

Yes. Simplified-issue Final Expense whole life policies use brief health questions and electronic prescription checks rather than a nurse exam or blood draw, while guaranteed-issue policies are available for complex health histories with graded benefit waiting periods per carrier policy terms.

How much Term Life or mortgage protection coverage does a Polk County family typically target?

Families commonly calculate a starting target using the DIME formula (Debt, Income replacement years, Mortgage balance, and Education costs) minus existing policies and savings, then compare 10, 15, 20, or 30-year level term quotes across multiple carriers.

How does Key-Person and Buy-Sell life insurance help Lakeland small businesses?

Key-Person life insurance gives a small business working capital to cover payroll, debt obligations, and operator replacement if an owner or lead technician passes away unexpectedly. Buy-Sell funding pairs life insurance with a written buyout agreement drafted by your attorney.
