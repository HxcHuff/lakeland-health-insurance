Start Here
Use it only for a defined gap after checking comprehensive options.

Short-term medical insurance is primarily designed for temporary transitions. It may be worth reviewing when the gap has a known end date and the applicant understands that federal individual-market protections do not generally apply in the same way.

Review may be appropriate when...

  • The gap between comprehensive plans is brief and has a documented end date.
  • ACA Special Enrollment, employer coverage, COBRA, Medicaid, and CHIP have already been checked.
  • The applicant can complete medical underwriting accurately and review exclusions before applying.
  • The applicant can absorb the deductible, uncovered services, and amounts above policy limits.

Comprehensive coverage should lead when...

  • There is ongoing treatment, a planned procedure, pregnancy, recurring therapy, or regular prescription use.
  • Coverage for pre-existing conditions, broad benefits, or predictable cost sharing is important.
  • The replacement-coverage date is uncertain.
  • The decision is based only on monthly cost instead of total claim exposure.
Current Rule Status
The federal definition and enforcement posture are not the same thing.

The sources below were reviewed August 12, 2026. They establish the regulatory framework, but they do not prove that a specific carrier, product label, term, network, or policy form is currently available to an applicant.

01

Federal definition

The 2024 final rule limits a new policy's initial term to no more than three months and the total coverage period, including renewals or extensions, to no more than four months.

02

Federal enforcement statement

On August 7, 2025, the Departments said they would not prioritize enforcement of the 2024 definition while considering future rulemaking. That statement did not itself amend or repeal the final rule.

03

Florida definition and disclosure

Florida's 2025 statute defines a short-term contract as lasting less than twelve months initially and no more than thirty-six months including renewals or extensions, and requires written consumer disclosures.

Frequently Asked Questions
Short-Term Medical: current boundaries
Is short-term medical ACA-compliant major medical coverage?

No. It is generally exempt from federal individual-market requirements that apply to comprehensive ACA coverage. Underwriting, pre-existing-condition exclusions, omitted benefits, and annual or lifetime dollar limits may apply.

How long can a new policy last?

The published federal definition, current non-enforcement posture, Florida law, and the policy offered must be read together. VERIFICATION REQUIRED: confirm the exact term and any renewal or extension before applying.

Is a TriTerm-labeled product available now?

VERIFICATION REQUIRED. Historical product labels and brochures do not establish current availability. Confirm the current quote, policy form, Florida approval, effective date, and contract terms.

What documents should I review?

Review the application, outline of coverage, policy or certificate, schedule of benefits, exclusions, pre-existing-condition definition, provider directory, prescription provisions, termination date, and required notices.
