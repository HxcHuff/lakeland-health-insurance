How a remote review works

1. Tell me the state and household. County, ZIP code, who needs coverage, and projected 2027 income. Use the state page first so the Marketplace and dates match where you live.
2. I review 2027 plan data for that ZIP code. Network, prescriptions, deductible, and out-of-pocket maximum on Marketplace plans in that state.
3. You keep the account. We go over trade-offs by phone or screen share. You authorize any application or change. A review is not enrollment.

Primary sources

  • HealthCare.gov: Dates and deadlines — accessed September 30, 2026; 2027 federal Marketplace Open Enrollment: November 1 start, December 15 deadline for a January 1 start, January 15 end.
  • CMS: State-based Exchanges — accessed September 30, 2026; States not listed as State-based Exchanges use HealthCare.gov.
  • Virginia's Insurance Marketplace: How to Enroll — accessed September 30, 2026; Open Enrollment is the yearly period from November 1 to January 29.
  • Georgia Access, Plan Year 2027 QHP Application Instructions for Issuers — updated August 10, 2026; 2027 open enrollment November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027.
  • Get Covered New Jersey — accessed September 30, 2026; Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 31.
  • Washington Healthplanfinder: Enrollment Periods — accessed September 30, 2026; Open enrollment begins November 1 and ends January 15.
  • Maryland Health Connection — accessed September 30, 2026; 2027 open enrollment dates were not posted on the official exchange site at access, so this hub does not list a Maryland deadline.
