2027 Florida enrollment timing

Florida uses the federal HealthCare.gov platform. CMS states that the plan year 2027 Open Enrollment Period for federal-platform states runs from November 1 through December 15, 2026. Completed eligible enrollments are scheduled for January 1, 2027 coverage.

Outside Open Enrollment, a Special Enrollment Period may apply after certain events such as loss of qualifying coverage, marriage, birth, adoption, or a qualifying move. The event and application result—not a general list—control eligibility.

Tax-credit and income limits

Use a good-faith projection of annual household income and the tax household expected for the coverage year. Report material changes promptly. Advance premium tax credits are reconciled on the federal tax return, so an estimator or initial eligibility result is not a guarantee of the final credit.

Polk County plan-review checklist

  1. Confirm the exact Florida ZIP code, county, applicants, and coverage year.
  2. Review eligibility results and every Marketplace document request.
  3. Check each provider and facility by exact plan ID and location.
  4. Check each prescription’s formulary tier, restrictions, and pharmacy network.
  5. Compare premium, deductible, copays, coinsurance, and maximum out-of-pocket exposure.
  6. Confirm enrollment, first-premium requirements, and carrier effectuation before relying on coverage.

Frequently asked questions

When is 2027 ACA Open Enrollment in Florida?

Florida uses HealthCare.gov. For plan year 2027, the federal Marketplace Open Enrollment Period runs November 1 through December 15, 2026, with coverage beginning January 1, 2027 for completed eligible enrollments.

Can self-employed people use the Florida Marketplace?

Self-employed people can apply for Marketplace coverage. Eligibility for premium tax credits depends on the Marketplace application, including projected annual household income and tax-household information.

Does losing coverage create a Special Enrollment Period?

Losing qualifying health coverage may create a Marketplace Special Enrollment Period. The event, date, prior coverage, documentation, and application result determine whether and when enrollment is allowed.

How should I compare ACA plans in Polk County?

Compare the exact plan for your ZIP code and coverage year using provider and facility networks, prescription formulary and pharmacy details, premium, deductible, cost sharing, and maximum out-of-pocket exposure.
