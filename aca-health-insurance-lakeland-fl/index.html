2027 Florida enrollment timing

Florida uses the federal HealthCare.gov platform. HealthCare.gov states that Open Enrollment starts November 1. December 15 is the last day to enroll or change for coverage that starts January 1. Open Enrollment ends January 15; enrollments from December 16 through January 15 generally start February 1.

2027 Enrollment Window Action Deadline Coverage Effective Date
Open Enrollment Window 1 Nov 1, 2026 – Dec 15, 2026 January 1, 2027
Open Enrollment Window 2 Dec 16, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027 February 1, 2027
Special Enrollment (SEP) Generally 60 days from qualifying event Event-specific (1st of following month)

Outside Open Enrollment, a Special Enrollment Period may apply after certain events such as loss of qualifying coverage, marriage, birth, adoption, or a qualifying move. The event and application result, not a general list, control eligibility.

Frequently asked questions

When is 2027 ACA Open Enrollment in Florida?

Florida uses HealthCare.gov. For plan year 2027, Marketplace Open Enrollment starts November 1, 2026. December 15, 2026 is the last day to enroll or change for coverage that starts January 1, 2027. Open Enrollment ends January 15, 2027; enrollments from December 16 through January 15 generally start February 1.

What are the 2027 Florida ACA income limits for a Marketplace subsidy in Polk County?

For 2027 coverage in Florida (using the 2026 HHS Federal Poverty Guidelines), the 100% to 400% FPL window is $15,960 to $63,840 for a single adult, $21,640 to $86,560 for a 2-person tax household, $27,320 to $109,280 for a 3-person household, and $33,000 to $132,000 for a family of four. Households between 100% and 250% FPL also qualify for Cost-Sharing Reductions when they select a Silver plan.

How do ACA subsidies work when one spouse is on Medicare and the other spouse is under 65?

On a joint federal tax return, both spouses count in the 2-person tax household size ($21,640 to $86,560 for 100% to 400% FPL in 2027) and both spouses' Modified Adjusted Gross Income counts, including the Medicare spouse's Social Security and retirement income, even though only the under-65 spouse applies for ACA Marketplace coverage.

Can self-employed people use the Florida Marketplace?

Self-employed people can apply for Marketplace coverage. Eligibility for premium tax credits depends on the Marketplace application, including projected annual household income and tax-household information.

Does losing coverage create a Special Enrollment Period?

Losing qualifying health coverage may create a Marketplace Special Enrollment Period. The event, date, prior coverage, documentation, and application result determine whether and when enrollment is allowed.

How should I compare ACA plans in Polk County?

Compare the exact plan for your ZIP code and coverage year using provider and facility networks, prescription formulary and pharmacy details, premium, deductible, cost sharing, and maximum out-of-pocket exposure.
