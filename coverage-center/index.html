Common questions

Coverage Center FAQ

Is the Coverage Center an enrollment page?

No. This is a plan-review hub for 2027 coverage decisions. A review request is not enrollment, a plan recommendation, an eligibility determination, or proof of coverage.

What happens in a plan review?

David personally reviews each request and follows up only through a channel authorized in the form. If you prefer not to submit the form, call (863) 640-3102 or choose an available appointment time.

Which lane should I choose?

Use the Medicare lane for Annual Enrollment, turning 65, or comparing Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement. Use the under-65 lane for ACA Marketplace coverage, a job change, self-employment, or a coverage gap.

Do I have to shop online to get help?

No. The HealthSherpa link is an optional ACA shopping path with its own privacy terms. You can request a plan review or call instead.
