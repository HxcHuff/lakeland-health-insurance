Does My Plan Cover Tri-County Human Services in Lakeland?

Direct answer: It depends on your plan. Mental health and substance use care are essential health benefits on Marketplace plans, but networks and prior authorization vary. Look up Tri-County Human Services in Lakeland in your plan's provider directory for your plan ID and year, then call to confirm. In a crisis, call or text 988; if someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Tri-County Human Services in Lakeland offers detox and substance-use treatment. Confirm behavioral-health benefits before admission.

If this is a crisis

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available 24/7 by call or text (988); Peace River Center runs a local 24/7 crisis line at 800-627-5906.

About Tri-County Human Services

Tri-County Human Services says it provides help and hope to people affected by behavioral health, substance use, and life challenges. Its offices are at 2026 Crystal Wood Drive in Lakeland. Call to confirm which programs fit your situation.

How Marketplace and employer plans cover behavioral health

Yes. Mental health and substance use disorder services are essential health benefits that Marketplace plans must cover. Networks, prior authorization, and cost-sharing still vary by plan, so check the facility and providers in your plan's directory.

Medicare Part A covers inpatient mental health care; Part B covers outpatient mental health services.

Medicare: Part A inpatient, Part B outpatient

Medicare Part A covers inpatient mental health care in a hospital, and Part B covers outpatient mental health services. Medicare Advantage plans cover these benefits but can use networks and prior authorization.

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 (plan must get the request by December 7). The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period runs January 1 to March 31 for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan.

Medicare marketing on this site is for Florida residents. Confirm hospital, doctor, and pharmacy participation with the plan and the office. Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage follow different network rules.
