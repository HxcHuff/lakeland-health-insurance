What Florida's 2026 Rate Data Shows

Florida OIR's approved plan-year 2026 individual-market table reports a 34.1% membership-weighted average premium increase based on 2025 enrollment. The table does not establish the increase for a particular Pinellas County plan or the amount a household pays after any premium tax credit.

The temporary enhanced premium tax credit rules ended after 2025. IRS guidance states that 2026 eligibility is generally limited to households with income from 100 through 400 percent of the federal poverty line, subject to the other tax, Marketplace enrollment, and minimum-essential-coverage requirements.

A Clearwater household's result depends on projected annual income, tax household, benchmark plan, residence, eligibility for other coverage, and the plan selected.

Coverage Before Medicare Eligibility

For an early retiree who is not yet Medicare-eligible, ACA Marketplace coverage may be one option. Compare it with any available former-employer, spouse, or COBRA coverage using the same criteria: total premium, cost sharing, provider network, prescriptions, service area, and coverage period.

Premium tax credits depend on the Marketplace application and current federal requirements. Eligibility for other minimum essential coverage can affect whether a person qualifies for a Marketplace credit.

Special Enrollment Periods in Clearwater

Outside Open Enrollment, HealthCare.gov identifies event categories that may create a Special Enrollment Period:

  • Loss of qualifying health coverage
  • Specified household changes, including marriage, birth, adoption, or foster placement
  • A qualifying residence change that meets applicable prior-coverage rules
  • Other Marketplace-recognized circumstances

Timing, prior-coverage conditions, plan-category limits, effective dates, and documentation vary by event. The Marketplace application and eligibility notice control the result.

2026 Bronze and Catastrophic Plans Work With HSAs

HealthCare.gov states that all 2026 Bronze and Catastrophic individual-market plans work with Health Savings Accounts. A person must still satisfy the other federal HSA eligibility rules before contributing.

HSA compatibility does not establish plan suitability. Compare providers, prescriptions, deductible, other cost sharing, annual out-of-pocket limit, and expected care before selecting a plan.

Compare the Exact Plan, Not the Metal Level Alone

Use the exact plan year and plan ID to verify the premium after any Marketplace credit, provider network, prescription formulary, pharmacy network, referral rules, prior authorization, and cost sharing. Two plans in the same metal level can differ on these details.

FAQ — Health Insurance in Clearwater, FL

What does Florida's 2026 individual-market rate increase mean for Clearwater residents?

Florida OIR reports a 34.1 percent membership-weighted average increase in approved 2026 individual-market premiums, based on 2025 enrollment. That is a statewide market measure, not the increase for every Clearwater household or policy. Net premiums also depend on premium-tax-credit eligibility and the plan selected.

I'm an early retiree in Clearwater. What are my health insurance options before Medicare?

If you're retired and not yet Medicare-eligible, ACA Marketplace coverage may be one option. Eligibility for premium tax credits depends on the Marketplace application and current rules. If you are considering an HSA, confirm that the specific plan is HSA-eligible and review tax questions with a qualified tax professional.

Can I enroll in health insurance in Clearwater outside Open Enrollment?

A Special Enrollment Period may be available after loss of qualifying coverage, specified household changes, or a qualifying residence change. Timing, prior-coverage conditions, plan choices, and documentation vary by event, and the Marketplace application determines eligibility.

What is an HSA-compatible Bronze plan for 2026?

HealthCare.gov states that all 2026 Bronze and Catastrophic individual-market plans work with Health Savings Accounts. A person must still satisfy the other federal HSA eligibility rules before contributing, and HSA compatibility does not determine whether a plan fits a household.
