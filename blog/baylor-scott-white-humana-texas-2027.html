The Texas Healthcare Giant and Network Shifts

Baylor Scott & White Health (BSW Health) is the largest not-for-profit health system in Texas, spanning more than 50 hospitals and over 800 patient access points throughout North and Central Texas. With flagship tertiary campuses including Baylor University Medical Center in Dallas and Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Temple, millions of Texans rely on BSW physicians for primary and specialized medical care.

Contract disputes and network tiering between BSW and major payers like Humana have left thousands of Texas seniors and commercial policyholders facing potential out-of-network disruptions. Navigating Open Enrollment requires understanding which Texas exchange and Medicare plans keep your local BSW doctors and hospitals in-network.

Key Baylor Scott & White Facilities Across Texas

The system's extensive clinical footprint includes key hospital hubs:

  • Baylor University Medical Center (Dallas): Major academic research and tertiary referral hospital.
  • Baylor Scott & White The Heart Hospital (Plano & Denton): Nationally ranked cardiovascular surgery and treatment center.
  • Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Temple: Central Texas Level 1 Trauma Center serving Temple, Killeen, and Waco.
  • Baylor Scott & White Medical Center – Round Rock & Austin: Key facilities serving the greater Austin metropolitan region.
  • Baylor Scott & White Quality Alliance: Network of thousands of primary care and specialist physicians across Texas.

Emergency Care Protections Under Federal Law

Under the federal No Surprises Act, patients requiring emergency room evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency facility, including Baylor University Medical Center in Dallas and BSW Temple Trauma Center, cannot be balance billed. Emergency room care must be covered by your insurer at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of carrier contract negotiations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Baylor Scott & White Health accept Humana in Texas for 2027?

Baylor Scott & White Health contract agreements with Humana have experienced changes across select Medicare Advantage and commercial HMO networks; Texas policyholders must verify their specific 2027 plan network status during Open Enrollment.

Can Texas patients receive emergency care at Baylor Scott & White hospitals under any plan?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency facility, including Baylor University Medical Center and regional BSW campuses, must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of network contract status.

Which Texas HealthCare.gov Marketplace carriers contract with Baylor Scott & White in 2027?

In Texas, Baylor Scott & White Health Plan, Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas (select networks), and individual exchange carriers such as Oscar Health and UnitedHealthcare offer participating provider networks depending on your county of residence.
