Exact 2027 Deadlines & Effective Dates (Do Not Miss These)

Whether your specific Alabama Humana or UnitedHealthcare plan ID received a 2027 non-renewal notice or your continuing plan changed doctor tiers and drug formularies, track these three 2027 federal Medicare enrollment windows:

  • Annual Election Period (AEP, October 15 to December 7, 2026): Enroll in your 2027 replacement coverage by December 7 for seamless January 1, 2027 effective dates and new ID cards before the new year starts.
  • Federal Non-Renewal Special Enrollment Period (December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027): If your 2026 plan is terminated and you miss December 7, your official non-renewal notice keeps a federal SEP open through February 28, 2027:
    • Enrolled December 8 to December 31, 2026: effective January 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled January 1 to January 31, 2027: effective February 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled February 1 to February 28, 2027: effective March 1, 2027.
  • 63-Day Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window (November 2, 2026 through roughly March 4, 2027): Displaced members returning to Original Medicare may purchase an eligible standardized Medigap plan (Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L, plus Plans C or F if eligible prior to January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting, paired with a standalone 2027 Part D drug plan (featuring the $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 defined-standard deductible). Note: enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific Medigap Guaranteed Issue right.

Fallback exposure if you take no action: Reverting automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 removes Part D prescription drug coverage and leaves you responsible for the 2027 Original Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible of $1,776 per benefit period and 20% Part B coinsurance with no annual out-of-pocket cap.

Meanwhile, national 2027 Humana and UnitedHealthcare plan rationalizations affect select low-margin or lower-star Plan IDs across the Southeast. Never rely on carrier brand name alone: verify your exact Alabama doctors, hospital systems, and prescriptions against the exact 2027 Plan ID before December 7 (or February 28 if you hold an official non-renewal notice).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama exiting Medicare Advantage in 2027?

No. Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama continues offering its in-state Blue Advantage PPO Medicare Advantage plans for 2027. The 2027 Patrius Health exit applies specifically to the 26-county Mississippi joint venture.

What new or continuing Medicare Advantage options are active in Alabama for 2027?

Alabama expands to roughly 113 Medicare Advantage plans for 2027, including BCBS Alabama Blue Advantage Choice and Core PPOs, Viva Medicare (Plus, Premier, Select, Infirmary Health Advantage, and the new Healthy Lifestyles C-SNP), plus active Humana, UnitedHealthcare, and other county-level plans.

When do Alabama seniors get a 63-day Medigap Guaranteed Issue right?

If your exact 2026 Alabama Medicare Advantage plan is non-renewed or leaves your county on December 31, 2026, federal rules grant a 63-day Medigap Guaranteed Issue window (roughly November 2, 2026 through March 4, 2027) to buy Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L without medical underwriting by submitting your termination letter.

What are the 2027 Part D and Original Medicare Part A numbers to budget for?

For 2027, Medicare Part D caps annual out-of-pocket prescription spending at $2,400 with a maximum defined-standard deductible of $700, while Original Medicare's Part A inpatient hospital deductible rises to $1,776 per benefit period plus 20% Part B coinsurance.
