Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 3-layer health coverage stack for self-employed households?

A 3-layer stack separates your coverage into: Layer 1 (Primary Medical Engine such as ACA, TriTerm Medical, or Short-Term Medical for major bills), Layer 2 (Supplemental Cash or Fixed Indemnity such as Health ProtectorGuard, HospitalWise, AccidentWise, or CriticalGuard to help offset deductibles), and Layer 3 (Standalone Dental, Vision & Hearing).

Does Health ProtectorGuard pay my entire hospital bill?

No. Enhanced Health ProtectorGuard (Form G202605) is a fixed-benefit indemnity policy that pays stated scheduled dollar amounts per covered service regardless of actual expenses incurred. It is not ACA Minimum Essential Coverage and works best when reviewed beside a primary medical policy.

When should a self-employed household choose ACA over TriTerm Medical?

Choose ACA Marketplace coverage if you qualify for meaningful Premium Tax Credits or Silver Cost-Sharing Reductions, if anyone in the household has pre-existing medical conditions or ongoing brand-name prescriptions, or if you need maternity and Essential Health Benefits from Day 1.
