Two Major Texas ACA Marketplace Exits Effective December 31, 2026

Under-65 Texans enrolled in two widely held individual Marketplace carriers must actively choose a new insurer for 2027:

  • Baylor Scott & White Health Plan (~100,000 Enrollees): Discontinuing all individual and family ACA plans—both on HealthCare.gov and off-exchange—after December 31, 2026. Importantly, Baylor Scott & White hospitals, clinics, and physicians are not closing and continue contracting with other 2027 Texas insurers; BSW employer-group and Medicare Advantage lines were also structured separately from the individual ACA exit.
  • Cigna Healthcare (All 11 States Including Texas): Exiting the individual and family ACA Marketplace completely across Texas effective December 31, 2026 as part of its nationwide individual-market withdrawal (~369,000 members across 11 states).

Why automatic crosswalks fail Texans: When a carrier exits HealthCare.gov, the Marketplace may automatically map your household into a different insurer's plan for January 1. That automated crosswalk does not verify whether your Baylor Scott & White, UT Southwestern, Memorial Hermann, Houston Methodist, or St. David's specialists are in network—or whether your prescriptions stayed on formulary.

Key 2027 Texas Deadlines & Subsidy Rules (Non-Expansion State)

  • December 15, 2026 Deadline: Complete your replacement carrier selection on HealthCare.gov by December 15 for uninterrupted coverage starting January 1, 2027.
  • January 15, 2027 Final OEP Close: Selections made from December 16 through January 15 generally take effect February 1, 2027 (leaving a one-month January gap unless you have an active plan).
  • 100% to 400% FPL Tax-Credit Rules: Because Texas has not adopted ACA Medicaid expansion, Marketplace premium tax credits for 2027 generally require projected household MAGI between 100% and 400% FPL ($15,960–$63,840 single / $33,000–$132,000 family of four using 2026 HHS guidelines), with full tax-time reconciliation and no repayment cap if actual income exceeds your estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Baylor Scott & White Health Plan really exiting the Texas ACA Marketplace for 2027?

Yes. Baylor Scott & White Health Plan announced it is discontinuing its individual and family ACA Marketplace plans (both on-exchange and off-exchange) after December 31, 2026, affecting roughly 100,000 Texans.

Will Baylor Scott & White hospitals still accept insurance in 2027?

Yes. Baylor Scott & White hospitals, clinics, and physicians are not closing. Displaced members simply need to enroll in a different 2027 HealthCare.gov insurer whose specific plan network includes Baylor Scott & White facilities.

Is Cigna also leaving the Texas ACA Marketplace for 2027?

Yes. Cigna is exiting the individual and family ACA Marketplace completely across all 11 of its exchange states, including Texas, effective December 31, 2026.

When is the deadline to replace my ending Texas ACA plan for January 1, 2027?

Enroll in your new 2027 HealthCare.gov plan by December 15, 2026 for uninterrupted January 1, 2027 coverage. Open Enrollment runs through January 15, 2027, with enrollments after December 15 generally taking effect February 1.
