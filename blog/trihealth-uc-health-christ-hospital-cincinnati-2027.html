Direct Answer: What Insurance Do Cincinnati Hospitals Accept in 2027?

Direct answer: In Greater Cincinnati for 2027, TriHealth (Bethesda North & Good Samaritan), UC Health (UC Medical Center & West Chester Hospital), The Christ Hospital, and Mercy Health (Anderson, Fairfield, West & Clermont) all accept Original Medicare with any Medigap supplement, while 2027 Ohio ACA Marketplace plans (Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield, Medical Mutual, Ambetter from Buckeye Health Plan, Molina, CareSource, Oscar, and UnitedHealthcare) and Medicare Advantage plans divide hospital access by network tier.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance does TriHealth (Bethesda North and Good Samaritan) accept in Cincinnati for 2027?

TriHealth hospitals and TriHealth Physician Partners accept Original Medicare with Medigap, major Southwest Ohio Medicare Advantage PPO and HMO plans (Anthem, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Aetna, Devoted), and select 2027 Ohio ACA Marketplace plans from Anthem BCBS, Medical Mutual, CareSource, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare.

Do The Christ Hospital and UC Health accept the same 2027 Ohio ACA Marketplace plans?

Some 2027 Southwest Ohio ACA networks (such as select CareSource, Anthem, and Medical Mutual tiers) include both UC Health and The Christ Hospital, while narrower HMO plans may contract with only one system. Always check both facility and physician group NPIs.

Are Mercy Health hospitals (Anderson, Fairfield, West, Clermont, and The Jewish Hospital) in-network with Ambetter and Anthem in 2027?

Mercy Health Cincinnati hospitals participate broadly in Original Medicare with Medigap, contracted Medicare Advantage plans, and select 2027 Anthem BCBS, Ambetter from Buckeye Health Plan, CareSource, and Medical Mutual ACA networks.
