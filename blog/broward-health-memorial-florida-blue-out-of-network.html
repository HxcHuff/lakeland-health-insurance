South Florida's Two Hospital Districts Explained

Broward County features a unique healthcare infrastructure divided into two distinct public health taxing districts: Broward Health (North Broward Hospital District) serving northern and central communities like Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, and Coral Springs, and Memorial Healthcare System (South Broward Hospital District) serving Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, and Hallandale Beach.

Because both systems operate major acute care facilities, trauma centers, and comprehensive pediatric pavilions (notably Joe DiMaggio Children's Hospital at Memorial), contract negotiations and narrow network exclusions with major insurers like Florida Blue frequently leave consumers confused about where their coverage actually applies.

Florida Blue Network Tiers: BlueOptions vs. BlueCare vs. myBlue

When purchasing an ACA Marketplace plan or employer group policy in Broward County, the insurer name on the card is only half the equation. The specific plan network code determines facility participation:

Network Name Network Structure Broward Health Access Memorial Healthcare Access
BlueOptions (PPO) Broad Network PPO In-Network at major hospitals and physician groups. In-Network across primary campuses and regional centers.
BlueCare (HMO) Standard Regional HMO In-Network with primary care physician referral required for specialist care. Contracted for select facilities; primary care referral mandatory.
myBlue (HMO) Narrow Value Network Often excluded or restricted to designated community clinic locations. Strict limitations; often excludes elective admissions at tertiary facilities.

Emergency Care Protections Under Federal Law

A frequent concern during insurance disputes or narrow-network plan selection is emergency treatment. Under the federal No Surprises Act:

  • Emergency Department Evaluation: If you experience a medical emergency and arrive at Broward Health Medical Center (Level 1 Trauma) or Memorial Regional Hospital (Level 1 Trauma), your care must be covered at in-network cost-sharing rates, regardless of which ACA plan you carry.
  • Prior Authorization Prohibited: Insurers cannot require advance pre-authorization for emergency stabilization services.
  • Post-Stabilization Transfer: Once an emergency patient is stabilized, continued inpatient elective care may require transfer to an in-network facility unless an out-of-network authorization is approved.

Alternative ACA Marketplace Carriers in Broward County

Consumers who want reliable access to Broward Health or Memorial Healthcare facilities have several competing options on the Florida Health Insurance Marketplace:

  • Oscar Health: Maintains active provider contracts across South Florida with extensive digital primary care and integrated access to Memorial Healthcare System.
  • Ambetter from Sunshine Health: Offers tiered provider networks with contracted rates across South Florida medical centers, providing cost-effective Silver CSR subsidy options.
  • UnitedHealthcare Individual Exchange: Provides selective HMO and EPO network choices in Southeast Florida markets.

Open Enrollment Checklist for Broward Residents

  1. Confirm Hospital Affiliation: Ask your primary care doctor and key specialists which hospital systems they admit patients to (Broward Health vs. Memorial vs. Holy Cross Health).
  2. Check Plan Prefix and Network Tier: Avoid assuming all Florida Blue plans include your preferred hospital. Double-check whether your plan is BlueOptions, BlueCare, or myBlue.
  3. Verify Pediatric Coverage: Families requiring pediatric specialty care must verify direct in-network access to Joe DiMaggio Children's Hospital or Salah Foundation Children's Hospital.
  4. Apply Subsidies Properly: Utilize Advance Premium Tax Credits (APTC) during Open Enrollment to offset the premium difference of broader PPO or tiered HMO coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Broward Health and Memorial Healthcare in-network for all Florida Blue plans?

No. Network status varies significantly by plan network type. While Florida Blue BlueOptions PPO generally includes both major Broward hospital systems, entry-level HMO networks such as myBlue frequently exclude specific public health district facilities or impose strict referral controls.

What happens if I visit Memorial Regional or Broward Health in an emergency with an out-of-network plan?

Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency department, including Memorial Regional Hospital and Broward Health Medical Center, must be billed and covered at your plan's in-network cost-sharing level.

Which Marketplace carriers contract with Memorial Healthcare System and Broward Health in 2027?

In Broward County, individual exchange carriers including Oscar Health, Ambetter from Sunshine Health, and Florida Blue BlueCare/BlueOptions contract with various components of both systems, but exact facility and physician group inclusion must be confirmed at the NPI and campus level during Open Enrollment.
