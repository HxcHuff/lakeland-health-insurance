Self-Employed Coverage Costs in Polk County for 2027

Direct answer (2027 plan year): Most self-employed people in Lakeland and Polk County with no employees buy ACA coverage on HealthCare.gov. Premium tax credits use estimated 2027 household income (net self-employment profit plus other taxable income), reconcile at tax time, and require checking Polk County doctors on the exact plan ID before you enroll.

Who this is for

  • Independent contractors, 1099 workers and gig workers
  • Sole proprietors and single-member LLC owners
  • Real estate agents, consultants and tradespeople
  • Owner-operators and small-business owners who do not offer group coverage
  • Spouses who work in a family business

If you have employees who are not family members or owners, group coverage or an individual coverage HRA may be a separate option. That is a different review.

The self-employed Marketplace problem: income you cannot predict

The Marketplace asks for this year's household income before the year happens. For self-employed people:

  • Use net profit: revenue minus business expenses, the figure that will land on Schedule C.
  • Include everything else: a spouse's wages, unemployment, Social Security and taxable retirement withdrawals.
  • Include leftover W-2 wages if you left a job this year.
  • Update HealthCare.gov when a big contract lands or falls through. Seasonal work, such as construction or tourism, can swing monthly income.

For 2027 coverage, credits apply from 100% to 400% of the poverty line: $15,960 to $63,840 for one person, and $33,000 to $132,000 for a family of four. Estimating too low can mean repaying credit at tax time. Estimating too high means you get the difference back as a refund. See how much income qualifies and what happens if income changes after you enroll.

Polk County by the numbers

CMS's 2026 Open Enrollment data for Polk County (FIPS 12105):

  • 126,521 residents picked a Marketplace plan; 19,974 of them were new.
  • 120,996 received advance premium tax credits.
  • The average premium was $768 a month before credits and $97 after.
  • Most picked Silver (71,511) or Bronze (52,025).

These figures cover every Polk County enrollee. CMS does not report self-employed buyers separately.

Taxes: the self-employed health insurance deduction

If your business shows a profit, the premiums you pay after any credit may be deductible on Schedule 1 through Form 7206. The deduction and the credit affect each other; IRS Publication 974 explains the method. See the self-employed health insurance deduction guide and confirm with your tax preparer.

What not to use as your main coverage

Short-term medical and fixed indemnity policies can underwrite, exclude pre-existing conditions and cap benefits. They are not ACA-compliant major medical coverage. See what short-term insurance does not cover.

When you can enroll

  • Open Enrollment for 2027 coverage: November 1, 2026 to January 15, 2027. Pick by December 15 for coverage starting January 1.
  • Special Enrollment Period: If you just left a job with health coverage, you may qualify for a window from 60 days before to 60 days after that coverage ends, subject to eligibility. Being self-employed does not by itself open a window.

Common questions

Can self-employed workers in Lakeland use the ACA Marketplace?

Yes. Self-employed workers without employees, including contractors, consultants, freelancers and sole proprietors, can use HealthCare.gov. Premium tax credits depend on household size and estimated income for the coverage year.

What income does the Marketplace use for self-employed workers?

Your expected household income for the coverage year, using net self-employment profit after business expenses, not gross receipts, plus other household income.

What happens if my self-employment income ends up higher than I estimated?

You may have to repay some or all of the extra premium tax credit when you file your federal return. For tax years after 2025, there is no repayment cap, so update HealthCare.gov when your income changes.

Should a self-employed person choose the lowest-premium plan?

Not automatically. Compare the premium with the deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, doctor network and prescription coverage before you enroll.
