Patrius Health Exiting All 26 Mississippi Counties (2027): Deadlines, SEP & Medigap GI Guide

Patrius Health, the Medicare Advantage joint venture between Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Mississippi and Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama, is permanently exiting the individual Medicare Advantage market across all 26 Mississippi counties at 11:59 p.m. on December 31, 2026. Every Blue Advantage PPO member in those counties must choose replacement 2027 coverage or revert automatically to Original Medicare without Part D prescription drug coverage.

Exact 2027 Deadlines & Effective Dates (Do Not Miss These)

Because Patrius Health is terminating all individual Blue Advantage PPO plans across its 26-county Mississippi service area on December 31, 2026, affected Mississippi seniors have three federal enrollment windows to act within:

  • Annual Election Period (AEP, October 15 to December 7, 2026): Enroll in your 2027 replacement coverage by December 7 for seamless January 1, 2027 effective dates and new ID cards before the new year starts.
  • Federal Non-Renewal Special Enrollment Period (December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027): If your 2026 plan is terminated and you miss December 7, your official non-renewal notice keeps a federal SEP open through February 28, 2027:
    • Enrolled December 8 to December 31, 2026: effective January 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled January 1 to January 31, 2027: effective February 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled February 1 to February 28, 2027: effective March 1, 2027.
  • 63-Day Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window (November 2, 2026 through roughly March 4, 2027): Displaced members returning to Original Medicare may purchase an eligible standardized Medigap plan (Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L, plus Plans C or F if eligible prior to January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting, paired with a standalone 2027 Part D drug plan (featuring the $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 defined-standard deductible). Note: enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific Medigap Guaranteed Issue right.

Fallback exposure if you take no action: Reverting automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 removes Part D prescription drug coverage and leaves you responsible for the 2027 Original Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible of $1,776 per benefit period and 20% Part B coinsurance with no annual out-of-pocket cap.

Which 26 Mississippi Counties Are Affected and Are Hospitals Open?

All Mississippi hospitals and doctor groups remain open. UMMC, Baptist Memorial, North Mississippi Health Services (NMHS), Singing River, and Memorial Health System Gulfport are unaffected operationally. The Patrius Health shutdown covers these 26 Mississippi counties: Calhoun, Chickasaw, Clay, Copiah, George, Hancock, Harrison, Hinds, Itawamba, Jackson, Kemper, Lafayette, Lauderdale, Lee, Lowndes, Madison, Marshall, Monroe, Pearl River, Pontotoc, Rankin, Stone, Tate, Tishomingo, Union, and Webster.

Before choosing a replacement 2027 Medicare Advantage carrier or transitioning to Original Medicare + Medigap Plan G, verify every specialist and hospital campus by exact 2027 Plan ID (note: Patrius Health's exit applies to its 26 Mississippi counties; BCBS of Alabama continues its own separate home-state offerings in Alabama).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Patrius Health shutting down all Mississippi Medicare Advantage plans for 2027?

Yes. Patrius Health, the joint venture between Blue Cross & Blue Shield of Mississippi and Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama, is discontinuing all individual Blue Advantage PPO Medicare Advantage plans across all 26 Mississippi counties effective December 31, 2026.

Which Mississippi counties are impacted by the Patrius Health exit?

All 26 counties in the Patrius Health service area are affected: Calhoun, Chickasaw, Clay, Copiah, George, Hancock, Harrison, Hinds, Itawamba, Jackson, Kemper, Lafayette, Lauderdale, Lee, Lowndes, Madison, Marshall, Monroe, Pearl River, Pontotoc, Rankin, Stone, Tate, Tishomingo, Union, and Webster.

Can displaced Patrius Health members buy Mississippi Medigap Plan G without health underwriting?

Yes. Within the federal 63-day Guaranteed Issue window (roughly November 2, 2026 through March 4, 2027), displaced Patrius Health enrollees can purchase standardized Medigap Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L (or C/F if eligible before January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting using their official termination notice. Enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific GI right.

What happens on January 1, 2027 if I do not pick a replacement plan?

Doing nothing returns you automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 without Part D prescription drug coverage, without an annual out-of-pocket cap, and subject to the 2027 Part A $1,776 inpatient deductible and 20% Part B coinsurance.
