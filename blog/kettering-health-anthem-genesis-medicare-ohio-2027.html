What Changes on January 1, 2027 at Kettering Health & Genesis HealthCare

Ohio Medicare Advantage enrollees face two high-impact regional hospital contract shifts entering 2027:

  • Kettering Health & Anthem Medicare Advantage (Dayton & Miami Valley): Effective January 1, 2027, Kettering Health hospitals, clinics, and physicians transition out of network for Anthem Medicare Advantage plans, including Anthem MyCare. This adds Anthem to Kettering Health's prior exits from Humana and Devoted Health Medicare Advantage networks.
  • Genesis HealthCare System (Zanesville & Southeastern Ohio): Genesis notified multiple Medicare Advantage carriers—including Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Medical Mutual, and CareSource—that its MA contracts were slated to terminate after 2026 absent finalized renewal agreements, making direct plan-by-plan confirmation essential before December 7.
  • Original Medicare + Medigap Unaffected: Both Kettering Health and Genesis HealthCare continue accepting Original Medicare (Parts A and B) paired with any standardized Medigap policy (Plan G or Plan N) nationwide.

Compare Contracted 2027 Dayton & Ohio Alternatives Before December 7

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kettering Health leaving Anthem Medicare Advantage in 2027?

Yes. Kettering Health announced it will go out of network with Anthem Medicare Advantage plans, including Anthem MyCare, effective January 1, 2027.

Does Kettering Health accept Humana or Devoted Medicare Advantage in 2027?

Kettering Health previously exited Humana and Devoted Health Medicare Advantage networks, so Dayton seniors leaving Anthem should verify exact 2027 contracted carriers rather than assuming those plans cover Kettering.

Does Original Medicare with Medigap still cover Kettering Health and Genesis HealthCare?

Yes. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) with any standardized Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy remains accepted at both Kettering Health and Genesis HealthCare facilities participating in Medicare.

Are emergency visits at Kettering Health still covered if my Medicare Advantage plan is out of network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Kettering Health are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status.
