Upstate, Midlands & Lowcountry Hospital Networks in 2027

Across South Carolina's three primary regions — the Upstate (Greenville, Spartanburg, Anderson), the Midlands (Columbia, Lexington, Sumter), and the Lowcountry & Pee Dee (Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Summerville, Florence, Myrtle Beach) — healthcare is anchored by Prisma Health (18 hospitals including Greenville Memorial Hospital, Greer Memorial, Patewood, Oconee Memorial, Prisma Health Richland, Prisma Health Baptist, and Tuomey) and MUSC Health (the Medical University of South Carolina in Charleston, Shawn Jenkins Children's Hospital, Hollings Cancer Center, plus regional campuses in Columbia, Florence, Lancaster, and Orangeburg).

In addition, Bon Secours St. Francis and Spartanburg Regional Healthcare System serve the Upstate, Lexington Medical Center anchors West Columbia, and Roper St. Francis Healthcare & McLeod Health serve Charleston, Florence, and Horry County.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 South Carolina ACA Marketplace plans cover Prisma Health in Greenville and Columbia?

Select BlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina BlueEssentials tiers, Ambetter from Absolute Total Care, and specific Molina or Oscar sub-networks in the Upstate and Midlands cover Prisma Health hospitals, though narrow HMO networks must be verified by county.

Do Charleston ACA plans cover both MUSC Health and Roper St. Francis Healthcare in 2027?

Some Lowcountry Marketplace plans in Charleston, Berkeley, and Dorchester counties align primarily with either MUSC Health or Roper St. Francis Healthcare, so enrollees should verify both hospital facilities and MUSC Physicians faculty billing before enrolling.

How do York County and Fort Mill residents handle cross-border care in Charlotte, North Carolina?

South Carolina ACA Marketplace plans generally cover non-emergency care only within South Carolina network boundaries, so Rock Hill and Fort Mill residents using Piedmont Medical Center or Atrium/Novant clinics must verify whether cross-state specialty referrals are permitted.
