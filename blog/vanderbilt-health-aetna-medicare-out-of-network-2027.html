Vanderbilt Health Leaving Aetna Medicare Advantage (2027)

Effective January 1, 2027, Vanderbilt Health hospitals and specialists across Nashville and Middle Tennessee move out of network for all Aetna Medicare Advantage individual and group plans (except Vanderbilt Clarksville Hospital), alongside select UnitedHealthcare plan exclusions.

What Changes at Vanderbilt Health on January 1, 2027

Middle Tennessee Medicare beneficiaries who visit Vanderbilt University Medical Center (VUMC), Vanderbilt-Ingram Cancer Center, Vanderbilt Wilson County, Vanderbilt Bedford, or Vanderbilt Tullahoma-Harton face major Medicare Advantage changes on January 1, 2027:

  • All Aetna Medicare Advantage Plans Out of Network: Starting January 1, 2027, Vanderbilt Health will be out of network for all Aetna Medicare Advantage individual and group retiree plans, with the sole facility exception of Vanderbilt Clarksville Hospital.
  • Select UnitedHealthcare MA Restrictions: Vanderbilt Health also remains out of network on select UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plan designs such as UHC Compass and UHC Complete (C-SNP)—making it critical to verify your exact 2027 contract and plan ID rather than relying on the insurer brand name.
  • Original Medicare + Medigap Unaffected: Original Medicare (Parts A and B) with any Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plan G or Plan N) continues to provide nationwide access across Vanderbilt Health campuses without HMO/PPO network walls.

Act Before December 7 to Avoid Out-of-Network Costs in January

Hospital-insurer contract terminations do not automatically grant a mid-year Special Enrollment Period. Middle Tennessee seniors currently in an Aetna Medicare Advantage plan who want scheduled care, oncology, cardiology, or surgery at Vanderbilt Health in 2027 should compare replacement 2027 plans before the December 7, 2026 AEP deadline (or during the January 1 – March 31 Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period if already enrolled in MA).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vanderbilt Health leaving Aetna Medicare Advantage in 2027?

Yes. Effective January 1, 2027, Vanderbilt Health will be out of network for all Aetna Medicare Advantage individual and group plans, with the sole exception of the Vanderbilt Clarksville Hospital facility.

Does Vanderbilt Health accept all UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans in 2027?

No. Vanderbilt Health is out of network for select UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage products such as UHC Compass and Complete (C-SNP). Always confirm your exact 2027 UHC plan ID before enrolling.

Are emergency room visits at Vanderbilt University Medical Center still covered if my plan is out of network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Vanderbilt hospitals are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of network status.

What is the deadline to switch out of Aetna Medicare Advantage before January 1, 2027?

Medicare Annual Enrollment closes December 7, 2026 for plan changes starting January 1, 2027. Existing Medicare Advantage members also have a one-time Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period from January 1 through March 31, 2027.
