Moving to a New State in 2027? Your Step-by-Step Health Insurance Checklist

Most ACA Marketplace plans and Medicare Advantage plans are strictly county-based - keeping an old-state HMO after moving leaves you with emergency-only coverage. Here is the exact checklist for switching smoothly across any of the 23 states where we are licensed.

Two Moving Traps That Catch Relocating Households Off Guard

  • The ACA Move Prior-Coverage Rule: Under HealthCare.gov rules, a Move SEP generally requires proving you had minimum essential coverage for at least 1 day during the 60 days before your move (unless you are moving from abroad, a U.S. territory, or a non-expansion Medicaid gap state).
  • Medicare Advantage Move = Medigap Guaranteed Issue Ticket: Moving out of your Medicare Advantage plan's service area gives you a federal 63-day Medigap Guaranteed Issue right (Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L with no health questions) - a rare opportunity to switch from Advantage to Medigap cleanly if you prefer nationwide Original Medicare access.

4-Step Relocation Timeline (30 Days Before Through 30 Days After)

  1. 30 Days Before Moving: Look up your new ZIP code's Marketplace platform (HealthCare.gov vs. state-run portal such as Georgia Access, Maryland Health Connection, Get Covered NJ, Virginia's Insurance Marketplace, or Washington Healthplanfinder) and verify new local doctors.
  2. On Move Day (Or Month Prior for Medicare): Report your address change to Social Security (ssa.gov) for Medicare or submit your Move SEP application for ACA coverage so your effective date starts the 1st of the following month.
  3. Cancel Your Old-State Plan Only After New Effective Date Is Confirmed: Terminate your old-state Marketplace plan effective the day before your new-state plan begins so you never pay double premiums or create an unsubsidized overlap on Form 1095-A.
  4. Check Deductible Reset Timing: Moving to a new state policy resets ACA deductibles to $0, so schedule non-urgent tests before switching if you already met your old deductible.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I have to switch health insurance after moving to a new state?

You have 60 days from your move date to enroll in a new ACA Marketplace plan (if you had prior coverage in the previous 60 days), and Medicare Advantage or Part D enrollees get a Move SEP starting the month before the move through 2 full months after.

Does moving out of state give me a Guaranteed Issue right for Medigap?

Yes, if you are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan and move outside its service area. You can return to Original Medicare and buy Medigap Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L without medical underwriting within 63 days.

Does my Original Medicare + Medigap Plan G work if I move to another state?

Yes. Original Medicare and standardized Medigap policies cover Medicare-participating doctors and hospitals nationwide, though you must update your address and enroll in a Part D plan offered in your new state.
