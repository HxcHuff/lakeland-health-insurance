What to Verify Before You Enroll

  1. Provider status for your PCP and specialists under the exact plan.
  2. Facility status for Lakeland Regional Health and Watson Clinic locations you use.
  3. Referral and prior authorization rules for specialty visits and procedures.
  4. Prescription coverage tier and pharmacy network for your key medications.

High-Risk Network Mistakes

  • Assuming all HMO or Medicare Advantage products have identical Watson Clinic access.
  • Skipping referral-rule checks until after enrollment.
  • Checking only a carrier name instead of your exact plan and network.
  • Not reconfirming directly with provider offices before final enrollment.

FAQ

Can Watson Clinic always be my primary care provider?

Not always. Some plans may restrict PCP assignment or require referrals based on network design.

Is Lakeland Regional automatically in-network for every plan?

No. You still need to confirm your exact plan and service line participation before enrollment.

What is the safest verification method?

Check the carrier directory by exact plan ID, then verify with the provider office and keep a dated note of the confirmation.
