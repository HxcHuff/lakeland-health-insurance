BCBSTX, Memorial Hermann & CHRISTUS Medicare Exit (2027)

Three major Texas Medicare Advantage issuers—Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas (BCBSTX), Memorial Hermann Health Plan, and CHRISTUS Health Plan—are ending individual Medicare Advantage coverage effective December 31, 2026. What Is Ending on December 31, 2026 Across Texas Medicare Plans

Thousands of Texas Medicare beneficiaries received official non-renewal notices this fall as three major health plans exit Texas individual Medicare Advantage (and, for BCBSTX, standalone Part D) effective December 31, 2026:

  • Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas (BCBSTX): Discontinuing its individual Blue Cross Medicare Advantage plans, Dual Care Plus HMO Special Needs Plans (SNP), and standalone Blue Cross MedicareRx (Part D) prescription drug plans for 2027. BCBSTX has directed members to review alternatives (including affiliate HealthSpring options), but enrollees must actively select a new 2027 plan.
  • Memorial Hermann Health Plan: Shutting down its health plan subsidiary across its 6-county Greater Houston service area effective December 31, 2026, displacing more than 14,000 Medicare Advantage members. Memorial Hermann hospitals and physicians are not closing and continue accepting other contracted 2027 Medicare plans.
  • CHRISTUS Health Plan: Ending all Texas Medicare Advantage products—including Guardian, Plus, and Complete HMO plans—effective December 31, 2026, impacting nearly 9,000 members across East Texas, Beaumont, and Corpus Christi counties. CHRISTUS hospitals and clinics remain open and accept other contracted insurers and Original Medicare.

Critical fallback warning: If your Medicare Advantage plan terminates and you do not choose a replacement by December 31, 2026, CMS returns you to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027—leaving you without Part D drug coverage, without routine dental/vision benefits, and without an annual out-of-pocket maximum.

Your 63-Day Guaranteed Issue Medigap Right & Extended Special Enrollment Period

Unlike a routine benefit tweak, a full plan termination or service-area exit triggers two powerful federal Medicare protections:

  • 63-Day Guaranteed Issue Medigap Right: When your Medicare Advantage plan ends its contract in your county, federal law grants a Guaranteed Issue (GI) window—typically beginning 60 days before coverage ends and lasting 63 days after December 31, 2026 (into early March 2027)—to buy standardized Medigap (including Plan G or Plan N, depending on Medicare eligibility date) without medical underwriting. Keep your official non-renewal letter as proof.
  • Special Enrollment Period (SEP) Through February 28, 2027: In addition to AEP (October 15 – December 7, 2026), beneficiaries whose MA plan is discontinued qualify for a federal non-renewal SEP running from December 8, 2026 through the last day of February 2027 to pick a replacement Medicare Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare with standalone Part D.

4 Steps Before Your Texas Plan Ends December 31

  1. Save Your Official Non-Renewal Letter: Do not discard the termination notice mailed by BCBSTX, Memorial Hermann Health Plan, or CHRISTUS Health Plan—insurers require it to process a Guaranteed Issue Medigap application without health questions.
  2. Enroll by December 7 (or December 31) to Prevent Any January 1 Gap: Selecting a replacement during AEP (ending December 7) ensures your new ID card arrives before January 1; choosing via the non-renewal SEP after December 31 delays the new effective date to the first of the following month.
  3. Verify Doctors, Hospitals & 2027 Drug Formularies Together: Confirm your exact physicians at Memorial Hermann, CHRISTUS, Houston Methodist, MD Anderson, or Baylor Scott & White and run every prescription under the 2027 Part D $2,400 out-of-pocket cap on Medicare.gov.
  4. Compare MA Replacement vs. Guaranteed Issue Medigap: If you have chronic health conditions and worried you could never pass medical underwriting later, a plan non-renewal is a rare federal pass to move into Medigap Plan G or Plan N with zero health questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas dropping Medicare Advantage and Part D for 2027?

Yes. Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Texas (BCBSTX) announced it is discontinuing its individual Medicare Advantage plans, Dual Care Plus HMO SNP plans, and standalone Blue Cross MedicareRx Part D plans for the 2027 plan year, requiring affected members to choose a new plan.

Are Memorial Hermann and CHRISTUS hospitals closing because their Medicare Advantage plans ended?

No. Memorial Hermann Health System and CHRISTUS Health hospitals, clinics, and employed physicians remain fully open. Only their in-house Medicare Advantage insurance plans are ending on December 31, 2026, so patients need a new 2027 insurance carrier that contracts with those facilities.

Do displaced BCBSTX, Memorial Hermann, and CHRISTUS members get Guaranteed Issue Medigap rights?

Yes. When your Medicare Advantage plan terminates in your service area on December 31, 2026, federal rules generally provide a 63-day Guaranteed Issue window to enroll in eligible Medigap policies without medical underwriting, provided you supply your official non-renewal notice.

What happens if I do nothing before December 31, 2026?

If your Texas Medicare Advantage plan terminates and you do not elect a replacement plan, you revert automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027—losing built-in Part D prescription coverage and annual out-of-pocket spending protection until you enroll via your non-renewal SEP (through February 28, 2027).
