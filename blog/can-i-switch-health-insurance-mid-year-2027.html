Mid-Year Plan Switch Rules (2027)

Can I Switch Health Insurance Plans in the Middle of the Year?

Realizing in April or July that your plan has a narrow network, steep deductible, or excluded medication is frustrating. Here is every legal mid-year switch window for both ACA Marketplace and Medicare in 2027 - plus what happens to your deductible if you switch mid-year.

Direct answer: You can switch health insurance mid-year in 2027 only if you qualify for an official Special Enrollment Period (SEP) or an active Medicare switch window. For ACA Marketplace plans, you need a qualifying life event (losing coverage, moving, marriage, birth/adoption - note that starting in 2026 and 2027, the low-income 150% FPL monthly window no longer comes with federal ACA subsidies). For Medicare, switches are available during MA OEP (Jan 1 to Mar 31), 5-Star SEPs, moves, or Extra Help.

Critical Mid-Year Warning: Your Deductible Resets to $0!

Before switching ACA Marketplace or employer plans mid-year - even when you have a valid Special Enrollment Period - check how much of your current year's deductible and out-of-pocket maximum you have already paid. Switching carriers mid-year almost always resets your deductible and out-of-pocket accumulators back to $0 on the new policy. If you already paid $4,000 toward a $6,000 out-of-pocket max, switching carriers in August could double your annual medical costs.

What about Medigap? You can apply to switch Medigap policies any month of the year, though outside a guaranteed issue window or state birthday rule, medical underwriting applies - and your Part B deductible ($292 in 2027) does not reset because Medicare tracks it federally.

4 Steps to Check Before Attempting a Mid-Year Plan Change

  1. Verify Your Exact 60-Day Clock: ACA Qualifying Life Events require submitting proof (such as a loss-of-coverage letter or utility bill for a move) within 60 days of the event.
  2. Confirm Prior-Coverage Requirements for Moves: For an ACA Move SEP, HealthCare.gov generally requires showing at least 1 day of minimum essential coverage during the 60 days prior to moving.
  3. Check Whether a Doctor Drop Qualifies for Continuity of Care: Even without an SEP, active pregnancy, chemotherapy, or scheduled surgery patients can often request temporary in-network continuity of care directly from the carrier.
  4. Never Cancel Your Old Plan Until the New Plan Is Confirmed Effective: Voluntarily cancelling coverage before your replacement binder payment clears can leave you uninsured with no SEP.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the 150% FPL monthly Marketplace SEP still available in 2027?

Not with federal subsidies. Starting in 2026 and 2027, federal law blocks Premium Tax Credits for enrollees using the 150% FPL low-income monthly window, so shoppers who need subsidized coverage outside Open Enrollment must qualify through a standard life event such as loss of coverage, moving, marriage, or birth/adoption.

When is the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA OEP) in 2027?

Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment runs January 1 through March 31, 2027. If you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, you may make one switch to another Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare plus a standalone Part D plan.

Does my deductible carry over if I switch health insurance plans mid-year?

Usually no when changing insurance companies. Your deductible and out-of-pocket maximum typically reset to $0 on a new carrier's policy, so always weigh year-to-date spending before switching.

Can I drop my employer plan mid-year just because I found a cheaper plan?

Not unless you experience an IRS Section 125 qualifying status change or your employer's plan renews on a non-calendar schedule, and voluntarily dropping an affordable job plan does not unlock ACA Marketplace tax credits.
