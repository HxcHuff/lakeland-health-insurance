Sentara & VCU Health Leaving Anthem in Virginia (2027)

Two of Virginia's largest health systems—Sentara Health and VCU Health—are parting ways with key Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield plans effective January 1, 2027. Here is what changes across Hampton Roads, Richmond, and Central Virginia before the December 7 Medicare and January 29 Virginia Marketplace deadlines.

What Changes on January 1, 2027 for Sentara Health, VCU Health & Anthem

Virginia patients enrolled in Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield face two distinct contract splits taking effect January 1, 2027:

  • Sentara Health & Anthem (Medicare Advantage + ACA Exchange): Sentara Health and Anthem did not reach a renewal agreement covering Anthem Medicare Advantage and Anthem ACA Exchange plans on Virginia's Insurance Marketplace (with Medicaid transitioning in late January). Effective January 1, 2027, Sentara hospitals, outpatient centers, and Sentara Medical Group physicians transition to out-of-network status for those Anthem lines across Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Williamsburg, Charlottesville, Harrisonburg, and Northern Virginia.
  • VCU Health & Anthem (Standard Medicare Advantage Carved Out): While VCU Health and Anthem renewed their commercial agreement, standard Anthem Medicare Advantage plans (HMO, PPO, and C-SNP) move out of network at VCU Medical Center and VCU Health physicians starting January 1, 2027. Exception: Anthem Medicare Advantage Dual Eligible Special Needs Plans (D-SNP) remain in network at VCU Health.

Virginia Enrollment Windows: December 7 (Medicare) & January 29 (ACA)

  • Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (Ends December 7, 2026): Because a hospital contract split alone does not create an automatic mid-year Special Enrollment Period, Anthem MA members who rely on Sentara or VCU Health should switch to a contracted 2027 Medicare Advantage carrier—or evaluate Original Medicare plus Medigap—before December 7 (or during MA OEP Jan 1 – Mar 31 if already in MA).
  • Virginia's Insurance Marketplace (Through January 29, 2027): Under-65 Virginians on Anthem ACA plans who use Sentara Health should actively switch carriers on Virginia's Insurance Marketplace (marketplace.virginia.gov)—enrolling by December 15 for January 1 coverage, with Open Enrollment remaining open through January 29, 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Sentara Health hospitals leaving Anthem Medicare Advantage and ACA plans in 2027?

Yes. Sentara Health and Anthem did not renew contracts for Anthem Medicare Advantage and Anthem ACA Exchange plans, moving Sentara facilities and providers out of network effective January 1, 2027.

Is VCU Health leaving all Anthem Medicare Advantage plans in 2027?

VCU Health is going out of network with standard Anthem Medicare Advantage HMO, PPO, and C-SNP plans effective January 1, 2027. Anthem Medicare Advantage Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP) and renewed Anthem commercial plans remain in network.

Does Original Medicare with Medigap still work at both Sentara Health and VCU Health?

Yes. Original Medicare (Parts A and B), with or without any standardized Medigap policy, continues to be accepted across Sentara Health and VCU Health facilities that participate in Medicare without commercial Medicare Advantage network lockouts.

When is the deadline to switch plans in Virginia for 2027?

Medicare Annual Enrollment closes December 7, 2026 for January 1 changes (with MA OEP running January 1 through March 31 for existing MA enrollees). Virginia's Insurance Marketplace Open Enrollment runs through January 29, 2027, with December 15 as the deadline for January 1 coverage.
