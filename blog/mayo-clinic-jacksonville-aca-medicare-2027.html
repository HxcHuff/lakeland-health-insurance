Why Mayo Clinic Jacksonville Network Verification Is Critical for 2027

Located on San Pablo Road in Jacksonville, Mayo Clinic in Florida is one of the Southeast's premier quaternary destination medical centers, anchoring NCI-designated oncology care at the Mayo Clinic Comprehensive Cancer Center, solid-organ transplant programs, complex neurosurgery, and advanced cardiology across Duval, St. Johns, Clay, and Nassau counties.

Because Mayo Clinic Jacksonville bills as an integrated academic medical group and hospital facility, its insurance participation is far more selective than community hospital systems. Enrolling in a narrow-network ACA HMO or an uncontracted Medicare Advantage plan during Open Enrollment can result in out-of-network denials or upfront deposit requirements for non-emergency consultations.

3 Steps to Protect Mayo Clinic Access in 2027

  1. Distinguish BlueOptions from myBlue and BlueCare on HealthCare.gov: When shopping for 2027 ACA coverage in Duval, St. Johns, or statewide Florida, filter specifically by Mayo Clinic's San Pablo Road facility and group NPI. Selecting a lower-premium myBlue HMO will direct your care to community hospitals rather than Mayo Clinic.
  2. Compare Original Medicare + Medigap Against Medicare Advantage: Many retirees move to Northeast Florida assuming any national PPO Medicare Advantage card is accepted at Mayo Clinic Jacksonville. In practice, Mayo Clinic restricts or declines non-contracted Medicare Advantage plans, making Original Medicare paired with Medigap Plan G the most reliable path.
  3. Coordinate Regional Primary Care & Pediatric Needs: Families who use Baptist Health Jacksonville or Nemours Children's Health alongside Mayo Clinic should confirm that their chosen plan tier covers both systems simultaneously.

Emergency Room & Continuity of Care Protections (No Surprises Act & FL Statute § 627.64194): Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at the Mayo Clinic Hospital emergency department in Jacksonville are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of plan network. For patients in active oncology, transplant, or complex specialty care whose plan network changes for 2027, Florida Statute § 627.64194 and federal continuity-of-care rules allow up to 90 days of continued in-network benefits upon timely clinical authorization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 Florida ACA Marketplace plans are in-network at Mayo Clinic Jacksonville?

Mayo Clinic in Florida (Jacksonville) generally participates in Florida Blue BlueOptions PPO plans on the individual ACA Marketplace, while narrow-network HMO products such as Florida Blue myBlue, most BlueCare HMO tiers, Ambetter, and Oscar Health typically exclude non-emergency care at Mayo Clinic Jacksonville.

Does Mayo Clinic Jacksonville accept Medicare Advantage or Original Medicare with Medigap in 2027?

Mayo Clinic in Florida accepts Original Medicare (Parts A and B) paired with any standardized Medigap policy such as Plan G or Plan N, whereas most Medicare Advantage HMO plans and many non-contracted Medicare Advantage PPO plans are out-of-network or require prior authorization for specialty consultations.

What protections apply if I need emergency care or ongoing oncology treatment at Mayo Clinic Jacksonville?

Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Mayo Clinic Hospital in Jacksonville are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of plan network, and Florida Statute § 627.64194 provides up to 90 days of continuity of care for active treatment when a network contract changes.
