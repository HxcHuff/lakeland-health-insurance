For decades, Humana has been one of the most widely held Medicare Advantage plans across Polk County, Florida. However, Watson Clinic has officially announced that beginning January 1, 2027, it will terminate all contract participation with Humana. This decision directly impacts thousands of patients in Lakeland, Winter Haven, Bartow, Auburndale, Plant City, and surrounding communities who rely on Watson Clinic doctors, specialists, urgent care clinics, and surgical centers.

If you currently have a Humana Medicare Advantage plan and want to continue seeing your Watson Clinic doctors without out-of-network penalties or denied claims, you have an immediate window of action during Medicare Annual Enrollment (AEP), which runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026.

What happens if you stay on Humana in 2027?

If you take no action during the fall enrollment window and remain enrolled in your Humana plan on January 1, 2027, your access to Watson Clinic will change dramatically depending on your plan design:

  • Humana Medicare Advantage HMO plans: HMO plans typically offer zero coverage for out-of-network services except in true emergency medical situations. If you visit a Watson Clinic primary care doctor, specialist, or walk-in clinic after January 1, 2027, the claim will be denied by Humana, leaving you personally responsible for 100% of all medical bills and facility fees.
  • Humana Medicare Advantage PPO plans: PPO plans do permit members to visit out-of-network healthcare providers, but at significantly higher cost-sharing. Members are usually subject to separate, higher out-of-network deductibles and 40% to 50% coinsurance instead of low flat copays. Furthermore, out-of-network providers may balance bill for charges that exceed Medicare allowable rates.
  • Continuity of Care protections: If you are undergoing an active course of medical treatment for a serious condition (such as ongoing chemotherapy, acute post-operative surgical care, or late-stage pregnancy), you may apply to Humana for temporary Continuity of Care. If approved, this temporary authorization typically lasts 30 to 90 days to allow for a transition of care, after which out-of-network terms take effect.

Critical enrollment deadlines for Polk County residents

If you need to replace your Humana plan to keep your Watson Clinic doctors, note these important Medicare enrollment dates:

  • October 15, 2026: Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) begins. Beneficiaries can compare 2027 plan options, drug formularies, and provider networks.
  • December 7, 2026: Annual Enrollment Period closes at midnight. This is the firm deadline to make a change that takes effect on January 1, 2027.
  • January 1, 2027: Watson Clinic officially stops accepting Humana plans. New Medicare coverage chosen during AEP takes effect.
  • January 1 – March 31, 2027: Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA-OEP). If you are already enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan as of January 1, you have one opportunity to switch to another Medicare Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare with a Part D prescription plan.

Action checklist: what Humana patients should do now

Do not wait until the December 7 deadline to review your options. Follow these practical steps to ensure uninterrupted medical care and prescription coverage:

  1. List your Watson Clinic healthcare providers: Write down your primary care doctor and any specialists you see regularly (such as cardiologists, oncologists, dermatologists, or orthopedists).
  2. Gather your current medication list: Write down the exact name, dosage, and frequency of every prescription drug you take. Drug formularies change every plan year, and alternative Medicare Advantage or Part D plans may place your medications on different cost tiers.
  3. Compare 2027 Polk County plans: Review plans from Florida Blue, UnitedHealthcare, and BayCarePlus that include your physicians in their provider directories.
  4. Evaluate Medigap vs. Medicare Advantage: If you prefer complete doctor freedom without prior authorizations or provider network cancellations, evaluate Original Medicare paired with a Medicare Supplement (such as Plan G or Plan N) and a standalone Part D plan.

Common questions

Does Watson Clinic accept Humana in 2027?

No. Watson Clinic announced that it will no longer accept Humana insurance plans starting January 1, 2027. This applies to Humana Medicare Advantage plans as well as Humana commercial plans.

What happens if I stay on my Humana Medicare Advantage plan in 2027?

If you remain enrolled in a Humana Medicare Advantage plan after January 1, 2027, Watson Clinic physicians, clinics, and outpatient facilities will be out of network. Humana HMO members will generally have zero coverage for non-emergency visits at Watson Clinic, while PPO members will face substantially higher out-of-network coinsurance and deductibles.

Which Medicare Advantage plans does Watson Clinic accept for 2027?

Watson Clinic contracts with several major Medicare Advantage carriers in Polk County for 2027, including Florida Blue (BlueMedicare), UnitedHealthcare, and BayCare Health Plans (BayCarePlus). Specific physician participation and HMO specialty-only restrictions vary by plan ID, so always verify your doctors on the exact 2027 formulary and provider directory.

Does Watson Clinic accept Original Medicare and Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans?

Yes. Watson Clinic accepts Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) along with Medicare Supplement insurance (Medigap) plans. Because Medigap plans do not use provider network restrictions, you can see any Watson Clinic provider who accepts Medicare assignment without network disruption.

When is the deadline to change Medicare plans to keep Watson Clinic doctors?

The Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026. Any plan change made during this window takes effect on January 1, 2027. If you miss this deadline, you may be locked into your Humana plan for the year unless you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period or switch during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period between January 1 and March 31.
