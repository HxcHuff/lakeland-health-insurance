How to change your Florida Blue myBlue PCP

Watson Clinic may accept myBlue, but acceptance does not mean every Watson physician can be assigned as your primary care provider. Here is how to verify the doctor and submit the change.

Published August 11, 2026 | By David Huff, Licensed Florida Agent #W371813 | NPN 18213932

Direct answer: Yes. Watson Clinic's current insurance page lists Florida Blue myBlue among its accepted ACA, Marketplace, and Exchange plans. However, a Watson Clinic physician can be selected as your PCP only if that individual doctor participates in your exact myBlue network, practices at the listed location, and has an available primary-care panel.

Acceptance is not the same as PCP assignment

Florida Blue states that myBlue members must select a participating primary care physician. Watson Clinic states that it accepts Florida Blue myBlue, but also directs patients to confirm that the plan is in network.

Those statements answer different questions. A clinic-level insurance listing does not confirm every doctor, location, specialty, referral path, or open patient panel. For a reliable answer, verify all four of these details:

  • The exact myBlue plan shown in the member account.
  • The individual Watson Clinic physician, not only the clinic name.
  • The physician's specific practice location.
  • Whether the physician can accept new myBlue patients as their PCP.

How to change your PCP in the Florida Blue app

If the app is displaying the digital insurance card, first use the back arrow to return to the dashboard. Florida Blue's published mobile instructions identify the following pathway:

  1. Open the Florida Blue app and sign in. Use the member account tied to the current myBlue coverage.
  2. Select Find & Get Care. This option appears on the app dashboard.
  3. Open the Get Care tab. Then select My Care Team.
  4. Select Update My PCP. The currently assigned primary care doctor should appear before you begin the change.
  5. Choose the covered family member. Complete this step if the account includes more than one person.
  6. Search for the Watson Clinic physician. Search by the doctor's full name when possible. If needed, search Watson Clinic or a Lakeland ZIP code and filter for primary care.
  7. Review the physician profile. Confirm the doctor, Watson Clinic location, myBlue network information, PCP classification, and new-patient availability.
  8. Submit the change. Save the confirmation and the effective date shown by Florida Blue.

Do not use the insurance card alone to verify the doctor

The card confirms the member's plan type and identifiers. It does not establish that a particular Watson Clinic physician is selectable as the member's PCP. Do not post or send an unredacted digital card outside a secure client workflow.

Confirm the change before scheduling care

Submitting the request does not necessarily mean the new assignment is immediately active. Review the confirmation for the effective date. If an appointment is approaching, call the Member Services number on the back of the Florida Blue ID card and confirm that the new PCP will be active on the date of service.

What if the Watson Clinic doctor does not appear?

Do not assume the physician is covered or assignable. Florida Blue notes that a doctor may be missing because the physician does not accept the member's plan option, does not participate in the applicable network, or has directory information that is being updated.

Call the Member Services number on the back of the Florida Blue ID card. You can also contact Watson Clinic Patient Relations at 863-680-7213. Ask:

Is this individual physician, at this specific Watson Clinic location, participating as a primary care provider under my exact Florida Blue myBlue plan, and is the physician accepting new patients?

For changes to an existing Florida Blue account, use the Member Services number on the member ID card.

Why this distinction matters

The useful question is not simply, “Does Watson Clinic accept Florida Blue?” The useful question is whether the member's exact plan, named doctor, location, PCP assignment, and effective date all match. This is the same provider-verification discipline that should be used before choosing or changing any network-based health plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does Watson Clinic accept Florida Blue myBlue?

Watson Clinic's current insurance page lists Florida Blue myBlue among its accepted ACA, Marketplace, and Exchange plans. Confirm the individual physician and location through the member-specific directory before receiving nonemergency care.

Can every Watson Clinic doctor be selected as a myBlue PCP?

No. The individual physician must participate as a PCP in the member's exact myBlue network and have an available patient panel.

How do I change my PCP in the Florida Blue app?

From the dashboard, select Find & Get Care, open Get Care, select My Care Team, and choose Update My PCP. Search for the physician, submit the change, and save the effective-date confirmation.

What should I do if the Watson Clinic doctor does not appear?

Do not assume the physician is covered. Call Florida Blue using the number on the member ID card and Watson Clinic Patient Relations at 863-680-7213 to verify the doctor, location, PCP status, and patient-panel availability.
