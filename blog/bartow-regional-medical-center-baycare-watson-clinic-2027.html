The Osprey Boulevard Split: BayCare Hospital Next Door to Watson Clinic Bartow

If you live in Bartow (33830), Mulberry (33860), Fort Meade (33841), or Alturas (33820), your main medical hub sits right off US-98 on Osprey Boulevard: Bartow Regional Medical Center at 2200 Osprey Blvd and Watson Clinic Bartow right next door at 2250 Osprey Blvd.

Because the two buildings sit side by side on the same medical campus, many South Polk residents assume they belong to the same insurance network. In reality, Bartow Regional Medical Center is owned and billed by BayCare Health System, while Watson Clinic Bartow bills under Watson Clinic LLP—and many Watson Clinic specialists also admit patients 10 minutes north at the new Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Bartow Regional Medical Center and Watson Clinic Bartow part of the same health system?

No. Although they sit next door to each other on Osprey Boulevard off US-98 in Bartow, Bartow Regional Medical Center is part of BayCare Health System, while Watson Clinic Bartow is part of Watson Clinic LLP. You should verify that both BayCare and Watson Clinic are in-network on your 2027 health plan.

What insurance does Bartow Regional Medical Center accept in 2027?

Bartow Regional Medical Center accepts Original Medicare, Medigap supplements, BayCarePlus Medicare Advantage, and participating 2027 Medicare Advantage and ACA Marketplace plans that hold active BayCare hospital contracts in Polk County. Always verify your exact 2027 plan ID before enrolling.

Are emergency room visits at Bartow Regional Medical Center covered if my plan is out-of-network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency evaluation and stabilization at the Bartow Regional Medical Center emergency department must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of routine network status.

Can Bartow and Mulberry residents use Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital?

Yes, the new Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road is just 10 minutes north of Bartow off US-98 and the Polk Parkway. Make sure your 2027 plan includes an active Orlando Health hospital contract in addition to BayCare and Watson Clinic.
