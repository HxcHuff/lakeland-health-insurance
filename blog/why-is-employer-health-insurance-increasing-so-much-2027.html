What Small Business Owners (2 to 50 Employees) Are Switching to in 2027

Traditional small-group health plans trap small business owners in an impossible loop: participate in double-digit annual renewals, cut benefits to high-deductible tiers employees hate, or drop group coverage entirely. That is why many 2027 employers are replacing traditional group plans with CHOICE Arrangements (ICHRA - Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement) or QSEHRA:

  • Predictable Fixed Budget: The employer sets a exact tax-deductible monthly allowance (e.g., $400/employee or $700/family) with zero surprise renewal spikes and zero minimum participation requirements.
  • Employee Plan Choice: Each worker picks the exact 2027 individual Marketplace plan that covers their own doctors and prescriptions - instead of forcing a 26-year-old and a 59-year-old into the same expensive group PPO.
  • For Employees Stuck on an Expensive Group Plan: Check our 2027 Employer Increase & 10.22% Affordability Guide to see if moving your spouse and children onto subsidized ACA coverage cuts your household payroll deduction immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my employer health insurance go up so much more than general inflation?

Employer medical trend (often 8% to 10%+ nationally, and higher for small groups with large claims) outpaces general CPI because of specialty/GLP-1 drug prices, hospital consolidation facility fees, higher medical utilization, and rising stop-loss reinsurance costs.

Can my employer legally charge me more for health insurance in 2027?

Yes, as long as Applicable Large Employers (50+ full-time equivalents) keep the lowest-cost self-only option at or below 10.22% of applicable wages/income under IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-26 (and employers have no federal requirement to subsidize spouse or dependent tiers).

What is an ICHRA (CHOICE Arrangement) for small businesses?

An Individual Coverage HRA lets employers of any size reimburse employees tax-exempt for individual Marketplace health insurance premiums up to a fixed monthly budget set by the employer, eliminating unpredictable annual group renewals.

Should I drop my employer plan if my premiums went up?

Only after checking the exact 10.22% IRS affordability test and comparing pre-tax payroll savings against after-tax Marketplace rates. Dropping an affordable job plan voluntarily forfeits ACA Marketplace subsidies.
