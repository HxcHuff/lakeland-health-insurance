Navigating Winter Haven's Multi-Specialty Clinics and BayCare Hospitals

In East Polk County (ZIP codes 33880, 33881, 33884, 33823, and 33850), most residents rely on a triad of local healthcare institutions: Bond Clinic (founded in 1948 and affiliated with AdventHealth as AdventHealth Bond Clinic across its downtown and First Street campuses), Gessler Clinic, and BayCare's Winter Haven Hospital and Winter Haven Women's Hospital.

While Lakeland's healthcare conversation often centers on Watson Clinic and Lakeland Regional Health, Winter Haven families face a distinct system split for 2027: because Bond Clinic aligns with AdventHealth's physician network while Winter Haven Hospital is owned by BayCare Health System, patients must confirm that both their clinic doctors and their nearest acute-care hospital are in-network under the same ACA Marketplace or Medicare Advantage plan.

Key Pitfalls to Avoid During Winter Haven Open Enrollment

  • Assuming Clinic and Hospital Share One Contract: Bond Clinic and Gessler Clinic are physician medical groups, whereas Winter Haven Hospital is owned by BayCare Health System. Always verify both the clinic tax ID/NPI and the hospital facility in your 2027 directory.
  • Cigna & Molina Marketplace Exits: If you held a Cigna or Molina ACA plan in 2026, review our 2027 Florida Carrier Exit Guide so you are not auto-mapped into a narrow network that excludes your Winter Haven doctors.
  • Cassidy Cancer Center & Bostick Heart Center Access: Patients receiving oncology or cardiac care at Winter Haven Hospital's specialty centers should confirm that both facility outpatient infusion/imaging and the attending physician group are covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bond Clinic in Winter Haven accept ACA Marketplace plans for 2027?

Bond Clinic participates in select Florida Blue commercial and Marketplace network tiers as well as specific regional exchange plans, though patients must confirm individual physician and specialty department participation for 2027.

Is Winter Haven Hospital in-network with BayCare Medicare Advantage and ACA plans?

Yes. Winter Haven Hospital and Winter Haven Women's Hospital are part of BayCare Health System and participate in BayCarePlus Medicare Advantage plans along with contracted Florida Blue, Ambetter, and Oscar Health exchange tiers.

How do Bond Clinic and Gessler Clinic differ in hospital admitting privileges in Winter Haven?

Many specialists at both Bond Clinic and Gessler Clinic maintain privileges or referral pathways with BayCare's Winter Haven Hospital, but outpatient clinic diagnostic billing is separate from hospital facility contracts and must be verified independently.
