Why Poinciana (34759 vs. 34758) & Four Corners Have a 4-County Insurance Split

In Northeast Polk and the Poinciana / Four Corners corridor, your 5-digit ZIP code determines which county rating area prices your health insurance—even when the closest hospital is across the street in another county:

  • Polk County Side (ZIPs 33837, 33896, 33897, 33844, 34759): Priced under Polk County (Rating Area 53). Your primary in-county hospitals are AdventHealth Heart of Florida on US-27 and BayCare’s Winter Haven Hospital, even if you live in Poinciana Village 7 or 8 (34759) right next to HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital or in ChampionsGate (33896) minutes from AdventHealth Celebration and Orlando Health Horizon West.
  • Osceola County Side (ZIPs 34758, 34746, 34747): Priced under Greater Orlando / Osceola rating rules. Moving two miles across Cypress Parkway from 34758 to 34759 changes both your monthly ACA subsidy benchmark and which Medicare Advantage plan IDs are available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Poinciana ZIP codes 34758 and 34759 have different health insurance plans in 2027?

ZIP code 34758 is in Osceola County, while ZIP code 34759 is in Polk County. Because Medicare Advantage and HealthCare.gov ACA Marketplace plans are filed by county, living on the 34759 Polk County side means your plan is priced and networked under Polk County rules even though HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital sits just across the line in 34758.

Do 2027 health plans cover both AdventHealth Heart of Florida and HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital?

Some broader networks and Original Medicare with Medigap cover both hospitals, but many 2027 ACA Marketplace HMOs and Medicare Advantage plans contract with either AdventHealth or HCA Florida Healthcare. Always verify both facilities by your exact 2027 plan ID.

Are emergency room visits at AdventHealth Heart of Florida and HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital always covered?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency room evaluation and stabilization at AdventHealth Heart of Florida, HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital, and Orlando Health Horizon West Hospital are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of county lines or routine contract status.

Can Four Corners residents in 33896 or 33897 use Orlando Health Horizon West or AdventHealth Celebration?

Yes, if your 2027 Polk County plan includes a regional Central Florida network (or if you have Original Medicare with a Medigap policy). Narrow Polk-only HMO plans may require staying inside Polk County for non-emergency hospital care unless approved by referral.
