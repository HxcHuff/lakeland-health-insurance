The Three-System Hospital Landscape in Middle Tennessee

In Greater Nashville (Davidson, Williamson, Rutherford, Sumner, Wilson, and Robertson counties), hospital care is anchored by three distinct systems: Vanderbilt Health (Vanderbilt University Medical Center, Monroe Carell Jr. Children's Hospital, Vanderbilt Wilson County, and Vanderbilt Bedford), HCA TriStar Health (TriStar Centennial, TriStar Skyline, TriStar Summit, TriStar Hendersonville, and TriStar StoneCrest), and Ascension Saint Thomas (Saint Thomas West, Midtown, and Rutherford).

Because Tennessee ACA Marketplace plans and Medicare Advantage networks frequently partner with specific hospital alliances, verifying your exact sub-network letter or carrier tier is essential before Open Enrollment closes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vanderbilt University Medical Center in-network for 2027 Tennessee ACA Marketplace plans?

Vanderbilt Health participates in select Middle Tennessee ACA Marketplace networks, but narrow-network exchange plans often divide coverage between Vanderbilt/Ascension Saint Thomas and HCA TriStar Health facilities.

How do BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee Network S and Network P differ for Nashville hospitals?

BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee uses distinct network letters (such as Network S, Network P, and Network L) that determine whether Vanderbilt Health, Ascension Saint Thomas, or TriStar Health hospitals are in-network in Davidson, Williamson, and Rutherford counties.

Are emergency visits at Vanderbilt or TriStar Skyline covered regardless of network status?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency room in Tennessee must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels.
