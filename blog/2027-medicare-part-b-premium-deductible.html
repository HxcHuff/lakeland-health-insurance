What Are the 2027 Medicare Part B Premium and Deductible?

Every fall, CMS publishes official Medicare Parts A and B premiums, deductibles, coinsurance, and IRMAA brackets for the coming calendar year. Below is the complete verified 2027 CMS cost sheet, how it affects Original Medicare, Medigap Plan G/N, and Medicare Advantage budgets, and what to check before December 7.

Direct answer: For 2027, CMS set the standard Medicare Part B monthly premium at $209.40 (up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026) and the Part B annual deductible at $292 (up $9 from $283 in 2026). Higher-income beneficiaries with 2025 MAGI above $112,000 (single) or $224,000 (married filing jointly) pay IRMAA surcharges ranging from $83.70 to $502.40 per month on top of $209.40.

How 2027 Part B Costs Interact With Medigap, Advantage & Part D

  1. Medigap Plan G & Plan N: Both plans cover the 2027 Part A $1,776 deductible, $444/$888 daily hospital coinsurance, $222/day SNF coinsurance, and 20% Part B coinsurance. You pay the $292 Part B annual deductible once per calendar year (plus small office/ER copays on Plan N).
  2. Medicare Advantage (Part C): You still must pay your monthly Part B premium ($209.40 standard, unless reduced by a verified Part B Giveback plan in your ZIP code) to stay enrolled in any Advantage plan.
  3. 2027 Part D Prescription Drug Rules: Standalone and MAPD prescription plans cap covered annual out-of-pocket drug spending at $2,400 with a maximum defined-standard deductible of $700 in 2027.
  4. Medicare Savings Programs (QMB / SLMB / QI): Lower-income Medicare beneficiaries may qualify for state programs that pay the $209.40 monthly Part B premium (and QMB also covers the $292 deductible and coinsurance).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard Medicare Part B monthly premium for 2027?

CMS set the standard 2027 Medicare Part B monthly premium at $209.40 per month, up $6.50 from $202.90 in 2026.

What is the 2027 Medicare Part B annual deductible?

The 2027 Medicare Part B annual deductible is $292 for all Part B beneficiaries, up $9 from $283 in 2026.

What income triggers Medicare IRMAA in 2027?

2027 IRMAA surcharges start when your 2025 Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) exceeds $112,000 for individual filers or $224,000 for married couples filing jointly.

Does Medigap Plan G pay the 2027 Part B deductible?

No. Under federal law, Medigap Plan G and Plan N do not pay the $292 Part B annual deductible, though they cover the remaining 20% Part B coinsurance and the $1,776 Part A hospital deductible.
