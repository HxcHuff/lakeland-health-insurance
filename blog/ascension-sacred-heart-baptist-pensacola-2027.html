Northwest Florida's Three Major Hospital Systems in 2027

Across Escambia, Santa Rosa, Okaloosa, and Walton counties, hospital and specialty care is anchored by three distinct systems: Ascension Sacred Heart (including Ascension Sacred Heart Pensacola on Bayou Boulevard, the Studer Family Children's Hospital, and Ascension Sacred Heart Emerald Coast in Miramar Beach), Baptist Health Care (centered at the Brent Lane Baptist Hospital campus in Pensacola, Gulf Breeze Hospital, and the Andrews Institute), and HCA Florida Healthcare (HCA Florida West Hospital in Pensacola, HCA Florida Fort Walton-Destin Hospital, and Santa Rosa Medical Center in Milton).

Because Pensacola sits on the Alabama state line and spans multiple Florida Panhandle counties, choosing an ACA Marketplace or Medicare plan for 2027 requires checking county-specific HMO boundaries as well as physician group tax IDs.

3 Steps for Escambia, Santa Rosa & Okaloosa Residents

  1. Verify Pediatric & Maternal Access at Studer Family Children's Hospital: For young families in Pensacola, Pace, Milton, and Navarre, Ascension Sacred Heart operates the region's primary children's hospital and Level III NICU. Confirm your 2027 ACA plan includes both the hospital and Sacred Heart pediatric specialists.
  2. Check Orthopedic & Outpatient Surgery Tax IDs at Gulf Breeze: If you see orthopedic specialists at the Andrews Institute in Gulf Breeze, verify both the physician group and the ambulatory surgery center before selecting a narrow-network plan.
  3. Avoid State-Line Out-of-Network Surprises: Florida ACA Marketplace HMOs do not cover non-emergency routine care across the border in Mobile or Baldwin County, Alabama (such as USA Health or South Baldwin Regional). If you travel between Pensacola and Alabama, review our Alabama 2027 Hospital Network Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover Ascension Sacred Heart Pensacola and Baptist Hospital?

Select Florida Blue plans (including BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific Ambetter and UnitedHealthcare sub-networks in Escambia and Santa Rosa counties cover Ascension Sacred Heart and Baptist Health Care, though narrow HMO tiers often favor one system.

Are Ascension Sacred Heart Emerald Coast in Miramar Beach and Pensacola in the same network?

Ascension Sacred Heart negotiates regional contracts across Pensacola, Gulf Breeze, Miramar Beach (Walton County), and Panama City (Bay County), but HealthCare.gov plans are county-specific, so verify cross-county referral rules along the Emerald Coast.

Is the Andrews Institute in Gulf Breeze covered under Baptist Health Care network contracts?

Baptist Hospital Gulf Breeze and the Andrews Institute for Orthopaedics & Sports Medicine affiliate with Baptist Health Care, but orthopedic surgeons and ambulatory surgery centers may bill under distinct tax IDs that should be verified individually.
