What we know

The calendar is fixed; the paperwork is personal. These anchors apply across Medicare and Marketplace coverage in Polk County:

  • Medicare Annual Enrollment (AEP) runs October 15 through December 7, 2026 for coverage effective January 1, 2027, per Medicare.gov.
  • Marketplace Open Enrollment in Florida runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027, per HealthCare.gov.
  • Benefits and costs reset every plan year. Premiums, deductibles, pharmacy networks, and drug tiers can change even when the plan name on your card looks the same.
  • Medicare Advantage and Part D members should have received an Annual Notice of Change by September 30 describing 2027 changes. Treat it as your starting point, not the finish line.

When 2027 details appear

Core 2027 plan details and Annual Notices of Change are already in circulation—Medicare Annual Enrollment opened October 15. Marketplace summaries of benefits and formularies follow as November 1 approaches.

Provider directories are the piece that keeps moving. Carriers may refresh them throughout the fall, so a lookup today is a snapshot, not a guarantee on January 1. I verify each doctor in the plan’s official directory for your ZIP and plan ID before enrollment, and I recommend a second check as you get close to your effective date if coverage starts January 1, 2027.

When you work with me, we walk through your list together against Evidence of Coverage and directory entries so you can compare options with numbers on the page—not memory from last year.

How to protect your access to your doctors

Work through this list in order. It is the same path I use when a Lakeland client asks me to pressure-test a plan before sign-up:

  1. Write down every doctor and site of care—primary care, specialists, hospitals, urgent care, and labs—with the street address you actually use.
  2. Pull your 2027 Annual Notice of Change and Evidence of Coverage for Medicare plans, or the plan’s 2027 brochure and formulary for Marketplace coverage. Highlight network, pharmacy, or drug-tier changes.
  3. Look up each name in the carrier’s 2027 provider directory for your plan ID and ZIP. A generic search engine or old ID card is not enough.
  4. Match every prescription to the 2027 formulary, including strength, brand versus generic, and any step-therapy rules.
  5. Leave margin before the deadline. If something falls out of network, you need time to compare another plan—not a panicked December switch.
