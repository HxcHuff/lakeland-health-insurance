What we know

These facts apply whether you have Medicare, an ACA Marketplace plan, or employer coverage that ends soon:

  • Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs October 15 through December 7, 2026 for coverage that starts January 1, 2027, per Medicare.gov.
  • Marketplace Open Enrollment in Florida runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027, per HealthCare.gov.
  • Plans change every year. Carrier contracts, provider networks, pharmacy networks, and drug formularies are updated for each plan year. Last year’s in-network list is not proof for 2027.
  • Your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) for Medicare Advantage or Part D should arrive by September 30 before the plan year it describes. Read it before you assume your current plan still fits.

What isn’t public yet

Carriers finalize and publish 2027 plan details—including benefits, formularies, provider directories, and pharmacy networks—on their own fall schedule. Until those documents are posted for your plan ID and ZIP code, you cannot complete a full doctor-and-prescription verification.

Once 2027 materials are available, I review them with each client individually: we compare official directories and Evidence of Coverage to your doctor and medication list before any enrollment decision. Building your list now keeps you ahead of the December deadline.

How to protect your access to your doctors

Use this checklist before you lock in 2027 coverage. I walk clients through the same steps in a plan review:

  1. List every doctor and care location you rely on, including primary care, specialists, hospitals, and stand-alone labs. Note the address you actually visit.
  2. Read your 2027 Annual Notice of Change and Evidence of Coverage if you have Medicare Advantage or Part D. Flag any mention of network, pharmacy, or formulary changes.
  3. Confirm in-network status for 2027 using the plan’s official provider directory for your plan ID and ZIP code—not a third-party search alone.
  4. Check the 2027 formulary for each prescription, including dosage and brand versus generic rules.
  5. Do not wait until December. Directories update on rolling schedules during fall enrollment. Starting early leaves time to compare alternatives if something no longer fits.
