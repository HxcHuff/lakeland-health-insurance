Why Lakeland patients start here

Medicare plan shopping in Polk County is personal. You want to know whether your Watson Clinic specialist, your Orlando Health doctors, and your pharmacy still line up with the plan you are considering for January.

I built my practice around those conversations. When you share your doctor list and medications, I pull the official 2027 provider directories and formularies for the plans you are weighing. We compare totals—premium, drug costs, and access—not just a friendly name on a brochure.

Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7, 2026, per Medicare.gov. If you received an Annual Notice of Change, bring it to the review.

Doctor-access checklist for 2027

Before you enroll—or before you decide to keep your current plan—work through this list:

  1. List every doctor and location you use, including Watson Clinic and Orlando Health offices, plus hospitals, labs, and urgent care.
  2. Read your 2027 Annual Notice of Change and Evidence of Coverage if you have Medicare Advantage or Part D.
  3. Confirm each provider in the plan’s official 2027 directory for your plan ID and ZIP code.
  4. Match prescriptions to the 2027 formulary, including dosage and tier.
  5. Recheck before January 1 if directories update late in the fall.

What happens when you reach out

Submit the form at the top of this page or text me. I return calls personally, usually the same business day. We schedule a focused review of your doctors, drugs, and plan options. There is no obligation to enroll.

You leave with a clearer picture of which plans fit your list—or what to fix before the December 7 Medicare deadline.
