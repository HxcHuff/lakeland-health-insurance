Navigating Morgantown, Charleston, Huntington & Panhandle Networks in 2027

Across West Virginia, hospital and specialist access has consolidated around three dominant academic and regional systems: WVU Medicine (anchored by J.W. Ruby Memorial Hospital and WVU Medicine Children's in Morgantown, alongside United Hospital Center in Bridgeport, Camden Clark in Parkersburg, Wheeling Hospital, Weirton Medical Center, Thomas Hospitals in South Charleston/Charleston, Princeton Community Hospital, and Berkeley/Jefferson Medical Centers in the Eastern Panhandle), Vandalia Health (combining Charleston Area Medical Center [CAMC] — General, Memorial, Women and Children's, Teays Valley, Greenbrier Valley, and Plateau — with Mon Health System in Morgantown/Preston/Weston and Davis Health System in Elkins), and Marshall Health Network (integrating Mountain Health Network — Cabell Huntington Hospital, St. Mary's Medical Center, Hoops Family Children's, and Rivers Health — with Marshall Health and HIMG).

When comparing 2027 ACA Marketplace plans on HealthCare.gov (Highmark Blue Cross Blue Shield West Virginia and CareSource) or evaluating 2027 Medicare Advantage options (including provider-sponsored Peak Health, Highmark, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, and Aetna), verifying both newly integrated hospital names and border-state referral rules is essential.

3 Steps for West Virginia Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Verify Newly Consolidated Hospital & Clinic Names by NPI: Search by physical street address and 10-digit NPI for recently integrated facilities such as WVU Medicine Thomas Hospitals in South Charleston, Vandalia Health Mon Health in Morgantown, and Marshall Health Network / HIMG in Huntington.
  2. Compare Peak Health vs. Highmark, Humana & UHC on Medicare Advantage: Confirm whether your 2027 Medicare plan covers all three major West Virginia systems as well as any out-of-state specialty center you visit.
  3. Check Eastern & Northern Panhandle Border-State Rules: If you live in Martinsburg/Charles Town and use Valley Health Winchester Medical Center in Virginia or Meritus / Johns Hopkins in Maryland — or live in Wheeling/Morgantown and travel to Pittsburgh — verify out-of-state network coverage.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at WVU Medicine J.W. Ruby Memorial, CAMC General/Memorial, Mon Health Medical Center, Cabell Huntington, St. Mary's, Camden Clark, and Wheeling Hospital ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are WVU Medicine, Vandalia Health (CAMC and Mon Health), and Marshall Health Network in the same 2027 West Virginia networks?

Highmark Blue Cross Blue Shield West Virginia and CareSource West Virginia offer distinct ACA Marketplace plans on HealthCare.gov, while Medicare Advantage carriers — including Peak Health, Highmark, Humana, UnitedHealthcare, and Aetna — structure specific HMO and PPO contracts across WVU Medicine, Vandalia Health, and Marshall Health Network.

How do Morgantown and Charleston hospital networks compare between WVU Medicine and Vandalia Health?

In Morgantown, WVU Medicine (J.W. Ruby Memorial Hospital) and Vandalia Health's Mon Health Medical Center operate side by side, while in the Kanawha Valley, Vandalia Health's Charleston Area Medical Center (CAMC) competes alongside WVU Medicine Thomas Hospitals.

What changed with Mountain Health Network and Marshall Health in Huntington?

Mountain Health Network (Cabell Huntington Hospital, St. Mary's Medical Center, Hoops Family Children's Hospital, and Rivers Health) united with Marshall Health to form Marshall Health Network, so enrollees should verify both hospital facility and Marshall Health faculty NPIs.
