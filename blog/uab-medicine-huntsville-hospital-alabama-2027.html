Alabama's Major Hospital Networks in 2027

Across Central, North, and Coastal Alabama, hospital access is anchored by UAB Medicine in Birmingham (including UAB Hospital, the O'Neal Comprehensive Cancer Center, Callahan Eye Hospital, and the recently integrated UAB St. Vincent's hospitals in Birmingham, Blount, Chilton, East, and St. Clair), Huntsville Hospital Health System across the Tennessee Valley (Huntsville Hospital, Madison Hospital, Decatur Morgan, and Helen Keller), Grandview Medical Center and Brookwood Baptist Health in Jefferson/Shelby counties, and USA Health & Infirmary Health along the Mobile and Baldwin County Gulf Coast.

On the Alabama HealthCare.gov Marketplace and in regional Medicare Advantage plans, network participation varies significantly between Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama, UnitedHealthcare, and Ambetter of Alabama.

3 Steps for Alabama Enrollees Before December 15

  1. Verify Both UAB Hospital and UAHSF Physician Billing: Specialists at The Kirklin Clinic of UAB Hospital bill through the University of Alabama Health Services Foundation (UAHSF). Confirm both the hospital and faculty group on your 2027 plan ID.
  2. Check UAB St. Vincent's Contract Alignment: Following UAB's acquisition of the former Ascension St. Vincent's hospitals in Central Alabama, verify how your 2027 carrier directory lists St. Vincent's Birmingham, East, and St. Clair campuses.
  3. Review Alabama Subsidy & Enrollment Rules: Visit our Alabama Health Insurance Guide for county-level HealthCare.gov deadlines and household income rules.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at UAB Hospital, Huntsville Hospital, Grandview Medical Center, and USA Health ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of plan network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 Alabama ACA Marketplace plans cover UAB Hospital and UAB St. Vincent's in Birmingham?

Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Alabama Marketplace plans and select UnitedHealthcare and Ambetter of Alabama sub-networks in Jefferson and Shelby counties cover UAB Hospital and UAB St. Vincent's campuses, though narrow HMO/EPO tiers must be verified by exact provider NPI.

Are Huntsville Hospital, Madison Hospital, and Decatur Morgan Hospital in the same network?

Yes. Huntsville Hospital Health System operates Huntsville Hospital, Madison Hospital, Decatur Morgan Hospital, Helen Keller Hospital, and Marshall Medical Centers across North Alabama and negotiates regional payer contracts.

How can Alabama Medicare beneficiaries keep access to both UAB O'Neal Cancer Center and local hospitals?

Original Medicare paired with a Medigap (Medicare Supplement) policy is accepted at UAB Hospital, O'Neal Comprehensive Cancer Center, Huntsville Hospital, Grandview Medical Center, and USA Health without prior authorization referrals for Medicare-covered services.
