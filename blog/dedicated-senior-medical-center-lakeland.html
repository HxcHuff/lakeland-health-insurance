Short answer: the center works through Medicare Advantage

Ask the center first. Its FAQ ties choosing it as a PCP to a Medicare Advantage plan it takes; Original Medicare works with any provider that takes Medicare.

The center's FAQ: "You will need to have a Medicare Advantage plan that we take in order to choose us as your Primary Care Physician."

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 (plan must get the request by December 7). The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period runs January 1 to March 31 for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan.
