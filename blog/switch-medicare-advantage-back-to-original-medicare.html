Can I switch from Medicare Advantage back to Original Medicare?

Leaving Medicare Advantage for Original Medicare is allowed only in certain enrollment periods, unless a Special Enrollment Period applies. Dropping Advantage does not automatically issue a Medigap policy. Drug coverage must be arranged again if the Advantage plan included it.

By David Huff, licensed Florida health agent #W371813 | NPN 18213932

Last reviewed: September 30, 2026.

Direct answer: Yes, during windows Medicare allows. Annual Enrollment (October 15 through December 7) can be used to switch from Advantage to Original Medicare, generally effective January 1. If you are already in Advantage, Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment (January 1 through March 31) allows one change to Original Medicare, generally effective the first of the next month. Medigap follows separate rules and is not created by those Advantage windows.

What happens to my prescriptions?

If the Advantage plan included Part D, returning to Original Medicare means you need a stand-alone drug plan unless you have other creditable coverage. During Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment, Medicare.gov describes an opportunity to join a separate drug plan when you return to Original Medicare. Check every drug on the new formulary before you switch. See checking prescriptions.

Will I automatically get Medigap?

Outside your 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period or a guaranteed-issue right such as the trial right, a Medigap insurer can review your health and decline you. Get a Medigap approval before you leave Medicare Advantage.

No. You apply for Medigap separately. Outside the federal Medigap Open Enrollment Period or another issue right, a company may ask health questions. Trial rights exist in limited situations described by Medicare.gov; they are not the same as AEP. Florida also has state Medigap rules that can apply. Medigap is not selected as an AEP Advantage plan change. See Advantage versus Supplement.

What should I verify before I leave Advantage?

Doctors who participate with Original Medicare, hospital acceptance, the Part D plan you would join, and whether any extra Advantage benefits you use would end. Extra benefits vary by plan and are not Original Medicare. Use the AEP checklist if you are in the fall window.

What is a trial right, in brief?

Medicare.gov describes limited situations in which a person who tries Medicare Advantage can later buy a Medigap policy with stronger issue rights than a standard underwritten application. Those rights are specific, time-limited, and not a general AEP feature. Read the current Medicare.gov Medigap pages rather than assuming you have a trial right.

If you do not have a trial right or federal Medigap Open Enrollment, a Medigap application can be declined or priced with health questions. That is why the return to Original Medicare should include a Medigap conversation before you drop Advantage, not after.

Keep the Advantage disenrollment confirmation. You need it if a claim is billed to the wrong plan during the change month.

Common questions

Can I leave Medicare Advantage in July for Original Medicare?

Only if a Special Enrollment Period applies to your event. The regular windows are Annual Enrollment and, if you are already in Advantage, Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment.

Does MA-OEP automatically issue a Medigap policy?

No. Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment is not a federal Medigap Open Enrollment Period. Medigap availability depends on separate rules.
