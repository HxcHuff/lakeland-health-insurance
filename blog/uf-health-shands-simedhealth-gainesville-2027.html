Navigating Gainesville & North Central Florida Networks in 2027

Across Alachua, Marion, Columbia, Levy, and Gilchrist counties, healthcare access revolves around UF Health Shands Hospital in Gainesville (including UF Health Shands Children's Hospital, UF Health Shands Cancer Hospital, and the Heart & Vascular and Neuromedicine hospitals), HCA Florida North Florida Hospital (near Oaks Mall on Newberry Road), and independent multi-specialty giant SIMEDHealth.

Further south in Marion, Sumter, and Lake counties, residents also navigate between AdventHealth Ocala, HCA Florida Ocala Hospital, and UF Health Central Florida (UF Health Spanish Plaines Hospital in The Villages and UF Health Leesburg Hospital). Choosing the right 2027 ACA Marketplace or Medicare plan requires verifying both hospital facility and academic physician group contracts.

3 Steps for Alachua & Marion County Enrollees Before December 15

  1. Verify Both Shands Facility and UF College of Medicine Faculty: Academic specialists at UF Health Shands bill through Florida Clinical Practice Association (UF Health Physicians). Ensure both the hospital and the faculty practice accept your 2027 plan tier.
  2. Check SIMEDHealth vs. Hospital Admission Privilege: Many SIMEDHealth physicians refer inpatient procedures to HCA Florida North Florida Hospital or UF Health Shands. Confirm your plan covers both your SIMEDHealth clinic and the hospital where your surgeon operates.
  3. Confirm Cross-County Referral Rules: If you live in Ocala, Chiefland, or Lake City (Columbia County) and travel to Gainesville for tertiary specialty care at UF Health Shands, check whether your county's HMO requires a primary care referral or out-of-area authorization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover UF Health Shands Hospital in Gainesville?

Select Florida Blue plans (such as BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific Ambetter and Oscar Health network tiers in Alachua and Marion counties cover UF Health Shands Hospital, though UF College of Medicine faculty physicians must be checked separately.

Does SIMEDHealth accept the same 2027 ACA and Medicare plans as UF Health Shands?

Not always. SIMEDHealth is an independent multi-specialty physician group with clinics in Gainesville, Ocala, Chiefland, Lady Lake, and Lake City, so its Medicare Advantage and ACA Marketplace contracts are negotiated separately from UF Health Shands.

Are UF Health Central Florida hospitals in Leesburg and The Villages in the same network as Gainesville?

UF Health Leesburg Hospital and UF Health Spanish Plaines Hospital in The Villages are part of the UF Health system, but ACA Marketplace plans are sold by county rating area, so Alachua, Marion, Sumter, and Lake county plan networks must be verified individually.
