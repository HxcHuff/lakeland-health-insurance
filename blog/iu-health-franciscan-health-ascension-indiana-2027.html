Navigating Central, Northwest & Northeast Indiana Networks in 2027

Across Marion, Hamilton, Hendricks, and Johnson counties in Central Indiana — as well as Lake/Porter counties in Northwest Indiana and Allen County in Fort Wayne — hospital and specialist care is divided among five major health systems: Indiana University Health (IU Health) (including the downtown Indianapolis Academic Health Center, Riley Hospital for Children, IU Health North in Carmel, West in Avon, Fishers, Bloomington, Arnett in Lafayette, and Ball Memorial in Muncie), Franciscan Health (Indianapolis Southside, Mooresville, Carmel, Lafayette East, Crown Point, and Michigan City), Ascension St. Vincent (86th Street Indianapolis, Peyton Manning Children's Hospital, Carmel, Fishers, Anderson, and Evansville), Community Health Network (Community Hospital North, East, South, Howard, and Anderson), and Parkview Health in Fort Wayne.

On the Indiana ACA Marketplace, carriers frequently structure narrow-network HMO plans around only one or two of these systems, making network verification critical before Open Enrollment ends.

3 Steps for Indiana Families Before December 15

  1. Check Hamilton & Marion County System Splits: In Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, and Indianapolis, an ACA HMO that covers Community Health Network or Ascension St. Vincent may exclude IU Health North or Franciscan Health Indianapolis.
  2. Verify Pediatric Access at Riley Hospital for Children or Peyton Manning Children's: Families with children needing specialty care should confirm whether their 2027 plan covers Riley Hospital for Children (IU Health) or Peyton Manning Children's Hospital (Ascension St. Vincent).
  3. Confirm Northwest Indiana vs. Chicago Referral Rules: Residents in Lake and Porter counties (Crown Point, Schererville, Valparaiso) using Franciscan Health, Powers Health (Community Healthcare System), or Northwest Health should note that Indiana ACA HMOs generally do not cover non-emergency care across the Illinois state line in Chicago.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at IU Health, Franciscan Health, Ascension St. Vincent, Community Health Network, and Parkview Health ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency procedures and scheduled outpatient visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Indiana ACA Marketplace plans cover IU Health, Franciscan Health, and Ascension St. Vincent together?

Most Indiana HealthCare.gov HMO plans (including Anthem Pathway, Ambetter / MHS, CareSource, and IU Health Plans) build narrow networks around specific hospital systems, so enrollees in Indianapolis, Carmel, and Fishers must check which systems participate in their exact sub-network.

Are Riley Hospital for Children and IU Health North in Carmel part of the IU Health network?

Yes. Riley Hospital for Children at IU Health, IU Health Methodist and University Hospitals, IU Health North (Carmel), IU Health West (Avon), IU Health Fishers, Bloomington, Arnett (Lafayette), and Ball Memorial (Muncie) are all part of Indiana University Health.

How can Indiana Medicare beneficiaries keep access to all five Indianapolis hospital systems?

Original Medicare paired with a Medigap (Medicare Supplement) policy is accepted across IU Health, Franciscan Health, Ascension St. Vincent, Community Health Network, Eskenazi Health, and Parkview Health without network restrictions for Medicare-approved care.
