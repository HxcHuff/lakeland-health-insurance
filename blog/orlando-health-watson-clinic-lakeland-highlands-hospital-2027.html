Why the Shared Name on Lakeland Highlands Road Confuses 2027 Shoppers

When Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital opened its 7-story, 302-bed hospital campus at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road (ZIP 33813), South Lakeland, Bartow, and Mulberry residents gained a major inpatient hospital, emergency room, surgical center, and obstetrics unit right off the Polk Parkway (SR 570).

However, the shared name on the building creates a critical billing distinction for 2027 Open Enrollment: Orlando Health owns and bills for the hospital facility, while Watson Clinic LLP remains an independent multi-specialty physician group with its own tax ID and carrier contracts. To avoid surprise out-of-network denials on scheduled surgeries or specialist follow-ups, your 2027 plan must include both Orlando Health’s facility contract and your Watson Clinic doctor’s 10-digit NPI.

Frequently Asked Questions

If my insurance covers Watson Clinic, does it automatically cover Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital?

Not automatically. Orlando Health bills for the hospital facility at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road under Orlando Health’s contract, while Watson Clinic physicians bill under Watson Clinic LLP’s tax ID. You should confirm that both Orlando Health and Watson Clinic are Tier 1 in-network on your exact 2027 plan ID.

Where is Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital located?

The hospital is located at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road, Lakeland, FL 33813, just south of the Polk Parkway (Exit 10) in South Lakeland.

Are emergency room visits at the new Lakeland Highlands Hospital covered by all insurance plans?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency evaluation and stabilization at the Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital emergency department must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of whether your plan holds a routine contract with Orlando Health.

Which 2027 plans cover both Lakeland Regional Health and Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands Hospital?

Original Medicare paired with a Medigap supplement (Plan G or Plan N) covers any hospital or physician accepting Medicare assignment, including both Lakeland Regional Health and Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands Hospital. On 2027 Medicare Advantage and ACA Marketplace plans, coverage depends on the specific carrier sub-network in Polk County (Rating Area 53).
