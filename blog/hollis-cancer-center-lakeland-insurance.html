Network, prior authorization, drugs, and your out-of-pocket max

Medicare covers chemotherapy and radiation under Part A when you're a hospital inpatient and under Part B when you're an outpatient or in a clinic. Medicare Advantage plans cover the same services but can use networks and prior authorization.

Original Medicare lets you use any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare, anywhere in the U.S.; Medicare Advantage plans may require network providers for non-emergency care.

Medigap (Medicare Supplement Insurance) is extra insurance from a private company that helps pay your share of costs in Original Medicare.

Medicare: chemo and radiation under Part A/B, MA vs Medigap
