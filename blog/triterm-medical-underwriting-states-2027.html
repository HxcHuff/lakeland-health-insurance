TriTerm Medical Underwriting, Health Questions & State Availability (2027)

Before you spend time quoting a 3-year TriTerm Medical or Short-Term Medical plan, know what the application actually asks, how the 12-month pre-existing waiting period works, and which states allow multi-term coverage.

Direct answer: Unlike ACA Marketplace plans, TriTerm Medical (TTM) and Short-Term Medical (STM) are medically underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company (a UnitedHealthcare company). Applications typically review 12-month tobacco/nicotine use, other active coverage, and a 5-year medical lookback for serious diagnoses or surgeries. TriTerm Medical excludes pre-existing conditions for the first 12 months (with potential coverage after month 12 per contract), whereas standard Short-Term Medical excludes pre-existing conditions for the entire term.

State Availability & Term Rules (Check Your ZIP First)

Non-ACA medical rules are regulated at both the federal and state levels, so plan availability and maximum duration change by state:

  • Short-Term Medical (Golden Rule Brochure G202605): Published availability covers states including FL, IN, MI, MS, NE, PA, SC, TN, TX, WI, and WV (term duration varies by state, generally up to 12 months minus 1 day where state law allows).
  • TriTerm Medical (3 consecutive terms: 364 + 365 + 365 days): Available only in states that permit multi-term association group filings through FACT (Federation of American Consumers and Travelers). Because state filings can update between plan years, always verify live ZIP-level availability before building a 2027 budget around TriTerm.
  • If TriTerm is not available or you do not pass underwriting: Compare guaranteed-issue 2027 ACA Marketplace plans (zero health questions, Day-1 pre-existing coverage) and review guaranteed-issue supplemental options such as Hospital SafeGuard GI (no medical underwriting, ages 18–64) to help buffer hospital out-of-pocket costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you be declined for TriTerm Medical or Short-Term Medical?

Yes. Both TriTerm Medical and Short-Term Medical are medically underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company. Applicants must answer state-specific health and tobacco questions, and certain recent diagnoses, surgeries, or medications can result in a decline.

How does the 12-month pre-existing condition rule work on TriTerm Medical?

Under Golden Rule's TriTerm Medical brochure (Form G202510), no benefits are paid during the first 12 months for a health condition that existed prior to the policy effective date. After 12 months on the plan, expenses related to pre-existing conditions may be covered subject to policy terms, exclusions, and state rules.

Why does TriTerm Medical require FACT association membership?

Golden Rule's TriTerm Medical and Short-Term Medical plans are issued as association group plans available only to members of FACT (the Federation of American Consumers and Travelers). Membership is typically handled as part of enrollment where available.

What are my options if I do not pass TriTerm Medical underwriting?

If you do not qualify for medically underwritten TriTerm or Short-Term coverage, guaranteed-issue ACA Marketplace plans cover pre-existing conditions from Day 1 during Open Enrollment or a Special Enrollment Period. For supplemental hospital cash without medical underwriting, guaranteed-issue Hospital SafeGuard GI (ages 18-64) can also be reviewed beside primary coverage.
