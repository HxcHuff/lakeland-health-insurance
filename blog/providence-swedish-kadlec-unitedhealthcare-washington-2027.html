Providence, Swedish & Kadlec Leaving UnitedHealthcare (2027)

What the January 1, 2027 contract split between Providence, Swedish, PacMed, Kadlec, and UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage means for patients in Seattle, Everett, Olympia, Spokane, and the Tri-Cities—and which plans still keep your doctors in network.

What Changes on January 1, 2027 Between Providence, Swedish & UnitedHealthcare

Both UnitedHealthcare and Providence (including Providence Swedish, PacMed Clinics, and Kadlec) have published official patient notices confirming that effective January 1, 2027, Providence hospitals, clinics, and employed physicians across Washington will no longer be in network for most UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans.

Current 2026 coverage continues without interruption through December 31, 2026. Beginning January 1, 2027, the network termination applies to:

  • UnitedHealthcare Individual Medicare Advantage Plans: Including both MA HMO and MA PPO individual plans (including AARP-branded UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans).
  • UnitedHealthcare Group Retiree Medicare Advantage Plans: Employer/retiree group MA policies administered by UnitedHealthcare.
  • UnitedHealthcare Chronic Special Needs Plans (C-SNP): Confirmed out-of-network on both UnitedHealthcare's October 2026 PNW notice and Providence's November 2026 statewide Washington update.

Which plans are NOT impacted? UnitedHealthcare and Providence confirm that Washington Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP) (subject to ongoing renewal finalization), employer-sponsored commercial group plans, and Original Medicare (Parts A & B)—with or without any Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan—remain accepted across Providence, Swedish, PacMed, and Kadlec locations.

4 Action Steps Before 2027 Enrollment Windows Close

  1. Verify Alternative Contracted 2027 Medicare Advantage Carriers by County: Contracted carrier lineups differ sharply between King/Snohomish/Thurston counties, Spokane/Stevens counties, the Tri-Cities, and Clark County (where Providence also partnered with Devoted Health for 2027). Always verify each doctor's 10-digit NPI against the exact 2027 plan ID on Medicare.gov Plan Finder.
  2. Don't Confuse Providence Health System With Providence Health Plan: While Providence hospitals and clinics remain open and contract with multiple insurers, Providence Health Plan is exiting the insurance market for 2027—so nobody can switch into a Providence-issued insurance policy for 2027.
  3. Compare Original Medicare + Medigap Rules in Washington: Washington state carries unique year-round Medigap switching rules for applicants already enrolled in a Medicare Supplement plan, while moving from Medicare Advantage to Medigap typically requires health underwriting unless a federal or state Guaranteed Issue trigger applies (such as your entire MA plan leaving your county).
  4. Ask About Continuity of Care for Active Treatment: If you are mid-treatment for oncology, pregnancy, recent surgery, or a serious chronic condition, call the member number on the back of your UnitedHealthcare card to request a formal Continuity of Care review before January 1, 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which UnitedHealthcare plans lose in-network access to Providence, Swedish, PacMed, and Kadlec on January 1, 2027?

Effective January 1, 2027, Providence hospitals and medical groups across Washington, including Swedish campuses, PacMed Clinics, Providence Regional Medical Center Everett, Providence St. Peter, Providence Sacred Heart and Holy Family in Spokane, and Kadlec in the Tri-Cities, will no longer be in network with UnitedHealthcare Individual Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans, Group Retiree Medicare Advantage plans, and Chronic Special Needs Plans (C-SNP).

Are UnitedHealthcare D-SNP, employer group, or Medigap plans affected at Providence and Swedish in 2027?

No. Both UnitedHealthcare and Providence confirm that Washington Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP), employer-sponsored commercial plans, and Original Medicare (with or without any Medicare Supplement / Medigap policy and standalone Part D plan) are not impacted by this January 1, 2027 Medicare Advantage change.

Does MultiCare also restrict Medicare Advantage PPO plans in Puget Sound?

Yes. MultiCare Health System exited Medicare Advantage PPO plans in the Puget Sound region (including Regence Medicare Advantage PPO) starting January 1, 2026, making it essential for King, Pierce, and Snohomish County beneficiaries to verify both hospital facility and clinic NPIs by exact 2027 plan ID.

Are emergency room visits at Swedish, Providence, and Kadlec hospitals still covered?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Swedish, Providence, and Kadlec hospitals are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of network contract status.
