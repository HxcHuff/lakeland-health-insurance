Regional Hospital Dominance Across the Virginia Insurance Marketplace

Virginia operates its own state-based exchange—the Virginia Insurance Marketplace—where network availability varies sharply between the Commonwealth's three largest population corridors:

  • Hampton Roads / Coastal Virginia (Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Newport News): Anchored by Sentara Health (Sentara Norfolk General Hospital, Sentara Virginia Beach General, Sentara CarePlex) and Riverside Health System.
  • Northern Virginia (Fairfax, Loudoun, Prince William, Arlington, Alexandria): Anchored by Inova Health System (Inova Fairfax Medical Campus, Inova Schar Cancer Institute, Inova Loudoun) and Virginia Hospital Center (VHC Health).
  • Central Virginia / Richmond Metro (Henrico, Chesterfield, Richmond City): Anchored by VCU Health, Bon Secours, and HCA Virginia (Chippenham, Johnston-Willis, Henrico Doctors').

Action Steps for Virginia Open Enrollment

  1. Distinguish HMO Referral Rules: Many Virginia Marketplace plans require a designated primary care physician (PCP) referral before seeing specialists at Inova or Sentara.
  2. Cross-Border D.C. & Maryland Care: Northern Virginia residents who see specialists in Washington, D.C., or Suburban Maryland should verify whether their Virginia exchange plan covers out-of-state non-emergency care.
  3. Explore Virginia Plan Options: Visit our Virginia Health Insurance Guide for full eligibility and plan comparison details.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 Virginia Insurance Marketplace plans cover Sentara Health hospitals in Hampton Roads?

Sentara Health Plans (formerly Optima Health) offers integrated in-network access across Sentara Norfolk General, Sentara Virginia Beach General, and Sentara Leigh, alongside select Anthem HealthKeepers and UnitedHealthcare exchange tiers.

How do Northern Virginia residents verify Inova Health System participation for 2027?

In Fairfax, Loudoun, Prince William, and Arlington counties, enrollees should check whether their Virginia Insurance Marketplace plan contracts with Inova Fairfax, Inova Loudoun, and Inova Medical Group physicians.

Are emergency room visits at Sentara, Inova, and VCU Health protected by federal law?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency department in Virginia must be covered at in-network cost-sharing rates.
