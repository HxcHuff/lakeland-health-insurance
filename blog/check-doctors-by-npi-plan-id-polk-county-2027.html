Type 1 Clinician NPI vs. Type 2 Facility NPI: Avoiding Split-Billing Traps

Every HIPAA-covered health care provider in the United States is assigned a unique 10-digit National Provider Identifier (NPI) in the federal CMS NPPES registry. Checking only a doctor's last name misses how claims are actually adjudicated:

Identifier Type What It Represents in Polk County Why You Must Check It Against Your 2027 Plan ID
Type 1 NPI (Individual Clinician) A specific physician (MD/DO), physician assistant (PA), or nurse practitioner (APRN) at Watson Clinic, LRH Physician Group, Bond Clinic, Gessler Clinic, or BayCare Medical Group. Confirms the individual practitioner is credentialed and participating at the exact street address where you schedule appointments—not merely listed at a hospital where they hold courtesy privileges.
Type 2 NPI (Organization / Facility) The billing medical group, outpatient imaging or surgery center, cancer center (such as Hollis Cancer Center), or acute-care hospital campus (LRH, Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands, Winter Haven Hospital, Bartow Regional, or Heart of Florida). Prevents split-billing surprises—where your surgeon's Type 1 NPI is in network, but the outpatient facility, pathology lab, or hospital campus Type 2 NPI bills out of network on an HMO or EPO plan.
Outpatient Surgery & Diagnostic Imaging Rule: Before scheduling an endoscopy, MRI, infusion, or outpatient procedure in 2027, ask the scheduling coordinator for both the rendering physician's Type 1 NPI and the facility's Type 2 NPI, then confirm both on your exact 2027 Plan ID.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a Type 1 NPI and a Type 2 NPI when checking health insurance?

A Type 1 NPI identifies an individual clinician—such as your primary care doctor, cardiologist, or nurse practitioner. A Type 2 NPI identifies a billing organization, multi-specialty clinic group, outpatient surgery center, or hospital facility (such as Watson Clinic LLP, Lakeland Regional Health Medical Center, or Winter Haven Hospital). Both must participate on your exact 2027 Plan ID to avoid out-of-network split-billing claims.

Why is asking a doctor's office "Do you take Ambetter or UnitedHealthcare?" risky for 2027?

Insurance carriers sell multiple sub-networks under the same brand name in Polk County—for example, Ambetter Signature vs. Ambetter Thrive on HealthCare.gov, or narrow HMO vs. broader PPO Medicare Advantage contracts. A clinic may accept one sub-network and decline another from the exact same carrier. Always verify using the 14-character HIOS Plan ID (ACA) or CMS Contract-Plan ID (Medicare).

Does Humana cover Watson Clinic doctors for the 2027 plan year?

For the 2027 plan year, Humana is out of network at Watson Clinic across plans. Patients who want in-network access to Watson Clinic physicians in 2027 should verify alternative ACA Marketplace or Medicare options by exact Plan ID and physician NPI before enrollment deadlines close.

Where do I find the exact 2027 Plan ID before enrolling?

On HealthCare.gov, every ACA Marketplace plan displays a 14-character HIOS Plan ID (five digits + FL + seven digits) on the plan details summary. On Medicare Plan Finder, every Medicare Advantage and Part D plan displays a Contract and Plan ID (beginning with H, R, or S followed by four digits and a three-digit plan number).
