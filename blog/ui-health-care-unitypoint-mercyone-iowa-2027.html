Navigating Des Moines, Cedar Rapids, Iowa City & Quad Cities Networks in 2027

Across Iowa's primary corridors — Des Moines and West Des Moines (Polk and Dallas counties), the Cedar Rapids–Iowa City Corridor (Linn and Johnson counties), the Quad Cities (Scott County), Waterloo/Cedar Falls (Black Hawk County), and Western Iowa (Council Bluffs and Sioux City) — hospital and specialist access centers on three statewide health systems: University of Iowa Health Care (UI Health Care) (operating the main University Campus at 200 Hawkins Drive, UI Stead Family Children's Hospital, Holden Comprehensive Cancer Center, and UI Health Care Medical Center Downtown), UnityPoint Health (Iowa Methodist, Iowa Lutheran, Blank Children's, and Methodist West in Des Moines; St. Luke's in Cedar Rapids and Sioux City; Allen Hospital in Waterloo; and Trinity in the Quad Cities), and MercyOne (Des Moines, West Des Moines, Waterloo, Dubuque, Mason City, Siouxland, and Genesis Health System in Davenport).

In addition, major independent multi-specialty groups — including The Iowa Clinic in West Des Moines, Physicians' Clinic of Iowa (PCI) and independent Mercy Medical Center in Cedar Rapids, and McFarland Clinic / Mary Greeley Medical Center in Ames — contract separately across 2027 ACA Marketplace and Medicare Advantage plans.

3 Steps for Iowa Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Distinguish Mercy Cedar Rapids from Statewide MercyOne: In Linn County, Mercy Medical Center Cedar Rapids (and Hall-Perrine Cancer Center) is an independent hospital distinct from Trinity Health's MercyOne system. Always verify by exact street address and 10-digit NPI.
  2. Check Independent Multi-Specialty Group Alignment: In Polk and Dallas counties, verify The Iowa Clinic alongside UnityPoint or MercyOne; in Story County (Ames), verify both McFarland Clinic and Mary Greeley Medical Center.
  3. Confirm Out-of-County & Border Referral Rules: If you live in Des Moines or Davenport and travel to UI Health Care Holden Comprehensive Cancer Center in Iowa City — or live in Council Bluffs and cross into Omaha for Nebraska Medicine or CHI Health — verify cross-county and cross-border network coverage.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at UI Health Care, UnityPoint Iowa Methodist, MercyOne Des Moines, St. Luke's Cedar Rapids, Mercy Cedar Rapids, and Genesis Davenport ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Iowa ACA Marketplace plans cover UI Health Care, UnityPoint Health, and MercyOne?

Wellmark Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Iowa (Blue HMO and Blue POS), Medica (Medica Insure, Elevate by Medica, and Medica with CHI Health), Oscar Health, and Ambetter Health contract differently by county, so Des Moines, Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, and Quad Cities residents should verify their exact sub-network before enrolling.

How do Des Moines and Cedar Rapids residents verify independent groups like The Iowa Clinic, PCI, and McFarland Clinic?

Independent multi-specialty groups such as The Iowa Clinic (Des Moines metro), Physicians' Clinic of Iowa (Cedar Rapids), and McFarland Clinic (Ames and Central Iowa) bill under their own group NPIs and should be verified separately from the UnityPoint, MercyOne, or Mary Greeley hospitals where their surgeons operate.

How does UI Health Care Medical Center Downtown (formerly Mercy Iowa City) appear in 2027 directories?

Following the integration of Mercy Iowa City into University of Iowa Health Care as UI Health Care Medical Center Downtown, patients should verify both the University Campus (200 Hawkins Drive) and the Downtown campus alongside UI Physicians faculty NPIs.
