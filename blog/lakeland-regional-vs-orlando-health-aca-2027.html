The Two Major Hospital Systems in Lakeland

Lakeland residents have access to two major acute-care hospital operations:

  • Lakeland Regional Health Medical Center: Located at 1324 Lakeland Hills Boulevard, this 864-bed facility hosts Polk County's only Level II Trauma Center and the Carol Jenkins Barnett Pavilion for Women and Children.
  • Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital: Located at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road in South Lakeland, this 302-bed hospital facility opened to patients in July 2026.

When selecting an Affordable Care Act (ACA) Marketplace plan during Open Enrollment (November 1 to January 15), many policyholders assume that an in-network status with one health system automatically includes the other. In reality, each insurer negotiates independent provider agreements with each facility.

Emergency Room Visits vs. Inpatient & Outpatient Specialist Care

Under the federal No Surprises Act and ACA essential health benefit regulations, emergency medical conditions are protected regardless of plan network:

  • Emergency Stabilization: If you experience an emergency and go to either Lakeland Regional Health Medical Center or Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands Hospital, your health insurer must process emergency room facility and physician claims at in-network cost-sharing rates without requiring prior authorization.
  • Elective Admissions & Outpatient Services: Once you are stabilized, or if you need planned surgery, chemotherapy infusion, MRI diagnostics, or specialist consultations, in-network coverage rules apply. If your plan does not contract with that hospital, non-emergency care will be billed at out-of-network rates or denied entirely on HMO plans.

The LRH Physician Group Out-of-Network Catch

A frequent coverage issue involves hospital physician networks. Lakeland Regional Health employs hundreds of clinicians under the LRH Physician Group. If you are admitted to LRH under an ACA plan that contracts with the hospital building but excludes LRH Physician Group providers, individual physician fees may face billing scrutiny.

To ensure seamless coverage, both the facility National Provider Identifier (NPI) and the physician practice group NPI should be verified against your 14-character Marketplace Plan ID.

How to Verify Both Hospital and Doctor Access in Polk County

  1. Identify your hospital priorities: Determine whether your primary preference is LRH in North Lakeland or Orlando Health Highlands in South Lakeland.
  2. Gather physician NPIs: Record your primary doctor and key specialists.
  3. Review the 14-digit Plan ID: Do not rely on carrier marketing names; verify the exact plan code on HealthCare.gov.
  4. Cross-check hospital and clinic directories: Validate facility inclusion for outpatient surgical suites, diagnostic labs, and emergency transport.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my ACA Marketplace plan cover emergency visits at both Lakeland Regional Health and Orlando Health?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and ACA emergency regulations, emergency room evaluation and stabilization must be covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of the hospital's network contract. However, elective admissions and outpatient follow-up care require an active in-network contract.

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace carriers contract with Lakeland Regional Health?

Florida Blue (BlueCare and BlueOptions tiers) maintains established in-network contracts with Lakeland Regional Health Medical Center and LRH Physician Group. Coverage under other carriers such as Ambetter, Oscar, and UnitedHealthcare varies by specific 14-digit Plan ID.

Can a doctor be out of network even if the hospital facility is in network?

Yes. Hospital facilities and physician practice groups bill under independent contracts. An attending surgeon, anesthesiologist, or imaging group may be out of network even if the physical hospital building participates in your health plan.
