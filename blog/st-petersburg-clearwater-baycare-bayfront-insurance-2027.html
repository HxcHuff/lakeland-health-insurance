3 Verification Steps for Clearwater, St. Petersburg, Largo & Palm Harbor Residents

  1. Verify BayCare vs. Orlando Health Bayfront Specialist Referrals in St. Pete: In St. Petersburg, independent cardiology and orthopedic groups may admit to either BayCare St. Anthony's or Orlando Health Bayfront. Confirm both your specialist group NPI and their admitting hospital on your 2027 plan.
  2. Check North Pinellas / Pasco County Commuter Coverage (Palm Harbor, Tarpon Springs & New Port Richey): Residents in Palm Harbor, East Lake, and Tarpon Springs often use AdventHealth North Pinellas or cross into New Port Richey (Pasco County). Make sure your 2027 HMO or EPO covers providers across Pinellas, Pasco, and Hillsborough counties.
  3. Confirm Moffitt Cancer Center & Tampa General Access Across the Bay: If you want seamless access across the Howard Frankland or Courtney Campbell bridges to Moffitt Cancer Center or Tampa General Hospital, verify Hillsborough tertiary hospital inclusion before enrolling.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and CMS Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at any hospital ER are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of whether the hospital is in your plan's network; network tiers govern scheduled surgeries, non-emergency inpatient admissions, and outpatient clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance does BayCare Morton Plant Hospital in Clearwater accept in 2027?

BayCare Morton Plant Hospital in Clearwater accepts Original Medicare with any Medigap supplement, BayCarePlus and contracted Medicare Advantage plans, and 2027 ACA Marketplace plans from Florida Blue, Oscar Health, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare (subject to specific plan sub-network).

Does Orlando Health Bayfront Hospital in St. Petersburg accept the same plans as BayCare?

Several 2027 Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter ACA plans and Medicare Advantage PPOs in Pinellas County include both Orlando Health Bayfront Hospital and BayCare St. Anthony's Hospital, while narrow-network HMOs may prefer one system.

Is Johns Hopkins All Children's Hospital in St. Petersburg covered on 2027 ACA Marketplace plans?

Johns Hopkins All Children's Hospital participates in select 2027 Florida Blue, Ambetter, Oscar Health, and UnitedHealthcare ACA Marketplace networks in Pinellas County. Parents should verify both the hospital facility and Johns Hopkins All Children's pediatric specialist group IDs.
