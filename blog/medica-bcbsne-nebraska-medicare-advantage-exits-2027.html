Medica & BCBSNE Nebraska Medicare Advantage Exits (2027): Omaha, Lincoln & SEP Guide

Medica is terminating all individual Medicare Advantage PPO plans (Medica Value PPO, Medica Preferred PPO, and Medica Select PPO) across 8 core Eastern Nebraska counties effective December 31, 2026, alongside select BCBSNE Core HMO / Access PPO and Wellcare county closures in Omaha, Lincoln, and rural Nebraska. Affected seniors must actively choose replacement 2027 coverage or revert automatically to Original Medicare without Part D prescription coverage.

Exact 2027 Deadlines & Effective Dates (Do Not Miss These)

Because Medica is exiting 8 core Eastern Nebraska counties on December 31, 2026 and select BCBSNE and Wellcare Medicare Advantage plan IDs are non-renewing across Omaha, Lincoln, and rural Nebraska counties, affected members must watch three federal timelines:

  • Annual Election Period (AEP, October 15 to December 7, 2026): Enroll in your 2027 replacement coverage by December 7 for seamless January 1, 2027 effective dates and new ID cards before the new year starts.
  • Federal Non-Renewal Special Enrollment Period (December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027): If your 2026 plan is terminated and you miss December 7, your official non-renewal notice keeps a federal SEP open through February 28, 2027:
    • Enrolled December 8 to December 31, 2026: effective January 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled January 1 to January 31, 2027: effective February 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled February 1 to February 28, 2027: effective March 1, 2027.
  • 63-Day Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window (November 2, 2026 through roughly March 4, 2027): Displaced members returning to Original Medicare may purchase an eligible standardized Medigap plan (Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L if first eligible for Medicare before January 1, 2020, or Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L (if first eligible before 2020) or Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L (if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting, paired with a standalone 2027 Part D drug plan (featuring the $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 defined-standard deductible). Note: enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific Medigap Guaranteed Issue right.

Fallback exposure if you take no action: Reverting automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 removes Part D prescription drug coverage and leaves you responsible for the 2027 Original Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible of $1,776 per benefit period and 20% Part B coinsurance with no annual out-of-pocket cap.

Which Nebraska Counties Are Impacted and Are Hospitals Closing?

No Nebraska hospitals are closing. Nebraska Medicine, CHI Health, Bryan Health, and Methodist Health System remain fully open across Omaha, Lincoln, and greater Nebraska. Medica's termination specifically discontinues Medica Value PPO, Medica Preferred PPO, and Medica Select PPO across Butler, Cass, Dodge, Douglas, Lancaster, Sarpy, Saunders, and Washington counties, while additional BCBSNE Core HMO / Access PPO and Wellcare county contractions affect metro and rural ZIP codes.

In addition, multiple rural Nebraska counties face shrinking or zero traditional MA-PD availability for 2027, making Original Medicare plus 63-day Guaranteed Issue Medigap Plan G and a standalone 2027 Part D plan an especially important path to price side by side with active Omaha and Lincoln Medicare Advantage Plan IDs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Nebraska counties are losing Medica Medicare Advantage plans in 2027?

Medica is terminating all individual Medicare Advantage PPO plans (Medica Value PPO, Medica Preferred PPO, and Medica Select PPO) effective December 31, 2026 across 8 Eastern Nebraska counties: Butler, Cass, Dodge, Douglas, Lancaster, Sarpy, Saunders, and Washington counties.

Are BCBSNE and Wellcare also closing specific Nebraska Medicare plans for 2027?

Yes. Select local BCBSNE Core HMO and Access/Secure PPO plan IDs and certain Wellcare PPO options are non-renewing or trimming county footprints in the Omaha metro and surrounding Nebraska counties for 2027. Check your October non-renewal or ANOC letter to verify your exact Plan ID.

Can displaced Nebraska seniors switch to Medigap Plan G without medical underwriting?

Yes. Displaced members with a non-renewal notice have a federal 63-day Guaranteed Issue window (roughly November 2, 2026 through March 4, 2027) to buy standardized Medigap Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L (if first eligible before January 1, 2020) or Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L (if first eligible before 2020) or Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L (if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) (if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) without health questions. Enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific GI right.

What happens if I live in a rural Nebraska county with no 2027 MA-PD option or take no action?

If your plan ends December 31 and you take no action, you revert to Original Medicare Parts A and B on January 1, 2027 with no Part D prescription drug coverage, no annual out-of-pocket cap, the $1,776 Part A deductible, and 20% Part B coinsurance.
