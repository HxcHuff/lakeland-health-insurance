How Health ProtectorGuard Choice 1, Choice 2, Choice 3 & Choice Value Work

Direct answer: Underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company (a UnitedHealthcare company, Policy Form HPG3-GRI and state variations), Health ProtectorGuard (HPG) is a fixed-indemnity insurance product that pays stated cash amounts for covered medical services regardless of the actual amount billed by a provider. It is a supplement to health insurance and is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the Affordable Care Act (ACA).

Why do shoppers see two different naming systems when researching HPG online?

  • The Modular "Choice 1 / Choice 2 / Choice 3 / Choice Value" Design: Built around a 2-step chassis where Step 1 is the Choice Hospital Base Plan (paying $1,000 per day for inpatient hospital confinement in Year 1) and Step 2 adds a Wellness, Office Visit, and Prescription Drug ("WORx") package—creating HPG Choice 1 (Level 1), HPG Choice 2 (Level 2), or HPG Choice 3 (Level 3), or keeping hospital-focused base benefits (often referred to by shoppers as Choice Value).
  • The Current Florida "Enhanced HPG" & "Guard" Series (Forms 52001-G202605 & 52002-G202605): Bundles hospital, surgical, doctor visit, wellness, and Rx cash payouts into unified tiers—Select 2000 ($2,000/day hospital), Preferred 4000 ($4,000/day hospital), and Premier 5000 ($5,000/day hospital)—plus the admission-focused Guard 4000, Guard 5000, and Guard 6000 series.

Critical Limitations: Fixed Indemnity Is Not Major Medical Insurance

Before selecting any Health ProtectorGuard tier, every Florida shopper should compare three structural differences against an ACA Marketplace major medical plan:

  1. No True Out-of-Pocket Maximum: Fixed indemnity pays a fixed dollar amount per day or per procedure. If a complex hospitalization or cancer treatment bills $90,000 after network repricing and your plan pays $4,000/day for 5 days ($20,000), you remain responsible for the remaining $70,000 balance.
  2. Pre-Existing Condition & Waiting Period Rules: Unlike ACA Marketplace plans, fixed indemnity policies are medically underwritten and exclude pre-existing conditions for a stated lookback/waiting period, plus exclude routine maternity and childbirth.
  3. Check ACA Subsidies First During Open Enrollment: Between November 1 and January 15, many Florida households earning between 100% and 400% of the Federal Poverty Level qualify for 2027 ACA Premium Tax Credits that lower the cost of comprehensive major medical coverage with a true annual out-of-pocket cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Health ProtectorGuard Choice 1, Choice 3, and Select 2000?

HPG Choice 1 and Choice 3 use the Choice hospital base schedule ($1,000 per day for Year 1 inpatient hospital confinement) paired with WORx Level 1 (4 office visits at $80/$100) or Level 3 (6 office visits at $125/$150). Select 2000 is part of the Enhanced Health ProtectorGuard series and doubles the Year 1 daily inpatient hospital payout to $2,000 per day plus $2,000 for the first inpatient admission day.

What is Health ProtectorGuard Choice Value?

Shoppers searching for Health ProtectorGuard Choice Value are typically looking at the entry-level HPG hospital base configuration ($1,000 per day inpatient hospital confinement and $4,000 first-day admission on Guard 4000) designed to keep monthly premiums lower by limiting outpatient office visit and wellness riders.

Does Health ProtectorGuard use the UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus network?

Yes. Policyholders can visit any doctor or hospital, but using a UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus network provider gives access to pre-negotiated network discounts and direct provider claim filing.

Is Health ProtectorGuard Choice 1 or Choice 3 considered Minimum Essential Coverage?

No. Health ProtectorGuard is a fixed-indemnity insurance policy underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company. It pays stated cash benefit amounts per covered service and is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the Affordable Care Act.
