How Nemours Children's Health Operates Across Florida in 2027

Nemours Children's Health is one of the nation's largest integrated pediatric health systems, operating its flagship freestanding 130-bed Nemours Children's Hospital, Florida in Orlando's Lake Nona Medical City alongside major multi-specialty pediatric clinics on the St. Johns River Southbank in Jacksonville, in Pensacola, and across Central Florida (including Lakeland, Winter Haven, Kissimmee, Winter Garden, and Melbourne).

When choosing a 2027 family ACA Marketplace plan on HealthCare.gov or coordinating coverage with Florida KidCare (MediKids, Florida Healthy Kids, or Children's Medical Services), parents must look beyond adult hospital networks. A plan that covers your adult primary care doctor in Polk, Orange, Osceola, or Duval County may still require specific sub-network participation or out-of-county referrals for pediatric cardiology, neurology, orthopedics, or oncology at Nemours.

3 Steps for Florida Families Enrolling for 2027

  1. Verify Both the Pediatric Physician Group & Inpatient Facility: In Orlando, Nemours owns both the Lake Nona hospital and the physician group. In Jacksonville, your child's Nemours specialist bills under The Nemours Foundation while inpatient surgery takes place next door at Wolfson Children's Hospital.
  2. Compare Central Florida Pediatric Systems Side-by-Side: Orlando and Polk County families frequently use teachers and specialists across Nemours Children's Hospital, Orlando Health Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children, and AdventHealth for Children. Review our AdventHealth vs. Orlando Health 2027 guide to see which ACA tiers include multiple pediatric hospitals.
  3. Coordinate Household MAGI and Florida KidCare: When household income places children in Florida KidCare or Children's Medical Services (CMS Plan) while parents enroll on HealthCare.gov, verify Nemours participation on both the child's KidCare plan and the family's Marketplace options.

Pediatric Emergency & Continuity of Care Protections (No Surprises Act & FL Statute § 627.64194): Under the federal No Surprises Act, pediatric emergency department screening and stabilization at Nemours Children's Hospital in Lake Nona are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of network contract status. For children in active treatment for complex or chronic conditions whose plan network changes for 2027, Florida Statute § 627.64194 provides up to 90 days of continuity of care at in-network cost-sharing levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 Florida ACA Marketplace plans cover Nemours Children's Hospital in Lake Nona and Nemours clinics in Jacksonville?

Nemours Children's Health participates in select Florida Blue networks (including BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific regional ACA Marketplace plans from carriers such as Oscar Health, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare across Central and Northeast Florida, though narrow HMO tiers must be verified by exact plan ID.

How do Nemours Children's Health specialists in Jacksonville coordinate with Wolfson Children's Hospital?

In Jacksonville, Nemours Children's Health operates the major outpatient pediatric subspecialty clinic on the Southbank while admitting pediatric inpatients primarily to Wolfson Children's Hospital (Baptist Health), so Northeast Florida families should confirm that their 2027 plan covers both entities.

Are emergency visits at Nemours Children's Hospital in Orlando covered under the No Surprises Act?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, pediatric emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Nemours Children's Hospital in Orlando (Lake Nona) are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of whether the family's ACA plan is contracted for routine specialty appointments.
