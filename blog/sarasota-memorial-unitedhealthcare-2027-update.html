Sarasota Memorial & UnitedHealthcare 2027 Deal Update

Good news for Sarasota and Venice patients: after threatening a January 1, 2027 termination, Sarasota Memorial Health Care System (SMH) and UnitedHealthcare reached a new multi-year agreement keeping hospitals and First Physicians Group in network—while nearby Lee Health and AdventHealth still face active UHC deadlines.

Confirmed Deal: Sarasota Memorial Stays In Network With UnitedHealthcare for 2027

Earlier this fall, Sarasota Memorial Health Care System (SMH) and First Physicians Group notified patients that their UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage contract was set to expire on December 31, 2026. That impasse is now resolved:

  • Multi-Year Agreement Effective January 1, 2027: Sarasota Memorial and UnitedHealthcare finalized a new multi-year contract ensuring uninterrupted in-network access across SMH-Sarasota, SMH-Venice, outpatient/urgent care centers, and First Physicians Group.
  • Covered Product Lines: The renewed agreement covers UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage (including AARP-branded plans), Group Retiree MA, Dual Special Needs Plans (D-SNP), Employer Commercial plans, and Individual ACA Marketplace plans.

Do not stop checking your 2027 ANOC yet: Even though Sarasota Memorial stayed in network with UHC, many 2027 Florida Medicare Advantage plans still modified copays, Part D formularies, dental allowances, or secondary physician group contracts (such as independent specialists or Millennium Physician Group offices).

Contrast: Active Florida UnitedHealthcare Splits Still Pending for Jan 1, 2027

Southwest and Central Florida snowbirds and residents who split care across county lines should note that Sarasota Memorial's deal does not resolve two other major Florida UnitedHealthcare standoffs:

  • Lee Health (Fort Myers / Cape Coral / Lee County): Lee Health gave formal notice ending hospital and physician contracts with UnitedHealthcare across employer, individual, and Medicare Advantage plans effective January 1, 2027.
  • AdventHealth Florida: Facing a December 31, 2026 contract termination window with UnitedHealthcare across impacted Florida facilities if no agreement is finalized.
  • Cigna & Molina ACA Exits in Florida: Under-65 Florida shoppers on Cigna or Molina Marketplace plans still must pick a new carrier by December 15, 2026 for January 1 coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sarasota Memorial Hospital dropping UnitedHealthcare in 2027?

No. After earlier expiration warnings, Sarasota Memorial Health Care System and UnitedHealthcare reached a new multi-year agreement keeping SMH hospitals, urgent care centers, and First Physicians Group in network for January 1, 2027.

Does the new Sarasota Memorial and UnitedHealthcare deal include AARP Medicare Advantage plans?

Yes. The multi-year agreement covers UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans (including AARP-branded plans), Group Retiree MA, D-SNPs, commercial employer plans, and ACA Marketplace plans.

Does Sarasota Memorial's deal also fix Lee Health or AdventHealth with UnitedHealthcare?

No. Sarasota Memorial's agreement is separate from Lee Health's January 1, 2027 UnitedHealthcare termination notice in Lee County and AdventHealth's separate Florida contract negotiations with UnitedHealthcare.

Should Sarasota Memorial patients still review their 2027 Medicare Advantage plan before December 7?

Yes. Keeping your hospital in network is only one piece of 2027 coverage—you should still check your Annual Notice of Change for prescription formulary changes, Part D deductibles, dental/OTC benefit cuts, and independent specialist networks.
