Naples, Marco Island & Collier County Networks in 2027

In Collier County and southern Lee County — spanning Naples, North Naples, Marco Island, Golden Gate, Ave Maria, and Bonita Springs — inpatient and specialty hospital care is anchored by two competing systems: nonprofit NCH (Naples Comprehensive Health) (operating NCH Baker Hospital Downtown, NCH North Naples Hospital on Immokalee Road, the NCH Rooney Heart Institute, and freestanding ERs in Northeast Collier and Bonita Springs) and Physicians Regional Healthcare System (operating Physicians Regional – Pine Ridge, Physicians Regional – Collier Blvd, and Physicians Regional – North Naples).

Meanwhile, primary and multi-specialty outpatient care across Collier County is split among NCH Medical Group, Physicians Regional Medical Group, and independent Millennium Physician Group. Because Cigna and Molina are exiting the Florida ACA Marketplace in 2027 and Lee Health's UnitedHealthcare split affects neighboring Lee County, Collier County residents should verify hospital and physician group contracts before Open Enrollment ends.

3 Steps for Naples, Marco Island & Bonita Springs Residents

  1. Verify NCH vs. Physicians Regional Sub-Networks: On the Collier County ACA Marketplace, several lower-premium HMO plans include Physicians Regional Pine Ridge and Collier Blvd while excluding NCH Baker and North Naples Hospitals for elective surgery (or vice versa).
  2. Check the Collier / Lee County Line in Bonita Springs & North Naples: If you live in North Naples (34109 / 34110 / 34119) or Bonita Springs (34134 / 34135) and see doctors across both Collier and Lee counties, confirm your 2027 HMO covers both rating areas.
  3. Confirm Tertiary Referral Paths to Miami or Tampa: Collier County patients who travel across Alligator Alley to Baptist Health Miami Cancer Institute / UHealth or north on I-75 to Moffitt Cancer Center in Tampa should verify out-of-county oncology coverage before enrolling.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at NCH Baker Hospital Downtown, NCH North Naples, NCH Marco Island Urgent/Emergency Center, and Physicians Regional Pine Ridge and Collier Blvd ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Collier County ACA Marketplace plans cover both NCH Healthcare System and Physicians Regional?

In Collier County (Naples, Marco Island, Ave Maria, and Immokalee), some ACA Marketplace sub-networks cover Physicians Regional Healthcare System while excluding NCH Baker and NCH North Naples Hospitals (or vice versa), so enrollees must verify their exact 2027 plan ID before enrolling.

Are NCH Baker Hospital Downtown and NCH North Naples Hospital in the same insurance network?

Yes. NCH (Naples Comprehensive Health) negotiates system-wide hospital contracts covering both NCH Baker Hospital Downtown and NCH North Naples Hospital, as well as NCH Rooney Heart Institute and NCH Wingfieldick Orthopedic Institute.

How do Bonita Springs and Estero residents coordinate Lee Health and Collier County hospital networks?

Because Bonita Springs and Estero sit on the Lee County and Collier County line, residents using both NCH or Physicians Regional in North Naples and Lee Health in Fort Myers/Estero must verify that their 2027 plan covers providers across both counties.
