Does Concentra Lakeland Bill Insurance or Workers' Comp?

Direct answer: It depends on your plan. Look up Concentra Urgent Care Lakeland in your plan's provider directory using your plan ID, and call the clinic before you go. Your plan sets urgent care cost-sharing separately from emergency room cost-sharing. Confirm the exact plan ID and coverage year before you rely on a directory listing.

Concentra Urgent Care in Lakeland treats walk-in illness and work injuries. Ask whether the visit bills insurance or workers' compensation.

Last reviewed: October 1, 2026.

Does urgent care cost the same as the ER under my plan?

Not necessarily. Plans set separate cost-sharing for urgent care and emergency room visits, and both amounts are listed in your plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Concentra Lakeland, 5040 US Hwy 98 N., Lakeland 33809; 863.859.3511; walk-in.

What should I bring to an urgent care visit?

Your insurance card and photo ID, a medication list, and your plan's urgent care copay from its Summary of Benefits and Coverage.

HealthCare.gov: HMOs usually limit coverage to network doctors and generally don't cover out-of-network care except in an emergency; EPOs cover services only in network except emergencies; POS plans require a PCP referral to see a specialist; PPOs let you use out-of-network providers without a referral for an additional cost.

Urgent care vs. the ER under your plan

Work-related injuries generally go through your employer's workers' compensation coverage, not your health plan. Report the injury to your employer first and ask where to be seen. If a health plan claim is denied as work-related, ask the plan how to resolve it.

How to check the clinic in your plan

Network status can differ by plan; check the plan's provider directory before enrolling. Use the exact plan ID and coverage year, then call the facility billing office and write down the date and the person you spoke with.
