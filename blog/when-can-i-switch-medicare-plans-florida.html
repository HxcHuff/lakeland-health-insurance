When Can I Switch Medicare Plans in Florida? Every Window

Last reviewed: October 5, 2026.

Annual Enrollment, Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment, initial enrollment, and event-specific Special Enrollment Periods for Polk County residents.

By David Huff, licensed Florida health insurance agent #W371813 · Updated September 30, 2026

Direct answer: Most people with Medicare can switch plans in two windows.

  • Annual Enrollment Period: October 15 to December 7 each year. Changes start January 1.
  • Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period: January 1 to March 31, for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan. It allows one change.

What changes in Annual Enrollment?

Plans can change premiums, networks, drug lists and benefits every January 1. Your plan must send an Annual Notice of Change by September 30. Use it to see what changes for next year, then check every doctor, pharmacy and prescription against the new plan documents. For 2027:

  • The Part D out-of-pocket limit rises to $2,400 (up from $2,100 in 2026).
  • The Medicare Advantage in-network out-of-pocket ceiling is $9,850.

See the Polk County Annual Enrollment checklist.

Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment: one change only

From January 1 to March 31, if you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, you can make one switch:

  • to another Medicare Advantage plan, or
  • back to Original Medicare, adding a stand-alone drug plan if you want one.

You cannot use this window to move from Original Medicare into Medicare Advantage, or to switch stand-alone drug plans while staying in Original Medicare.

Can I switch Medigap plans any time in Florida?

You can apply at any time. Outside your 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period or a federal guaranteed-issue right, an insurer can use medical underwriting and can decline you. Check underwriting before you drop a Medigap policy or leave Original Medicare. See Medicare Advantage vs Medicare Supplement.

Before you change anything

  1. Confirm which window applies to you.
  2. List every doctor, facility, pharmacy and prescription.
  3. Check each one on the exact new plan and plan year.
  4. Compare total yearly cost, not only the premium.
  5. Confirm the start date so you have no gap.

Common questions

When can I switch Medicare plans in Florida?

During the Annual Enrollment Period from October 15 to December 7, during Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment from January 1 to March 31 if you are already in a Medicare Advantage plan, or during a Special Enrollment Period if a qualifying event applies to you.

What is the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period?

It runs October 15 through December 7 each year. You can switch between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage, change Medicare Advantage plans, or join, switch or drop a drug plan. Changes generally take effect January 1.

What is the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period?

It runs January 1 through March 31 for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan. You can make one change: to another Medicare Advantage plan, or to Original Medicare with the option to join a stand-alone drug plan. The change takes effect the first day of the month after the plan gets your request.

Can I switch Medicare plans outside those windows?

Only with a Special Enrollment Period. Examples include moving out of your plan's service area, having Medicaid or Extra Help, losing employer coverage, or a 5-star plan being available. The allowed change and timing depend on the event.

Can I switch Medigap plans any time in Florida?

You can apply any time, but outside your 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period or a guaranteed-issue right, the insurer can use medical underwriting and may decline your application.
