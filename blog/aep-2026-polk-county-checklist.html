Medicare Annual Enrollment Checklist for Polk County

Last reviewed: September 30, 2026.

Annual Enrollment runs Oct 15 to Dec 7. Check doctors, prescriptions, and total yearly costs before you switch Medicare plans in Polk County. A review is not enrollment.

By David Huff, licensed Florida health insurance agent #W371813 · Updated September 30, 2026

Direct answer: Medicare Annual Enrollment for 2027 coverage runs October 15 to December 7, 2026. Changes take effect January 1, 2027.

  • Start with your Annual Notice of Change. Your plan had to send it by September 30.
  • Check everything against the 2027 plan documents: every doctor, hospital, pharmacy and prescription.
  • Compare total yearly cost, not just the premium.
  • Change plans only if a verified comparison shows your current plan no longer fits.

The checklist

1. Read the Annual Notice of Change

  • Premium, deductible and out-of-pocket limit for 2027
  • Drug list and tier changes for each of your prescriptions
  • Network changes for your doctors and hospitals
  • Prior authorization or referral changes
  • Changes to extra benefits you actually use

2. Check every prescription

  • Search each drug by name, strength and dose on the 2027 drug list.
  • Note the tier, prior authorization, step therapy and quantity limits.
  • Confirm your pharmacy is in network, and check whether it is a preferred pharmacy on that plan.

3. Check every doctor and facility

  • Search each doctor, specialist, hospital, imaging center and lab by the exact 2027 plan name.
  • Call the billing office to confirm.
  • Polk County checks usually include Lakeland Regional Health, Watson Clinic, and Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital, which opened in July 2026.

4. Compare total yearly cost Add the premiums, the expected copays and coinsurance, and your drug costs for the full year. A $0 premium plan with high drug costs can cost more than a plan with a premium.

5. Confirm your enrollment rights Annual Enrollment covers Medicare Advantage and Part D changes. Medigap is separate. If you are thinking of leaving Medicare Advantage for Original Medicare with a Medigap policy, check medical underwriting before you disenroll. See switching from Medicare Advantage back to Original Medicare.

6. Check the effective date and keep records Keep your confirmation number. Watch for the new plan's ID card in December.

Documents to bring to a review

  • Your Medicare card and your current plan card
  • The 2027 Annual Notice of Change
  • A list of prescriptions with dose and pharmacy
  • A list of doctors and facilities
  • Any Extra Help or Medicaid letters

Common questions

When is Medicare Annual Enrollment for 2027 coverage?

October 15 through December 7, 2026. Changes you make during that period generally take effect January 1, 2027.

What should I check before Annual Enrollment?

Read your Annual Notice of Change, then check every prescription, doctor, hospital and pharmacy against the 2027 plan documents and compare total yearly cost, not only the premium.

When does my plan send the Annual Notice of Change?

Medicare plans must send it by September 30. It summarizes changes for January 1 but does not replace the full drug list, provider directory or Evidence of Coverage.

What is the Part D out-of-pocket limit for 2027?

$2,400 for covered drugs, up from $2,100 in 2026, according to CMS.

Can I change Medicare plans after December 7?

Only in limited cases. From January 1 through March 31, people already in a Medicare Advantage plan can make one change. Otherwise you need a Special Enrollment Period, such as for a move or for Medicaid or Extra Help.
