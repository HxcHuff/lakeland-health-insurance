Medicare AEP 2026 for 2027 Coverage — Polk County Checklist

What to verify in official 2027 information before the December 7 deadline: providers, prescriptions, costs, plan rules, and the effective date.

Updated August 17, 2026 · By David Huff, Licensed FL Agent
Short answer: Medicare AEP runs October 15 – December 7, 2026. An approved AEP change generally takes effect January 1, 2027. Read your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC), then verify your doctors, prescriptions, costs, and plan rules against the exact official 2027 information.

⚠️ The deadline that matters: December 7, 2026

An AEP request submitted after December 7 generally cannot take effect January 1. A Special Enrollment Period may apply only when the specific eligibility and timing rules are met. Allow enough time to verify the decision before the deadline.

The 2026 AEP Polk County timeline

By September 30, 2026
Your current carrier sends an Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) summarizing important 2027 coverage and cost changes. It does not replace exact plan-document, network, formulary, pharmacy, and utilization-rule checks.
October 1 – 14, 2026
Official 2027 plan information can be reviewed. AEP enrollment requests for 2027 coverage cannot be submitted before October 15.
October 15, 2026
AEP officially opens. Eligible change requests for a January 1, 2027 effective date can be submitted starting today.
November (mid-AEP)
Continue exact-plan verification and resolve conflicting provider, formulary, pharmacy, cost, or effective-date information.
December 7, 2026
AEP ends. December 7 is the last day to submit an eligible AEP change request.
January 1, 2027
An approved AEP change generally takes effect. Confirm acceptance, the effective date, ID card, network, formulary, and pharmacy before relying on new coverage.
January 1 – March 31, 2027
Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA-OEP). A person already in Medicare Advantage may make one change to another Medicare Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare and, if needed, join a standalone Part D plan.

The Polk County AEP checklist — six things to verify before switching

1. Every prescription, on every plan you're considering

  • Run each medication through the 2027 formulary
  • Check the exact plan's tier placement; tier counts and meanings vary by plan
  • Confirm quantity limits and step therapy haven't changed
  • Check preferred-pharmacy pricing vs standard pharmacy
  • For specialty drugs: verify the plan covers them at all (some plans don't)

2. Every provider you actually use

  • Primary care physician — confirm in-network for 2027 plan ID
  • Every specialist seen in past 12 months
  • Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, BayCare Bartow, Heart of Florida (whichever applies)
  • Preferred imaging center for MRIs/CTs
  • Preferred pharmacy chain (Publix, Walgreens, CVS, Costco, Walmart)
  • Confirm directly with the provider's billing office, not just the carrier directory

3. Maximum Out-of-Pocket (MOOP)

  • 2027 in-network MOOP for the plan
  • 2027 combined MOOP if a PPO (in + out of network)
  • Compare against a high-cost medical year — surgery, hospital care, and Part B-administered treatments; model Part D prescription costs separately
  • $0 premium plans can still carry substantial deductibles, copays, and annual out-of-pocket limits

4. Prior authorization requirements

  • Are MRIs / CTs newly requiring prior auth in 2027?
  • Specialty drugs you take regularly — still approved or now require new auth?
  • Surgical procedures (especially spine, joint replacement)
  • If you're mid-treatment, confirm continuity-of-care rules

5. Ancillary benefits you'll actually use

  • Dental allowance (and which providers accept it in Polk County)
  • Vision allowance and frame benefit
  • Hearing aid coverage and copay
  • OTC quarterly allowance (and which retailers it covers)
  • Meal benefit after hospital discharge
  • Transportation benefit (rides to medical appointments)
  • Part B premium giveback (if any)

6. Total annual cost — not just premium

  • Monthly premium × 12
  • + Expected copays based on your typical year
  • + Expected drug costs by tier
  • + Any deductible
  • = Apples-to-apples annual cost vs current plan

What to bring to your AEP enrollment call

  • Your current Medicare ID card
  • Your ANOC letter (the September mailing from your carrier)
  • A complete list of prescriptions with dosages and pharmacy
  • Your provider list — every doctor and specialist you want to keep
  • Your ZIP code and any planned moves in 2027
  • Any travel patterns — snowbird, frequent flyer, RV traveler all matter for network choice

Polk County AEP review points

  • A $0 plan premium is not $0 total cost. The real comparison includes doctors, hospitals, drug costs, copays, coinsurance, the medical out-of-pocket limit, referrals, and prior authorization rules.
  • Your doctors matter more than the headline benefit. A plan with attractive extras is not a good fit if your primary doctor, specialists, or preferred hospital are not in network.
  • Star ratings are only one signal. A higher-rated plan still needs to match your prescriptions, pharmacy, providers, and budget.
  • Provider directories need a second check. Carrier directories can lag behind real contract changes. Verify with the provider's billing office before enrolling.
  • Dental, vision, OTC, and giveback benefits should be weighed against medical costs. Extras are useful when you will actually use them, but they should not outweigh network fit, prescriptions, and out-of-pocket exposure.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 2026 Medicare AEP dates in Polk County?

October 15, 2026 through December 7, 2026. An approved AEP change generally takes effect January 1, 2027. The federal dates are the same in Polk County.

What should I check on my current plan before AEP?

Check prescriptions and formulary rules, doctors and facilities, the 2027 medical out-of-pocket limit where applicable, prior authorization, supplemental benefits, and total annual cost. The ANOC is a starting point; verify each item in the exact official 2027 plan information.

When does my carrier send the Annual Notice of Change?

By September 30 each year. Read it before AEP starts October 15. Pull from the carrier's online portal if you can't find the mailed copy.

What providers should Polk County residents check during AEP?

Primary care, every specialist seen in the past year, your hospital (Lakeland Regional, Watson Clinic, BayCare, Heart of Florida), imaging center, and preferred pharmacy. Verify with both the carrier and the provider directly.

Can I switch Medicare plans after AEP closes on December 7?

Limited. From January 1 through March 31, a person already in Medicare Advantage may make one change to another Medicare Advantage plan or return to Original Medicare and, if needed, join a standalone Part D plan. Other Special Enrollment Periods depend on the exact qualifying event and permitted action.

How long does AEP plan enrollment take?

Timing varies. Do not rush the verification work: identify the enrollment right, confirm providers and prescriptions, compare total costs and rules, and review the effective date before submitting a request.

Should I switch plans every AEP just to get a better deal?

No. Switching for the sake of switching disrupts continuity. Switch only when a comparison shows a meaningfully better fit on total annual cost, network match, formulary, or ancillaries you'll actually use.
