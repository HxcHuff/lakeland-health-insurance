The Atrium Health vs. Novant Health Split in North Carolina

Across the Charlotte metro area (Mecklenburg, Cabarrus, Union, Gaston, and Iredell counties) and the Piedmont Triad (Winston-Salem, Greensboro, and High Point), healthcare is anchored by two integrated giants: Atrium Health (including Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center, Levine Children's Hospital, and Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist) and Novant Health (including Novant Health Presbyterian Medical Center and Novant Health Forsyth Medical Center).

On the North Carolina ACA Marketplace and in regional Medicare Advantage plans, insurers frequently build narrow-network products around one health system to negotiate lower premium rates. Selecting the wrong sub-network during Open Enrollment can leave your family's primary care physician or hospital out-of-network on January 1, 2027.

Checklist for North Carolina Policyholders

  1. Verify the Sub-Network Name, Not Just the Carrier: In North Carolina, a single insurer may offer multiple plans in the same county that use completely different hospital networks.
  2. Check Triangle & Coastal Referrals: If you travel to Duke Health, UNC Health, or Novant Health New Hanover Regional Medical Center for specialty care, confirm whether your county's EPO plan allows cross-region referrals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do North Carolina ACA Marketplace plans cover both Atrium Health and Novant Health in 2027?

Most narrow-network ACA plans in Charlotte and the Piedmont Triad align primarily with either Atrium Health or Novant Health; Blue Cross NC Blue Value, Blue Home, and carrier-specific EPO networks must be checked by exact sub-network name.

Is Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist in Winston-Salem covered under the same plans as Charlotte?

Not always. North Carolina HealthCare.gov plans are sold by county rating area, so a carrier sub-network in Forsyth or Guilford County may have different hospital contracts than its Mecklenburg County counterpart.

Are emergency room visits at Atrium Health Carolinas Medical Center and Novant Presbyterian protected?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency room in North Carolina must be billed at your policy's in-network cost-sharing rate.
