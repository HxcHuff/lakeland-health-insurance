South Florida's NCI-Designated Cancer Center & Academic Health System

Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center — part of UHealth (University of Miami Health System) and the Miller School of Medicine — is South Florida's only National Cancer Institute (NCI)-designated cancer center. Alongside UHealth Tower, Bascom Palmer Eye Institute, and UHealth satellite campuses in Coral Gables (The Lennar Foundation Medical Center), Doral, Kendall, Plantation, Hollywood, and Boca Raton/Deerfield Beach, UHealth serves patients across Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and Monroe counties.

Because UHealth faculty physicians bill through the University of Miami Medical Group while teaching hospitals at the Miami Health District also involve Jackson Memorial Hospital, patients must verify both the UHealth facility contract and the faculty physician group tax ID before selecting a 2027 ACA Marketplace or Medicare plan.

Florida Statute § 627.64194 Continuity of Care & No Surprises Act Protections

  1. Statutory Continuity of Care (Florida Statute § 627.64194): If an insurance carrier terminates or modifies its network contract with Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center or UHealth while you are in active oncology treatment (including chemotherapy, immunotherapy, radiation therapy, or scheduled surgical oncology), Florida Statute § 627.64194 and federal transition standards allow patients to request up to 90 days of continued care at in-network cost-sharing rates.
  2. Cross-County Referral Rules (Broward & Palm Beach to Miami-Dade): Many ACA Marketplace HMOs sold in Broward or Palm Beach County restrict non-emergency care to your home county unless you select a regional/statewide network tier (such as Florida Blue BlueOptions) or obtain a formal out-of-county tertiary referral to Sylvester in Miami.
  3. Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at the UHealth Tower emergency department and Bascom Palmer ophthalmic emergency room are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of whether your plan contracts with UHealth for routine care.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 South Florida ACA Marketplace plans cover Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center and UHealth?

Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, UHealth Tower, and Bascom Palmer Eye Institute participate in select Florida Blue tiers (such as BlueOptions and contracted BlueCare networks) and specific regional ACA Marketplace sub-networks in Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties, while narrow value HMOs frequently exclude UHealth faculty physicians.

How does Original Medicare with Medigap compare to Medicare Advantage at Sylvester Cancer Center in 2027?

UHealth and Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center accept Original Medicare (Parts A and B) with any Medigap supplement such as Plan G without network referrals, whereas Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans require verifying both UHealth facility and University of Miami Medical Group participation and prior authorization rules.

How does Florida Statute § 627.64194 protect active oncology patients at Sylvester if a 2027 plan network changes?

Under Florida Statute § 627.64194 and federal continuity-of-care rules, patients undergoing active cancer treatment such as chemotherapy, radiation, or scheduled oncology surgery can request up to 90 days of continued in-network cost-sharing when a provider contract terminates or changes.
