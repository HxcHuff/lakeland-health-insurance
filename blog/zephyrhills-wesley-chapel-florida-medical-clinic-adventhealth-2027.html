Frequently Asked Questions

Does Florida Medical Clinic Orlando Health take the same insurance as AdventHealth Zephyrhills in 2027?

Not always. Because Florida Medical Clinic is aligned with Orlando Health while AdventHealth Zephyrhills and AdventHealth Wesley Chapel belong to AdventHealth, narrow-network 2027 ACA Marketplace HMOs and select Medicare Advantage plans may cover one system while placing the other out-of-network.

How does the AdventHealth and UnitedHealthcare December 31, 2026 contract deadline affect Zephyrhills and Wesley Chapel?

AdventHealth Zephyrhills, AdventHealth Dade City, and AdventHealth Wesley Chapel are part of AdventHealth’s statewide Florida agreement with UnitedHealthcare expiring December 31, 2026. If not renewed for 2027, non-emergency care at those campuses would be out-of-network for UnitedHealthcare plans starting January 1, 2027.

Are emergency room visits at AdventHealth Wesley Chapel or BayCare Hospital Wesley Chapel always covered?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department visits at AdventHealth Zephyrhills, AdventHealth Wesley Chapel, BayCare Hospital Wesley Chapel, and Orlando Health Wiregrass Ranch Hospital are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status.
