Why Your 2027 ANOC Letter Matters More Than Usual This Fall

Every September and October, Medicare Advantage (Part C) and Medicare Part D prescription drug plans send members an Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) comparing 2026 benefits side by side with 2027 benefits taking effect on January 1, 2027.

During the Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 through December 7, 2026), many Polk County plans adjusted their in-network Maximum Out-of-Pocket (MOOP) limits, specialist and inpatient hospital copays, Part D prescription drug tiers, and dental or Over-the-Counter (OTC) allowances. If you set the booklet aside without reviewing those changes, your existing plan automatically rolls over on January 1 with the new 2027 cost structure.

The 6 Lines to Check in Your 2027 ANOC Packet

ANOC Section What to Look For (2026 vs. 2027 Column) Why It Matters in Polk County
1. Monthly Plan Premium & Part B Giveback Check whether the plan premium stayed $0 or increased, and whether any Part B premium reduction ("giveback") shrank or ended for 2027. Several Florida plans reduced Part B givebacks for 2027, which directly changes your net Social Security check. See our Polk County Part B Giveback Guide.
2. Maximum Out-of-Pocket (MOOP) Compare your 2026 vs. 2027 in-network (and combined PPO) Maximum Out-of-Pocket cap. Your MOOP is your financial ceiling in a year with surgery, hospitalization, or Part B chemotherapy/infusions.
3. Provider & Hospital Directory Look for provider network notices affecting Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands, BayCare Winter Haven, or Bond Clinic. An ANOC booklet rarely lists every doctor that left the network—you must verify each physician NPI in the 2027 directory. See Does Watson Clinic Accept Humana in 2027?
4. Inpatient Hospital & Outpatient Surgery Copays Check days 1–5 or 1–7 daily hospital copays and ambulatory surgical center / outpatient hospital observation copays. A plan with a $0 monthly premium can still raise inpatient hospital copays by $75–$150 per day for 2027.
5. Part D Drug Deductible & Tier Structure Check if a new Part D drug deductible applies to Tiers 3, 4, and 5, or if Tier 3/4 switched from a flat copay to a percentage coinsurance. Even with the 2027 Part D out-of-pocket cap, shifting a brand drug from a $47 copay to 25% coinsurance changes your monthly pharmacy bill.
6. Dental, Vision, Hearing & OTC Allowances Compare quarterly OTC/flex card amounts, annual comprehensive dental maximums, and dental network rules. Many plans trimmed supplemental allowances or narrowed dental networks for 2027.

What Your ANOC Letter Does Not Show Automatically

The printed ANOC summary table only shows broad category changes (such as "Specialist copay: $35 in 2026 → $45 in 2027"). It does not print your personal list of doctors or your specific prescriptions inside the summary table.

  • Doctor & Clinic Contract Changes: A physician group like Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health Physician Group, or Bond Clinic can change contract status for 2027 while your ANOC simply states, "There are changes to our provider network for next year."
  • Formulary Drug Tier Shifts & Prior Authorizations: Your ANOC directs you to the separate 2027 Comprehensive Formulary (Drug List) to see if your blood thinner, inhaler, GLP-1/diabetes medication, or heart prescription moved to a higher tier or added prior authorization.

What to Do If Your Plan Sent a Non-Renewal Notice Instead of an ANOC

If your carrier is discontinuing your specific Medicare Advantage plan in Polk County for 2027, you receive a formal Plan Non-Renewal Notice rather than a standard ANOC. A plan termination triggers both your standard AEP window (through December 7) and a Special Enrollment Period—plus federal Guaranteed Issue rights to buy a Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plan G or Plan N) without medical underwriting. Read our dedicated guides on what to do when your Medicare Advantage plan is ending and switching to Medigap Plan G under Guaranteed Issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I receive my 2027 Medicare Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) letter?

CMS requires Medicare Advantage and Medicare Part D plans to send Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) packets by September 30 each year. If you did not receive yours in the mail or via your online member portal, contact your plan or request a local 2027 benefit comparison before December 7.

Does my 2027 ANOC letter tell me if my Watson Clinic or Lakeland Regional doctor is still in-network?

No. The ANOC booklet only notifies you that provider network changes exist for 2027 and directs you to the updated 2027 Provider Directory. You should verify each primary care doctor, specialist, and hospital by exact 2027 plan ID before December 7.

What is the deadline to change Medicare plans after reading my 2027 ANOC letter?

The Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026. Any Medicare Advantage or Part D plan change submitted by December 7 takes effect on January 1, 2027.

What if I miss something in my ANOC letter and realize in January that my plan changed?

If you are enrolled in a Medicare Advantage plan on January 1, the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (MA OEP) from January 1 through March 31 allows a one-time switch to another Medicare Advantage plan or back to Original Medicare with a Part D plan. However, checking before December 7 avoids January pharmacy or doctor disruptions.
