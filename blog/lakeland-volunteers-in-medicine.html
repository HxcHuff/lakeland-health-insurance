Short answer

LVIM's screening asks whether you live in Polk County, are uninsured, have household income below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level, and are working, recently unemployed, a full-time student, or live in a household where someone works. LVIM makes the final eligibility decision.

Does this clinic take my health plan?

Possibly. Networks are set plan by plan, so look up Lakeland Volunteers in Medicine in your plan's provider directory and call with your plan ID before your visit.

Clinic care vs. a Marketplace plan

No. Clinic discounts apply to that clinic's own services. They don't pay for hospital stays, emergency room visits, or outside specialists, and there is no out-of-pocket maximum like a Marketplace plan has.

Marketplace plans must cover essential health benefits including emergency services, hospitalization, pregnancy/maternity/newborn care, mental health and substance use disorder services, and pediatric services.

LVIM describes comprehensive health care for eligible patients.

Network status can differ by plan; check the plan's provider directory before enrolling. Use the exact plan ID and coverage year, then call the facility billing office and write down the date and the person you spoke with.

Use how much income qualifies for a subsidy, zero-premium plans in Florida to gather names, locations, and prescriptions before you compare plans.

Enrollment timing and Special Enrollment Periods

Open Enrollment for next year runs November 1 to January 15 on HealthCare.gov. Enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1. Outside that window you generally need a Special Enrollment Period, such as losing other coverage.

Losing other coverage (for example, Medicaid) may qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period, subject to Marketplace eligibility rules.

HealthCare.gov (Florida uses it): Open Enrollment starts November 1; enroll by December 15 for January 1 coverage; Open Enrollment ends January 15, with February 1 coverage for December 16 to January 15 enrollments.

What to verify before you enroll

  • Search Lakeland Volunteers in Medicine and each doctor by name and location in the plan directory
  • Confirm the exact plan ID and year with the billing office
  • Check prescriptions and pharmacy separately from the facility listing
  • Read urgent-care versus emergency-room cost sharing in the Summary of Benefits

Additional verified details

LVIM's eligibility screen: Polk County resident, uninsured, household income below 200% of the Federal Poverty Level, and working, recently unemployed, a full-time student, or in a household where someone works.

Lakeland Volunteers in Medicine cites 48,998 uninsured people in Polk County (about 7%).

After the temporary 2021–2025 enhancement, standard Premium Tax Credit rules apply; eligibility depends on household income and size.
