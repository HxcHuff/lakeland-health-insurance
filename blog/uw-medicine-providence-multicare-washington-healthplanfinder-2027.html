Navigating Puget Sound, Spokane & Southwest WA Networks in 2027

Across the Puget Sound corridor (King, Pierce, Snohomish, Thurston, and Kitsap counties), Eastern Washington (Spokane, Yakima, and the Tri-Cities), and Southwest Washington (Clark County / Vancouver), hospital and specialist access centers on five flagship systems: UW Medicine (operating UW Medical Center Montlake and Northwest campuses, Harborview Medical Center, Valley Medical Center in Renton, and Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center), Providence Swedish (Swedish First Hill, Cherry Hill, Ballard, Issaquah, and Edmonds; Providence Regional Medical Center Everett; Providence St. Peter in Olympia; Providence Sacred Heart Medical Center in Spokane; and Kadlec in Richland), MultiCare Health System (MultiCare Tacoma General, Mary Bridge Children's, Good Samaritan in Puyallup, Overlake affiliation, MultiCare Deaconess & Valley in Spokane, and MultiCare Yakima Memorial), Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (VMFH) (Virginia Mason Medical Center in Seattle and St. Joseph Medical Center in Tacoma), and Eastside/regional leaders including Overlake Medical Center, EvergreenHealth, Seattle Children's Hospital, and PeaceHealth / Legacy Salmon Creek in Vancouver.

On Washington Healthplanfinder (wahealthplanfinder.org, where Open Enrollment runs November 1 through January 15), Cascade Care and Cascade Select public-option plans standardize deductibles and copays, but every carrier builds a distinct county-locked EPO or HMO provider directory.

3 Steps for Washington Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Distinguish Washington's Four Blue Brands & Cascade Select Networks: Verify whether your county is served by Premera Blue Cross / LifeWise, Regence BlueShield, Asuris Northwest Health (Spokane/Eastern WA), or Regence BCBS of Oregon (Clark County), and check Cascade Select public option hospital lists carefully.
  2. Audit Fred Hutch, Seattle Children's & Optum Clinics (Everett Clinic / Polyclinic): Confirm 10-digit NPIs for Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center, Seattle Children's Hospital, and Optum Care Washington before locking in a 2027 plan.
  3. Check Clark County (Vancouver) to Portland, OR Access: If you live in Vancouver or Camas and cross the Columbia River to OHSU, Providence St. Vincent, or Legacy Emanuel in Portland, confirm bi-state network inclusion.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Harborview Medical Center (Washington's Level I Trauma Center), UW Medical Center, Swedish, MultiCare Tacoma General, Virginia Mason, and Providence Sacred Heart ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are UW Medicine, Providence Swedish, MultiCare, and Virginia Mason Franciscan Health in every 2027 Washington Healthplanfinder Cascade Care plan?

No. While Cascade Care and Cascade Select plans standardize deductibles and copays on Washington Healthplanfinder (wahealthplanfinder.org), Premera Blue Cross, Regence BlueShield, Kaiser Permanente Washington, Molina, Ambetter (Coordinated Care), CHPW, Bridgespan, and UnitedHealthcare build separate county-specific EPO/HMO networks.

How do I verify Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center (Fred Hutch) and UW Medicine for 2027?

Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center serves as the adult oncology program for UW Medicine, so patients should verify three separate directory listings: Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center (South Lake Union), UW Medical Center (Montlake and Northwest campuses), and UW Physicians (UWP) faculty specialists.

How do Providence Sacred Heart and MultiCare Deaconess compare on 2027 Spokane networks?

In Spokane County, Providence (Sacred Heart Medical Center, Holy Family Hospital, and Providence Medical Group) and MultiCare Inland Northwest (Deaconess Hospital, Valley Hospital, and Rockwood Clinic) contract separately across Asuris Northwest Health, Premera, Molina, Ambetter, Kaiser, and Medicare Advantage plans.
