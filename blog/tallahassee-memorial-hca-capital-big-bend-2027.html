Tallahassee & Big Bend Hospital Networks in 2027

Across Leon, Wakulla, Gadsden, Jefferson, Madison, and Taylor counties in Florida's Big Bend region, acute hospital and specialty care is anchored by two major facilities: 772-bed Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare (TMH) — including the Bixler Trauma & Emergency Center, the Tallahassee Memorial Cancer Center, and the M.T. Mustian Center — and 288-bed HCA Florida Capital Hospital on Capital Medical Boulevard (plus freestanding ERs in Southwood and Lake Jackson).

What makes the Tallahassee health insurance market unique in Florida is the central role of Capital Health Plan (CHP), an independent HMO affiliate of Florida Blue with dedicated health centers at Governors Square, Metropolitan Boulevard, and Centerville Road, alongside national Marketplace carriers like Ambetter from Sunshine Health and UnitedHealthcare. Because Cigna and Molina are exiting the Florida ACA Marketplace for 2027, verifying whether your plan covers TMH, HCA Florida Capital Hospital, or both is critical before Open Enrollment ends.

3 Steps for Leon, Wakulla & Gadsden County Residents

  1. Compare Capital Health Plan / Florida Blue vs. Ambetter & UHC Sub-Networks: In Leon and surrounding Big Bend counties, some lower-premium ACA HMO tiers emphasize HCA Florida Capital Hospital while restricting non-emergency inpatient admissions at Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare (or vice versa).
  2. Verify Independent Specialty Groups Separately: If you see specialists at Tallahassee Orthopedic Clinic (TOC), Southern Medical Group, or Tallahassee Primary Care Associates (TPCA), check each clinic's group NPI in addition to the hospital facility.
  3. Check Tertiary Referral Paths to Gainesville or Jacksonville: Big Bend patients who travel east on I-10 to UF Health Shands in Gainesville or Baptist MD Anderson / Mayo Clinic in Jacksonville should confirm out-of-county referral rules before enrolling in a narrow Leon County HMO.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare (including the Bixler Trauma Center and Northeast ER) and HCA Florida Capital Hospital ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare (TMH) and HCA Florida Capital Hospital?

In Leon, Wakulla, Gadsden, and Jefferson counties, select Florida Blue plans (including Capital Health Plan affiliated tiers, BlueOptions, and BlueCare), Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare sub-networks cover either Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare, HCA Florida Capital Hospital, or both depending on the exact plan ID.

How does Capital Health Plan (CHP) work with Tallahassee Memorial and HCA Florida Capital Hospital in 2027?

Capital Health Plan is a regional HMO affiliate of Florida Blue serving the Big Bend area; CHP members generally use CHP health centers for primary care while accessing contracted inpatient and specialty hospital services at Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare and HCA Florida Capital Hospital with appropriate referrals.

Do TMH Physician Partners and Tallahassee Primary Care Associates (TPCA) accept the same 2027 plans as the hospitals?

Not always. While TMH Physician Partners is employed by Tallahassee Memorial HealthCare, independent groups like Tallahassee Primary Care Associates (TPCA) and HCA Florida Capital Physicians contract under separate tax IDs that must be verified individually before enrolling.
