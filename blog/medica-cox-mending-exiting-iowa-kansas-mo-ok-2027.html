Three Regional 2027 ACA Carrier Exits Across IA, KS, MO & OK

Shoppers across Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, and Oklahoma must actively review their 2027 HealthCare.gov options as three regional insurers pull back on December 31, 2026:

  • Medica Exiting Iowa, Kansas & Oklahoma ACA: Medica is ending its individual ACA Marketplace coverage in Iowa, Kansas, and Oklahoma after 2026, requiring thousands of households across Des Moines, Wichita, Overland Park, Oklahoma City, and Tulsa to move to a new carrier.
  • Mending / Taro Health Shutting Down in Oklahoma: Mending (Taro Health) is shutting down its health insurance operations entirely for 2027, displacing Oklahoma exchange enrollees.
  • Cox HealthPlans Leaving HealthCare.gov in Southwest Missouri: Cox HealthPlans stopped direct on-exchange Marketplace plans across its 5-county Springfield/Southwest Missouri footprint (while maintaining select off-exchange individual plans). To keep CoxHealth hospitals accessible on HealthCare.gov, CoxHealth partnered with Oscar Health across an 8-county regional footprint—meaning displaced CoxHealth members who want subsidies must actively enroll in an exchange carrier like Oscar or another contracted insurer.

Watch Expansion vs. Non-Expansion Subsidy Cutoffs by State

Iowa, Missouri (MO HealthNet), and Oklahoma (SoonerCare) all maintain ACA Medicaid expansion up to 138% FPL, while Kansas has not adopted Medicaid expansion—meaning Kansas adults under 65 generally need projected 2027 MAGI between 100% and 400% FPL ($15,960–$63,840 single / $33,000–$132,000 family of four) to receive HealthCare.gov tax credits. All four states share the December 15, 2026 deadline for January 1 coverage (Open Enrollment closes January 15, 2027).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Medica leaving the ACA Marketplace in Iowa, Kansas, and Oklahoma for 2027?

Yes. Medica is ending its individual ACA Marketplace plans in Iowa, Kansas, and Oklahoma after December 31, 2026, requiring affected members to select a replacement insurer on HealthCare.gov.

What is happening with Cox HealthPlans in Missouri for 2027?

Cox HealthPlans discontinued direct on-exchange Marketplace plans on HealthCare.gov for its 5-county southwest Missouri area for 2027 (maintaining select off-exchange offerings) while partnering with Oscar Health on exchange plans across eight regional counties.

Why is Mending / Taro Health ending in Oklahoma for 2027?

Mending (Taro Health) announced it is shutting down its health insurance business completely for 2027, requiring Oklahoma exchange enrollees to pick a new HealthCare.gov insurer during Open Enrollment.

When is the deadline to replace Medica, Cox HealthPlans, or Mending for January 1 coverage?

Complete your new plan selection on HealthCare.gov by December 15, 2026 for uninterrupted January 1, 2027 coverage; enrollments between December 16 and January 15 generally begin February 1.
