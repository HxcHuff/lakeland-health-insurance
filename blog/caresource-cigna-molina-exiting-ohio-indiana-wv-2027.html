CareSource, Cigna & Molina Exiting OH, IN & WV ACA (2027)

Three major Marketplace carriers are exiting Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia individual ACA coverage on December 31, 2026: CareSource (OH, IN, WV), Cigna (OH, IN), and Molina Healthcare (OH). Here is how to replace your coverage by December 15 and protect your hospital network.

Which Individual ACA Carriers Are Leaving OH, IN & WV on December 31, 2026

Thousands of households across Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia must choose a new individual ACA carrier before 2027 begins:

  • CareSource Exiting Ohio, Indiana & West Virginia ACA: CareSource is terminating both its on-exchange HealthCare.gov Marketplace plans and off-exchange individual policies in Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia effective January 1, 2027 (while retaining Medicaid managed care in those states and keeping select ACA footprints such as Georgia).
  • Cigna Exiting Ohio & Indiana ACA: Cigna Healthcare is withdrawing completely from individual and family Marketplace coverage across Ohio and Indiana (part of its 11-state exit) after December 31, 2026.
  • Molina Healthcare Exiting Ohio ACA: As Molina slashes its individual Marketplace footprint from 14 states down to 6 for 2027, Ohio individual Marketplace members must actively transition to a remaining 2027 carrier.

Medicaid Expansion (138% FPL) vs. 400% FPL Marketplace Cutoff

Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia all maintain ACA Medicaid expansion for eligible adults ages 19–64 up to 138% FPL. Households above 138% FPL qualify for HealthCare.gov premium tax credits up to 400% FPL ($63,840 single / $132,000 family of four using 2026 HHS guidelines), with uncapped tax-time reconciliation if actual 2027 income exceeds your application estimate. Complete your carrier switch by December 15, 2026 for uninterrupted January 1 coverage.

4 Steps Before December 15 Across OH, IN & WV

  1. Do Not Rely on a Blind HealthCare.gov Crosswalk: When CareSource, Cigna, or Molina exits your county, an auto-assigned replacement insurer may exclude your current hospital system or tier your prescriptions much higher.
  2. Verify Remaining County Insurers by Exact Plan ID: Check Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, Ambetter, Medical Mutual (OH), Highmark BCBS (WV), Oscar, and UnitedHealthcare participation by exact county and 10-digit doctor NPI.
  3. Check Silver CSR vs. Gold Tier Value: If your household falls between 139% and 250% FPL, Silver Cost-Sharing Reductions can dramatically cut deductibles compared to an auto-assigned Bronze plan.
  4. Confirm Your First 2027 Binder Payment: Set up autopay or submit your initial binder payment directly to your new carrier before January 1 so coverage activates smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareSource leaving the Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia ACA Marketplace for 2027?

Yes. CareSource confirmed it is exiting individual ACA Marketplace and off-exchange individual plans in Ohio, Indiana, and West Virginia effective January 1, 2027, while continuing its Medicaid programs in those states.

Are Cigna and Molina also leaving Ohio and Indiana for 2027?

Yes. Cigna is exiting individual ACA coverage in both Ohio and Indiana (part of its 11-state exit), and Molina Healthcare is exiting Ohio's individual ACA Marketplace for 2027.

Does CareSource leaving the ACA Marketplace end CareSource Medicaid in Ohio or Indiana?

No. CareSource's 2027 exit applies to individual ACA Marketplace and off-exchange individual plans; state Medicaid managed care programs operate separately through each state Medicaid agency.

When is the deadline to switch out of CareSource, Cigna, or Molina for January 1, 2027?

Complete your new HealthCare.gov plan selection by December 15, 2026 for uninterrupted January 1, 2027 coverage. Open Enrollment runs through January 15, 2027, with enrollments after December 15 taking effect February 1.
