Navigating Tampa Bay's Four Major Hospital Networks in 2027

Across Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, and Polk counties, hospital and specialist access is divided among four primary health systems: BayCare Health System (16 hospitals including St. Joseph's in Tampa, Morton Plant in Clearwater, St. Anthony's in St. Petersburg, and South Florida Baptist in Plant City), Tampa General Hospital (TGH) & USF Health, AdventHealth West Florida Division, and HCA Florida Healthcare.

With Cigna and Molina exiting the Florida ACA Marketplace for 2027, thousands of Tampa Bay policyholders must select a replacement plan on HealthCare.gov while verifying that their primary care doctors, USF Health academic specialists, and local hospitals remain in-network.

3 Steps for Tampa Bay Families Before December 15

  1. Verify Both TGH Facility and USF Health Doctors: Many specialist clinics at Tampa General Hospital are staffed by USF Health Morsani College of Medicine faculty who bill under a separate tax ID from the hospital.
  2. Check BayCare Medical Group (BMG) PCPs: If your family uses BayCare Medical Group or BayCare Urgent Care in Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, or Clearwater, verify BMG participation on your 2027 plan tier.
  3. Coordinate Oncology & Pediatric Access: Families using St. Joseph's Children's Hospital, Johns Hopkins All Children's Hospital in St. Petersburg, or Moffitt Cancer Center should verify tertiary referral rules before enrolling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover both BayCare hospitals and Tampa General Hospital?

Select Florida Blue plans (such as BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific Oscar Health and Ambetter network tiers include participating access to BayCare facilities and Tampa General Hospital, though USF Health faculty physician billing must be verified separately.

Are St. Joseph's Hospital in Tampa and Morton Plant Hospital in Clearwater in the same network?

Yes. St. Joseph's Hospital, Morton Plant Hospital, Mease Countryside, St. Anthony's Hospital, South Florida Baptist Hospital, and Winter Haven Hospital are all operated by BayCare Health System and share system-wide hospital contracts.

Are emergency visits at Tampa General Hospital Level 1 Trauma Center covered by all ACA plans?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at Tampa General Hospital and all BayCare emergency rooms must be billed at in-network cost-sharing rates.
