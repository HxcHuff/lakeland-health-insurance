The Two-Stage HonorHealth & AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Network Split

HonorHealth has published an official patient alert explaining a two-stage exit from the Blue Cross Blue Shield of Arizona (AZ Blue) Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage network across Scottsdale, Phoenix, and the East Valley:

  • Stage 1 — Effective July 1, 2026: AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage removed HonorHealth primary care providers (PCPs) from its in-network directory.
  • Stage 2 — Effective January 1, 2027: HonorHealth—including all employed physicians, specialists, outpatient centers, and hospitals—will no longer participate in the AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage network. Specialists, hospitals, and outpatient centers remain in network only through December 31, 2026.

Important scope clarification: HonorHealth states this termination applies only to AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage plans. HonorHealth continues to be in network with all other contracted Blue Cross Blue Shield of Arizona commercial and Medicare plans, as well as Original Medicare (Parts A & B) with or without a Medigap policy.

  • East Valley / Mesa & Fountain Hills: HonorHealth Mountain Vista Medical Center, HonorHealth Four Peaks Medical Center, and HonorHealth Medical Group primary and specialty clinics

Other Arizona Facility Carve-Outs Catching Patients in 2027

  • Northern Arizona Healthcare (Flagstaff & Verde Valley): While NAH primary care clinics accept Medicare and most commercial plans, NAH warns patients that its Urgent Care locations are out of network for Blue Cross Blue Shield, Banner, and MultiPlan policies.
  • Mayo Clinic Arizona (Phoenix & Scottsdale): Excluded from most narrow Maricopa County Medicare Advantage HMOs and ACA Marketplace HMOs—requiring Original Medicare + Medigap or verified contracted plans for scheduled care.

4 Steps for HonorHealth Patients Before Enrollment Windows Close

  1. Check Your Exact AZ Blue Card & ANOC Letter: Confirm whether your card specifically says AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) versus another Blue Cross Blue Shield of Arizona plan, and review your Annual Notice of Change (ANOC).
  2. Cross-Check Both HonorHealth & Banner Health If You Use Both Systems: Many Maricopa County Medicare Advantage HMOs align tightly with either Banner Health or HonorHealth/Dignity. If your household sees specialists across both systems, verify broad-network PPO or multi-system options by NPI.
  3. Use Medicare AEP (Oct 15 – Dec 7) or MA OEP (Jan 1 – Mar 31): Switching during Medicare Annual Enrollment locks in an in-network plan for January 1, 2027; beneficiaries already in a Medicare Advantage plan also have one switch window between January 1 and March 31.
  4. Request Continuity of Care for Active Complex Treatment: Patients in active oncology, post-surgical recovery, or serious chronic care can call the member services number on their card to ask about transitional Continuity of Care authorization.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does HonorHealth leave the AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage network?

Following AZ Blue's removal of HonorHealth primary care providers on July 1, 2026, all HonorHealth employed physicians, specialists, outpatient centers, and hospitals will no longer be in network with AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage plans effective January 1, 2027.

Does HonorHealth still accept other Blue Cross Blue Shield of Arizona plans in 2027?

Yes. HonorHealth confirms this termination applies only to AZ Blue Best Life (HMO) Medicare Advantage plans. HonorHealth continues to participate in network with all other contracted Blue Cross Blue Shield of Arizona plans, as well as Original Medicare with or without a Medigap supplement.

What other Arizona clinic or urgent care network exclusions catch patients off guard?

In Northern Arizona (Flagstaff and Verde Valley), Northern Arizona Healthcare (NAH) Urgent Care locations are out of network for Blue Cross Blue Shield, Banner, and MultiPlan members even though NAH primary care clinics accept Medicare and most commercial plans, and Mayo Clinic Arizona is excluded from most narrow Phoenix ACA and Medicare Advantage HMOs.

Are HonorHealth emergency departments still covered if my plan is out of network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency room evaluation and stabilization at all HonorHealth emergency departments are covered at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of network contract status.
