Aetna Exiting Maryland Medicare Advantage Statewide (2027): SEP, Medigap GI & Hospital Guide

Aetna is exiting the individual Medicare Advantage market statewide across Maryland effective December 31, 2026, with zero individual Aetna Medicare Advantage plans listed in 2027 CMS Maryland landscape files. Exact 2027 Deadlines & Effective Dates (Do Not Miss These)

Because Aetna is discontinuing all individual Medicare Advantage plans across Maryland effective December 31, 2026, displaced Maryland seniors have three linked federal timelines (plus Maryland's own state Medigap birthday-rule protection once enrolled in Medigap):

  • Annual Election Period (AEP, October 15 to December 7, 2026): Enroll in your 2027 replacement coverage by December 7 for seamless January 1, 2027 effective dates and new ID cards before the new year starts.
  • Federal Non-Renewal Special Enrollment Period (December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027): If your 2026 plan is terminated and you miss December 7, your official non-renewal notice keeps a federal SEP open through February 28, 2027:
    • Enrolled December 8 to December 31, 2026: effective January 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled January 1 to January 31, 2027: effective February 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled February 1 to February 28, 2027: effective March 1, 2027.
  • 63-Day Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window (November 2, 2026 through roughly March 4, 2027): Displaced members returning to Original Medicare may purchase an eligible standardized Medigap plan (Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L, plus Plans C or F if eligible prior to January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting, paired with a standalone 2027 Part D drug plan (featuring the $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 defined-standard deductible). Note: enrolling in another Medicare Advantage plan forfeits this specific Medigap Guaranteed Issue right.

Fallback exposure if you take no action: Reverting automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 removes Part D prescription drug coverage and leaves you responsible for the 2027 Original Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible of $1,776 per benefit period and 20% Part B coinsurance with no annual out-of-pocket cap.

What Happens to Johns Hopkins, MedStar, UMMC & CareFirst in 2027?

Johns Hopkins Medicine, MedStar Health, University of Maryland Medical System (UMMS / UMMC), LifeBridge Health, and Luminis Health hospitals remain fully open. In addition, Johns Hopkins Advantage MD continues offering Medicare Advantage plans in Maryland for 2027, and CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield remains active with restructured 2027 options including Advantage Signature PPO and Advantage DualPrime.

Because Aetna's statewide exit forces every individual Aetna MA member in Maryland onto a new contract, verify each doctor, cancer center, and hospital campus by exact 2027 Plan ID before enrolling, or evaluate Original Medicare plus a 63-day Guaranteed Issue Medigap Plan G (Maryland also provides an annual 30-day Medigap birthday rule allowing existing Medigap policyholders to switch to an equal or lesser plan without underwriting).

Is Aetna leaving Maryland Medicare Advantage completely in 2027?

Yes. CMS 2027 landscape files show zero individual Aetna Medicare Advantage plans in Maryland for 2027. Existing individual Aetna Medicare Advantage coverage in Maryland terminates December 31, 2026.

Are Johns Hopkins Advantage MD and CareFirst still operating in Maryland for 2027?

Yes. Johns Hopkins Advantage MD continues operating in Maryland for 2027, and CareFirst remains active with restructured options such as Advantage Signature PPO and Advantage DualPrime while retiring select legacy plan IDs.

Can displaced Maryland Aetna members get Medigap Plan G without health questions?

Yes. What happens if I take no action before January 1, 2027?
