Navigating Valley of the Sun & Tucson Hospital Networks in 2027

Across Maricopa, Pinal, and Pima counties, hospital and specialist access is divided among five major systems: Banner Health (including Banner – University Medical Center Phoenix, Banner – University Medical Center Tucson & South, Banner Desert & Cardon Children's in Mesa, Banner Thunderbird in Glendale, Banner Estrella, Banner Boswell & Del E. Webb in Sun City, and Banner MD Anderson Cancer Center in Gilbert), HonorHealth (Scottsdale Osborn, Scottsdale Shea, Scottsdale Thompson Peak, Deer Valley, John C. Lincoln, Sonoran Crossing, and Four Peaks), Dignity Health Arizona (St. Joseph's Hospital and Medical Center / Barrow Neurological Institute, Chandler Regional, and Mercy Gilbert), Mayo Clinic Arizona (Phoenix Hospital & Scottsdale Clinic), and TMC Health (Tucson Medical Center).

On the Arizona ACA Marketplace, insurers frequently offer system-branded or narrow HMO tiers that include Banner Health while excluding HonorHealth (or vice versa), making pre-enrollment verification essential.

3 Steps for Maricopa, Pinal & Pima County Residents

  1. Check the Sub-Network Name on BCBS of Arizona, Oscar & Ambetter Plans: In Maricopa County, a single carrier often sells multiple plans side by side — one built around Banner Health and another built around HonorHealth or Dignity Health.
  2. Verify Sun City, Surprise & East Valley Oncology Access: If you use Banner MD Anderson Cancer Center in Gilbert, Barrow Neurological Institute at Dignity Health St. Joseph's, or Mayo Clinic Arizona, confirm tertiary specialty coverage before December 15.
  3. Compare Pima County (Tucson) Networks Separately: Tucson plans use Pima County rating area networks centered on Banner – University Medical Center Tucson, Tucson Medical Center (TMC), and Carondelet St. Mary's / St. Joseph's.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Banner Health, HonorHealth, Dignity Health, Abrazo, TMC, and Mayo Clinic Hospital emergency rooms are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency inpatient admissions, scheduled procedures, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Phoenix ACA Marketplace plans cover both Banner Health and HonorHealth?

Many Maricopa County ACA Marketplace plans structure system-specific HMOs around either Banner Health, HonorHealth, Dignity Health, or Abrazo, so Scottsdale, Phoenix, Mesa, and Glendale residents must verify their exact sub-network name before enrolling.

Does Mayo Clinic Hospital in Phoenix accept Arizona ACA Marketplace or Medicare Advantage plans?

Mayo Clinic Arizona in Phoenix and Scottsdale is out-of-network on most narrow Arizona ACA Marketplace HMOs and many Medicare Advantage plans; Original Medicare paired with a Medigap policy or verified contracted plans should be confirmed directly prior to scheduling care.

Which hospital systems anchor Tucson and Pima County 2027 ACA and Medicare networks?

In Tucson and Pima County, hospital networks center on Banner - University Medical Center Tucson and South, TMC Health (Tucson Medical Center), and Northwest Healthcare / Carondelet Health Network, which contract separately across Pima County rating area plans.
