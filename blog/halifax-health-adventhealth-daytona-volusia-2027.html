Volusia & Flagler County Hospital Networks in 2027

Across Volusia and Flagler counties — from Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach, Port Orange, and New Smyrna Beach on the coast to Deltona, Orange City, and DeLand along the I-4 corridor — hospital and specialist care splits between two major systems: public tertiary center Halifax Health (operating the 678-bed Halifax Health Medical Center of Daytona Beach with the area's only Level II Trauma Center, Halifax Health Medical Center of Port Orange, and Halifax Health | UF Health Medical Center of Deltona) and AdventHealth East Florida (operating AdventHealth Daytona Beach, AdventHealth Fish Memorial in Orange City, AdventHealth DeLand, AdventHealth New Smyrna Beach, and AdventHealth Palm Coast & Palm Coast Parkway).

With Cigna and Molina exiting the Florida ACA Marketplace in 2027 and narrow HMO networks reshaping Volusia County plans, checking whether your policy covers Halifax Health, AdventHealth, or both is essential before enrolling.

3 Steps for Daytona Beach, Deltona & Palm Coast Residents

  1. Check West Volusia (Deltona / Orange City / DeLand) Sub-Networks: In Deltona and Orange City, Halifax Health | UF Health Medical Center of Deltona and AdventHealth Fish Memorial sit just minutes apart along I-4, yet narrow ACA HMO plans frequently cover only one of the two campuses for scheduled surgery.
  2. Verify Seminole & Orange County Commuter Rules: If you live in Deltona or DeBary (32725 / 32738 / 32713) and commute down I-4 into Sanford, Lake Mary, or Orlando for care at AdventHealth Orlando or Orlando Health, confirm cross-county HMO referral rules before enrolling.
  3. Confirm Oncology & Trauma Follow-Up: Halifax Health operates the Charles L. and Miki N. Grant Cancer Center for Hope in Daytona Beach, while AdventHealth Daytona Beach operates a comprehensive cancer institute — verify which oncology infusion center is in-network for your 2027 plan.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Halifax Health Daytona Beach (including Level II Trauma services), Port Orange, Deltona, and all AdventHealth East Florida ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency inpatient admissions, scheduled surgeries, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Volusia County ACA Marketplace plans cover both Halifax Health and AdventHealth Daytona Beach?

Some Florida Blue, Oscar Health, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare sub-networks in Volusia and Flagler counties contract with AdventHealth East Florida hospitals while excluding Halifax Health for non-emergency care (or vice versa), so enrollees must check their exact 2027 plan ID before enrolling.

Is Halifax Health | UF Health Medical Center of Deltona in the same network as Halifax Health Daytona Beach?

Halifax Health Medical Center of Daytona Beach, Halifax Health Medical Center of Port Orange, and Halifax Health | UF Health Medical Center of Deltona operate within the Halifax Health system, though UF Health faculty specialists practicing in Deltona should be verified by individual NPI.

Which AdventHealth campuses serve Volusia and Flagler County Medicare and ACA patients?

AdventHealth's East Florida Division includes AdventHealth Daytona Beach, AdventHealth New Smyrna Beach, AdventHealth Fish Memorial in Orange City, AdventHealth DeLand, and AdventHealth Palm Coast and Palm Coast Parkway in Flagler County.
