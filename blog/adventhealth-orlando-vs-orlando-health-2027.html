Central Florida's Two Healthcare Giants in 2027

Across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, and Lake counties — from Orlando, Winter Park, Apopka, and Lake Nona to Sanford, Oviedo, Kissimmee, Celebration, and Clermont — hospital care is anchored by two integrated systems: AdventHealth Central Florida Division (including AdventHealth Orlando on Rollins Street, AdventHealth for Children, Winter Park, Altamonte, Celebration, Apopka, East Orlando, Kissimmee, and Waterman in Tavares) and Orlando Health (including Orlando Regional Medical Center / ORMC Level 1 Trauma Center, Winnie Palmer Hospital for Women & Babies, Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children, Dr. P. Phillips Hospital, Horizon West, Lake Mary, South Seminole, and South Lake Hospital in Clermont).

In addition, Lake Nona's Medical City houses Nemours Children's Hospital and the Orlando VA Medical Center, while HCA Florida Osceola Hospital and HCA Florida Lake Monroe Hospital serve Kissimmee and Sanford. Selecting the right 2027 ACA or Medicare plan requires checking whether your carrier tier covers one or both major Orlando health systems.

3 Steps for Orange, Seminole, Osceola & Lake Families

  1. Verify Maternity & Pediatric Hospital Alignment: Expectant parents planning to deliver at Orlando Health Winnie Palmer Hospital or AdventHealth for Women should confirm both their OB/GYN group (such as Women's Care Florida or Orlando Health Physician Associates) and the hospital facility on their 2027 plan ID.
  2. Check Cross-County Commuter Rules (Four Corners, Clermont & Horizon West): Families living along US-27 in Clermont (Lake County), Four Corners (Polk/Osceola), or Horizon West (Orange County) frequently cross county lines for primary and urgent care. Make sure your ACA HMO covers providers across adjacent Central Florida counties.
  3. Confirm Physician Group Tax IDs: Both AdventHealth Medical Group and Orlando Health Medical Group employ hundreds of specialists whose outpatient clinic billing must match your 2027 sub-network.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at AdventHealth Orlando, Orlando Health ORMC, Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children, and HCA Florida ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of hospital network status; network tiers govern non-emergency admissions, scheduled surgery, and outpatient clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Central Florida ACA Marketplace plans cover both AdventHealth and Orlando Health?

Select Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter sub-networks in Orange, Seminole, Osceola, and Lake counties include both AdventHealth and Orlando Health, while narrow-network HMO tiers often align primarily with one hospital system.

Are Winnie Palmer Hospital and Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children part of the Orlando Health network?

Yes. Orlando Health Winnie Palmer Hospital for Women & Babies, Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children, Orlando Regional Medical Center (ORMC), Dr. P. Phillips Hospital, Horizon West Hospital, and Lake Mary Hospital are all operated by Orlando Health.

Does AdventHealth Centra Care Urgent Care accept the same plans as AdventHealth Orlando?

AdventHealth Centra Care locations across Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, and Polk counties generally participate in the same ACA Marketplace and Medicare plans as AdventHealth hospitals, though copays vary by urgent care benefit tier.
