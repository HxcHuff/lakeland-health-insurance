Exact 2027 Deadlines & Effective Dates (Do Not Miss These)

Because roughly 55 Michigan Medicare Advantage plans are retiring or leaving select counties on December 31, 2026, Michigan seniors who received a non-renewal notice have three linked federal enrollment windows:

  • Annual Election Period (AEP, October 15 to December 7, 2026): Enroll in your 2027 replacement coverage by December 7 for seamless January 1, 2027 effective dates and new ID cards before the new year starts.
  • Federal Non-Renewal Special Enrollment Period (December 8, 2026 to February 28, 2027): If your 2026 plan is terminated and you miss December 7, your official non-renewal notice keeps a federal SEP open through February 28, 2027:
    • Enrolled December 8 to December 31, 2026: effective January 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled January 1 to January 31, 2027: effective February 1, 2027.
    • Enrolled February 1 to February 28, 2027: effective March 1, 2027.
  • 63-Day Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window (November 2, 2026 through roughly March 4, 2027): Displaced members returning to Original Medicare may purchase an eligible standardized Medigap plan (Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L if first eligible for Medicare before January 1, 2020, or Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L (if first eligible before 2020) or Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L (if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) without medical underwriting, paired with a standalone 2027 Part D drug plan (featuring the $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap and up to a $700 defined-standard deductible). Fallback exposure if you take no action: Reverting automatically to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027 removes Part D prescription drug coverage and leaves you responsible for the 2027 Original Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible of $1,776 per benefit period and 20% Part B coinsurance with no annual out-of-pocket cap.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Michigan Medicare Advantage plans are exiting or retiring for 2027?

Roughly 55 Medicare Advantage plans active in Michigan during 2026 are leaving specific counties or retiring completely effective December 31, 2026, including localized Priority Health plan retirements (such as PriorityMedicare Vital in Kent County and Northern Michigan adjustments) plus national Humana and UnitedHealthcare trims.

Are BCBSM, Priority Health, HAP, or Trinity Health leaving Michigan completely?

No. Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan (BCBSM/BCN), Priority Health, HAP, and Trinity Health Plan of Michigan all continue operating in Michigan for 2027, and Devoted Health is entering the Michigan market. Disruptions are concentrated in specific retired Plan IDs and county service-area adjustments.

Do displaced Michigan members get a Special Enrollment Period and 63-day Medigap GI right?

Yes. If your exact 2026 Michigan Medicare Advantage plan is non-renewed, you qualify for a federal Non-Renewal SEP running December 8, 2026 through February 28, 2027, plus a 63-day Medigap Guaranteed Issue window (roughly November 2, 2026 through March 4, 2027) to buy Plans A, B, C, F, K, or L (if first eligible before 2020) or Plans A, B, D, G, K, or L (if newly eligible on or after January 1, 2020) without health questions. Enrolling in another MA plan forfeits that specific GI right.

What happens if my Michigan plan terminates and I do nothing?

Taking no action after a full plan termination reverts you to Original Medicare Parts A and B on January 1, 2027 with no Part D drug coverage, no annual out-of-pocket cap, the $1,776 Part A deductible per benefit period, and 20% Part B coinsurance.
