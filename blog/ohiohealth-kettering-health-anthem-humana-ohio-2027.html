Central and Southwest Ohio Hospital Network Splits

Across Central and Southwest Ohio, patients rely on major regional health systems that frequently negotiate separate contracts with Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield, Humana, Medical Mutual of Ohio, CareSource, Ambetter (Buckeye Health Plan), and Molina:

  • Central Ohio (Columbus / Franklin, Delaware, Licking, Fairfield Counties): Care is split between OhioHealth (Riverside Methodist Hospital, Grant Medical Center, Dublin Methodist Hospital), The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center & The James Cancer Hospital, and Mount Carmel Health System.
  • Southwest Ohio / Miami Valley (Dayton / Montgomery, Greene, Butler, Warren Counties): Care is divided between Kettering Health (Kettering Health Main Campus, Soin Medical Center, Sycamore) and Premier Health (Miami Valley Hospital, Atrium Medical Center).

Action Checklist for Ohio Open Enrollment

  1. Check Physician Group Tax IDs: Confirm whether your doctor bills through OhioHealth Physician Group, The Ohio State University Physicians, or Kettering Physician Network.
  2. Verify Emergency Protections: Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency evaluation and stabilization at Grant Medical Center, Riverside Methodist, or Kettering Health Main Campus ERs must be covered at in-network cost-sharing rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are OhioHealth hospitals in Columbus in-network for 2027 HealthCare.gov Marketplace plans?

OhioHealth facilities, including Riverside Methodist Hospital and Grant Medical Center, participate in select Central Ohio ACA Marketplace networks, though carrier participation differs from Mount Carmel and OSU Wexner Medical Center plans.

How do Kettering Health and Premier Health networks differ for Dayton 2027 health plans?

In the Dayton and Miami Valley region, individual exchange and Medicare Advantage plans frequently contract with either Kettering Health or Premier Health (Miami Valley Hospital), making provider directory checks essential during Open Enrollment.

Does the No Surprises Act protect emergency room visits at OhioHealth and Kettering Health?

Yes. Federal law requires insurers to cover emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency department, including OhioHealth Grant Medical Center and Kettering Health Main Campus, at in-network cost-sharing rates.
