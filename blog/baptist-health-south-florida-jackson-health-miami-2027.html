Miami-Dade & Monroe County Hospital Networks in 2027

In Miami-Dade and Monroe counties, hospital and specialty care is anchored by four flagship systems: Baptist Health South Florida (including Baptist Hospital of Miami on Kendall Drive, Miami Cancer Institute, South Miami Hospital, Doctors Hospital in Coral Gables, West Kendall Baptist, Homestead Hospital, and Mariners & Fishermen's Community Hospitals in the Florida Keys), Jackson Health System (Jackson Memorial Hospital, Holtz Children's Hospital, Jackson North, Jackson South, and Jackson West), UHealth – University of Miami Health System (Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center and Bascom Palmer Eye Institute), and Mount Sinai Medical Center in Miami Beach.

Miami-Dade is the largest ACA Marketplace enrollment county in the United States, and insurers offer dozens of HMO tiers with sharply different hospital contracts. Choosing the wrong sub-network can exclude Baptist Health or UHealth specialists on January 1, 2027.

3 Steps for Miami-Dade & Keys Residents Before December 15

  1. Separate Jackson Memorial Hospital from UHealth Faculty Billing: Even when Jackson Memorial Hospital is in-network, University of Miami faculty physicians practicing at the Medical Campus bill under UHealth tax IDs. Confirm both entities before enrolling.
  2. Verify Oncology & Ophthalmology Access: If you require access to Bascom Palmer Eye Institute, Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, or Baptist Health's Miami Cancer Institute, verify tertiary specialty inclusion rather than relying on primary care clinic directories.
  3. Check Monroe County (Florida Keys) Referral Rules: Residents in Key Largo, Islamorada, Marathon, and Key West often rely on Mariners Hospital, Fishermen's Community Hospital, or Lower Keys Medical Center locally while traveling to Baptist Hospital of Miami for specialized surgery. Ensure your plan covers both Monroe and Miami-Dade counties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover Baptist Health South Florida hospitals in Miami-Dade and Monroe?

Select Florida Blue plans (such as BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific Oscar Health and Ambetter sub-networks include Baptist Hospital of Miami, South Miami Hospital, Doctors Hospital, Homestead Hospital, and Mariners and Fishermen's hospitals in the Florida Keys, though ultra-narrow Miami-Dade HMOs must be checked carefully.

Are Jackson Memorial Hospital and UHealth (University of Miami Health System) in the same network?

Not always. While University of Miami Miller School of Medicine faculty staff many specialty services at Jackson Memorial Hospital, UHealth Tower, Sylvester Comprehensive Cancer Center, and Bascom Palmer Eye Institute often contract separately from the Public Health Trust (Jackson Health System).

Are Baptist Health Boca Raton Regional and Bethesda Hospital in Palm Beach County in the same system?

Yes. Boca Raton Regional Hospital and Bethesda Hospital East and West in Boynton Beach are part of Baptist Health South Florida, though ACA HMO plans in Miami-Dade County may restrict non-emergency care in Palm Beach County unless a multi-county network tier is selected.
