Navigating New Orleans, Baton Rouge, Lafayette & Shreveport Networks in 2027

Across Louisiana's major metro areas, inpatient and specialty care is anchored by four dominant hospital systems: Ochsner Health (headquartered at Ochsner Medical Center on Jefferson Highway, with campuses across Baptist, West Bank, Kenner, St. Tammany Health System partnerships on the Northshore, Ochsner Baton Rouge, Ochsner Lafayette General, and Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport/Monroe), LCMC Health in Greater New Orleans (operating University Medical Center New Orleans, East Jefferson General Hospital, West Jefferson Medical Center, Touro, Children's Hospital New Orleans, and Lakeview Hospital in Covington), Franciscan Missionaries of Our Lady Health System (FMOLHS) (operating Our Lady of the Lake Regional Medical Center in Baton Rouge, Our Lady of Lourdes in Lafayette, St. Dominic's, and St. Francis in Monroe), and Willis-Knighton Health System in Shreveport and Bossier City.

On the Louisiana ACA Marketplace, carriers frequently structure parish-specific narrow networks — such as BCBS of Louisiana's system-aligned tiers (Blue Connect, Community Blue, Precision Blue) alongside Ambetter (Louisiana Healthcare Connections) and UnitedHealthcare — making hospital verification essential before December 15.

3 Steps for Louisiana Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Distinguish Blue Connect vs. Community Blue vs. Precision Blue: In Greater New Orleans and Baton Rouge, Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Louisiana designs distinct narrow networks around either Ochsner Health/St. Tammany or LCMC Health/FMOLHS/Baton Rouge General.
  2. Check Northshore to Southshore Commuter Coverage: If you live in St. Tammany Parish (Covington, Mandeville, Slidell) and cross the Causeway or I-10 Twin Span for specialty care at Ochsner Jefferson Highway, Touro, or Children's Hospital New Orleans, confirm cross-parish referral rules.
  3. Verify Shreveport & Lake Charles Regional Splits: In Caddo/Bossier parishes (Willis-Knighton vs. Ochsner LSU) and Calcasieu Parish (Lake Charles Memorial vs. CHRISTUS Ochsner Southwestern Louisiana), check both hospital facility and physician clinic NPIs.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Ochsner Medical Center, University Medical Center New Orleans, East Jefferson, Our Lady of the Lake, Baton Rouge General, and Willis-Knighton ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Louisiana ACA Marketplace plans cover both Ochsner Health and LCMC Health in Greater New Orleans?

Many Louisiana HealthCare.gov HMO and POS plans (including Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Louisiana Blue Connect, Community Blue, Vantage, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare sub-networks) align with specific hospital systems in Orleans, Jefferson, and St. Tammany parishes, so enrollees must check whether Ochsner Health, LCMC Health, or both participate.

Which hospital systems anchor Baton Rouge and Lafayette 2027 ACA and Medicare networks?

In Baton Rouge and Acadiana, hospital networks center on Franciscan Missionaries of Our Lady Health System (Our Lady of the Lake in Baton Rouge and Our Lady of Lourdes in Lafayette), Baton Rouge General Medical Center, and Ochsner Baton Rouge / Ochsner Lafayette General.

How do Shreveport and Bossier City residents verify Willis-Knighton and Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport?

In Northwest Louisiana (Caddo and Bossier parishes), Willis-Knighton Health System and Ochsner LSU Health Shreveport contract separately across ACA Marketplace and Medicare Advantage plans, so patients should verify both hospital facility and physician clinic NPIs.
