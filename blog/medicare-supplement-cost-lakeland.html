What Does a Medicare Supplement Cost in Lakeland?

The current quote matters more than an internet price range. Compare the same standardized plan letter, rating method, underwriting rules, discounts, and effective date.

Published April 30, 2026 · Updated August 12, 2026 · By David Huff, Licensed FL Agent
Short answer: There is no reliable single Lakeland or Polk County price range that applies to every applicant. Obtain a current carrier-specific quote for the applicant's ZIP code, age, tobacco status, household discounts, eligibility, and requested effective date.

Why a current quote is required

Medicare.gov explains that Medigap premiums can vary by policy, location, and other factors. Insurance companies can charge different premiums for the same standardized plan letter, and the rating method can affect how premiums change over time.

  • Plan letter: compare the same standardized benefits.
  • Rating method: identify whether the policy is community-rated, issue-age-rated, or attained-age-rated.
  • Applicant and location factors: use the information requested in the current approved quote process.
  • Discounts and household rules: verify eligibility and whether a discount can change later.
  • Underwriting and enrollment rights: confirm whether medical underwriting applies.
  • Effective date: verify the rate and policy form for the date coverage is requested.

2026 high-deductible Plan G amount

Medicare.gov lists the high-deductible amount for Plans F and G as $2,950 in 2026. A high-deductible policy does not begin paying until the policyholder has paid Medicare-covered costs up to that amount, subject to the policy terms. Availability and eligibility must be verified.

Enrollment timing and underwriting

Federal Medigap Open Enrollment is a one-time six-month period that starts the first month a person has Medicare Part B and is age 65 or older. During that period, an insurance company cannot use medical underwriting to deny a Medigap application because of pre-existing health problems.

Outside that period, an insurance company may deny an application or offer fewer choices unless a guaranteed-issue right or another applicable protection exists. Do not cancel existing coverage until the new policy is issued and the effective date is confirmed.

Medigap and prescription coverage are separate decisions

Modern Medigap policies do not include outpatient prescription drug coverage. Review a separate Part D plan using current Medicare Plan Compare data and the applicant's prescriptions and pharmacy. Plan premiums, formularies, pharmacy networks, and cost sharing can change.

A controlled Lakeland comparison

  1. Confirm Original Medicare eligibility and the requested coverage date.
  2. Determine whether Medigap Open Enrollment or a guaranteed-issue right applies.
  3. Select the standardized plan letter before comparing insurance companies.
  4. Run official quotes using the same applicant information and effective date.
  5. Compare premium, rating method, discounts, underwriting, service, and policy documents.
  6. Review a separate Part D plan and verify prescriptions and pharmacy.
