The 2027 Carrier Footprint Shift in Florida

The Affordable Care Act (ACA) Individual Marketplace in Florida is undergoing substantial restructuring for the 2027 plan year. Two prominent health insurers have announced their exit from the exchange:

  • Cigna Healthcare: Exiting the ACA individual marketplace in multiple states, concluding all Cigna Connect products across Florida at the end of 2026.
  • Molina Healthcare of Florida: Discontinuing individual marketplace products in Florida for 2027.

If you currently receive coverage through a Cigna or Molina policy purchased on HealthCare.gov, your current plan will terminate on December 31, 2026. You must transition to another carrier to maintain health benefits starting January 1, 2027.

The HealthCare.gov Auto-Renewal Trap: Why You Must Act Before December 15

Federal regulations stipulate that when an issuer exits an exchange service area, HealthCare.gov automatically maps affected policyholders into an alternative available plan if the consumer takes no action by December 15, 2026.

While automatic enrollment prevents an immediate gap in insurance status, the algorithm chooses replacement coverage based on premium tier and broad category matching—not by analyzing your specific medical records. Auto-assigned plans frequently present major obstacles:

  • Lost Physician Contracts: The assigned carrier may not contract with your primary physician at Watson Clinic, Bond Clinic, or Lakeland Regional Health.
  • Formulary Changes: Maintenance medications may shift to higher copay tiers or require new prior authorization approvals.
  • Hospital Facility Gaps: The auto-mapped plan may omit your preferred regional hospital in favor of a narrower facility network.

Action Checklist for Impacted Cigna and Molina Policyholders

  1. Collect Your Care Roster: Compile the names, addresses, and NPI numbers of every physician, specialist, and clinic your family utilizes.
  2. Audit Prescription Lists: Note exact medication names, daily dosages, and refill frequencies.
  3. Verify 2027 Income Projections: Update your expected 2027 Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) on HealthCare.gov to ensure accurate advance premium tax credits (APTC).
  4. Submit Choice Before December 15: Finalize your new plan selection on or before December 15, 2026, to guarantee coverage begins smoothly on January 1, 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I do not select a new ACA plan before Cigna or Molina leaves?

If you take no action by December 15, 2026, HealthCare.gov will automatically assign you to an alternative carrier for coverage starting January 1, 2027. However, the auto-assigned plan may not include your primary doctor, preferred hospital, or current medications.

What is the deadline to select a replacement ACA plan for January 1 coverage?

The deadline to select an active replacement plan for coverage starting January 1, 2027, is December 15, 2026. If you enroll between December 16 and January 15, your new coverage will take effect February 1, 2027.

Which ACA carriers are available in Polk County to replace Cigna or Molina?

Available individual exchange carriers in Polk County for 2027 include Florida Blue, Ambetter from Sunshine Health, Oscar Health, and UnitedHealthcare Individual and Family Plans. Network participation must be verified by individual plan ID.
