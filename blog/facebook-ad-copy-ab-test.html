Facebook Ad Image Rules: Keep text to under 20% of the image area (Facebook's old rule is gone, but text-heavy images still get lower delivery). Use 1200×628px for feed placement. High contrast, readable on mobile. Your face performs better than graphics — always test a photo of you first.

Copy Rules

  • No ALL CAPS (except "$0" or "ER" as abbreviations)
  • No exclamation marks in headlines
  • No "FREE" — use "$0" or "no cost" instead
  • No health claims or guarantees of specific savings
  • Include "Licensed FL broker" somewhere in the ad or landing page
  • Keep primary text under 300 characters for mobile feed (the full text shows on click)

Compliance Notes

CMS/ACA compliance: Don't guarantee specific dollar amounts for subsidies. Use "may qualify," "estimated," and "depends on income" language. Never say "free health insurance" — say "$0 premium" or "no monthly premium cost." All blog posts include proper disclaimers.

Facebook ad policy: Health insurance ads on Facebook must not imply the viewer has a specific condition. Avoid "Are you sick?" or "Do you have diabetes?" framing. The current ad copy focuses on financial situations (income, costs) rather than health conditions — keep it that way.

Florida DOI: All ads must be truthful and not misleading. License number should be visible on the landing page (it's in the blog footer and disclaimer). No bait-and-switch — the blog content must deliver on the ad's promise.
