Facebook Ad Image Rules: Keep image text concise, use 1200×628px for feed placement, and verify mobile readability and contrast. Test a compliant broker photo against a simple information graphic.

Copy Rules

  • Use sentence case; retain standard abbreviations only where needed
  • No exclamation marks in headlines
  • Do not lead with a zero-price or cost-elimination claim
  • Use current, plan-specific estimates and avoid guarantees of savings or outcomes
  • Include "Licensed FL broker" somewhere in the ad or landing page
  • Keep primary text under 300 characters for mobile feed (the full text shows on click)

Compliance Notes

CMS/ACA compliance: Do not guarantee a subsidy, premium, eligibility result, benefit, or savings amount. State that the Marketplace application controls financial-assistance eligibility and that plan documents control premiums, benefits, and cost sharing.

Facebook ad policy: Health insurance ads on Facebook must not imply the viewer has a specific condition. Avoid "Are you sick?" or "Do you have diabetes?" framing. The current ad copy focuses on financial situations (income, costs) rather than health conditions — keep it that way.

Florida DOI: All ads must be truthful and not misleading. License number should be visible on the landing page (it's in the blog footer and disclaimer). No bait-and-switch — the blog content must deliver on the ad's promise.
