Florida Health Coverage Without Income Verification: 2026 Guide

By David Huff | Licensed FL Broker #W371813 | Published Mar 27, 2026 · Last updated August 12, 2026
Home > Blog > Non-Income Based Health Insurance

1. Health Care Sharing Arrangements

Health care sharing arrangements are not insurance. Eligibility, member contributions, eligible expenses, exclusions, and payment processes are controlled by the current member guidelines rather than an insurance policy. A sharing request is not the same as an insurance claim, and membership should not be presented as comprehensive major medical coverage.

Verification checkpoint

Read the current member guidelines, pre-existing-condition rules, sharing limits, provider-payment process, dispute process, and financial disclosures. Confirm separately how you would handle services the arrangement does not share.

Potential role: A values-aligned sharing arrangement may be considered by someone who understands and accepts its non-insurance structure. Do not use it as a substitute for reviewing ACA-compliant coverage and its consumer protections.

2. Short-Term Limited-Duration Insurance

Short-term limited-duration insurance is medically underwritten health insurance intended for temporary gaps. It is not ACA-compliant comprehensive individual-market coverage and may exclude pre-existing conditions, omit categories of care, impose benefit limits, or deny an application based on health.

Current duration rule

CMS states that short-term coverage sold or issued on or after September 1, 2024 is generally limited to an initial term of no more than three months and a maximum coverage period of no more than four months, including renewals and extensions. Florida law, the carrier filing, availability, underwriting, and the actual policy form must also be checked before enrollment.

  • Verify the effective date, termination date, renewal or extension treatment, and whether replacement coverage will be available afterward.
  • Review exclusions, pre-existing-condition language, prescription coverage, provider access, and the benefit maximum.
  • Compare an ACA Marketplace Special Enrollment Period before accepting a temporary policy.

Related guides: Short-Term Health Insurance Guide and ACA vs. Short-Term Plans.

3. Fixed-Indemnity Insurance and Health ProtectorGuard

Fixed-indemnity insurance pays scheduled amounts for covered events or services. It is insurance and may qualify as an excepted benefit, but it is not comprehensive major medical coverage. The scheduled payment is not based on the provider's full bill and may be much less than the amount owed.

Health ProtectorGuard is a fixed-indemnity product family underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company. The current Florida brochures describe the Core and Guard series; the actual policy, certificate, benefit schedule, exclusions, waiting periods, underwriting decision, rate, and effective date control.

Required boundary: Health ProtectorGuard does not replace ACA-compliant comprehensive major medical coverage. Do not infer that a scheduled hospital or surgical benefit will equal the provider charge, deductible, coinsurance, or remaining balance.

Where Accident and Dental Products Fit

Accident insurance, dental insurance, vision coverage, hearing benefits, and discount arrangements each address a narrower category. Their presence in a proposal does not make a limited medical arrangement comprehensive. Review each contract independently and keep its role separate from hospital, physician, prescription, and major-medical risk.

4. Direct Health Care Agreements

A direct primary care or other qualifying direct health care agreement is a written service agreement with a provider. Florida Statute 624.27 states that a qualifying agreement does not constitute insurance. The agreement must describe covered services, fees, duration, termination terms, and other required provisions.

What to verify

Confirm the services included in the membership and the separate charge for services outside the agreement. A direct health care agreement does not replace hospital, specialist, emergency, imaging, surgery, or prescription coverage unless the written agreement expressly includes a particular service.

5. Association or Group-Based Coverage

The phrase “association plan” does not identify the underlying legal structure. Coverage may be insured, self-funded, limited-benefit, or combined with non-insurance services. Before enrolling, identify the issuing carrier or plan sponsor, policy form, network, covered benefits, exclusions, state filing, and who handles claims and appeals.

6. International and Travel Medical Coverage

International and travel medical policies are designed around travel, residence abroad, or care outside a home country. Coverage area, duration, evacuation benefits, exclusions, pre-existing-condition treatment, and U.S. coverage vary by contract. They should not be assumed to replace comprehensive U.S. major medical coverage.

How the Coverage Roles Differ

Coverage type Primary role Critical verification
ACA-compliant major medical Comprehensive individual-market coverage Eligibility, subsidy, network, formulary, cost sharing, and annual out-of-pocket limit
Short-term medical Temporary medically underwritten gap coverage Duration, exclusions, underwriting, benefits, and replacement plan
Fixed indemnity Scheduled payments for covered events or services Benefit schedule, exclusions, waiting periods, and remaining financial exposure
Health care sharing Non-insurance member-sharing arrangement Member guidelines, eligible expenses, sharing limits, and dispute process
Direct health care agreement Defined provider services for a stated fee Written scope, fees, termination terms, and services outside the agreement

Do Not Build a “Bridge Strategy” From Marketing Labels

A medically underwritten temporary policy and a fixed-indemnity policy can be reviewed side by side, but combining limited products does not create ACA-compliant comprehensive coverage. Evaluate each contract on its own, then model the remaining exposure for hospital care, surgery, prescriptions, mental health, maternity, pre-existing conditions, and the date the temporary coverage ends.

Use a controlled comparison

  1. Check ACA Marketplace eligibility, including any Special Enrollment Period and financial assistance.
  2. Confirm the applicant can qualify for the non-ACA product and obtain the exact policy form and benefit schedule.
  3. List excluded or limited services and identify the applicant's remaining financial responsibility.
  4. Confirm the exit plan before temporary coverage ends.
  5. Do not enroll based on premium alone.

Start With the Coverage Need

  • Loss of job-based coverage: check Marketplace and COBRA timing before a temporary alternative.
  • Retirement before Medicare: calculate the entire bridge period and the risk of a coverage interruption.
  • Variable self-employment income: update Marketplace income estimates when circumstances change rather than abandoning financial assistance solely because income is uncertain.
  • Known care, prescriptions, pregnancy, or chronic conditions: prioritize comprehensive coverage and current provider and formulary verification.

Bottom Line

Income is relevant to Marketplace financial assistance; it is not a shortcut for deciding which coverage is safe. Start with ACA-compliant coverage, then evaluate any non-ACA arrangement by its legal status, underwriting, exclusions, benefit design, duration, and remaining financial risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy Florida health coverage without using income to calculate a subsidy?

Yes. Full-price ACA coverage and non-ACA arrangements can be reviewed without using income to calculate Marketplace financial assistance. Eligibility, enrollment timing, underwriting, benefits, and consumer protections still differ.

Is fixed-indemnity coverage comprehensive health insurance?

No. Fixed-indemnity coverage is insurance that pays scheduled amounts for covered events or services. It is an excepted benefit, not ACA-compliant comprehensive major medical coverage.

How long can new short-term medical coverage last?

CMS states that policies sold or issued on or after September 1, 2024 are generally limited to an initial term of no more than three months and a maximum coverage period of no more than four months, including renewals and extensions. Verify the current state rule, carrier filing, and exact policy before applying.

Is a direct health care agreement insurance in Florida?

No. Florida Statute 624.27 states that a qualifying direct health care agreement does not constitute insurance. The written agreement controls which provider services and fees are included.
