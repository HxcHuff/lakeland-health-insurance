The Texas Medical Center Network Divide in Greater Houston

Greater Houston is home to the world's largest medical complex, anchored by Houston Methodist Hospital, Memorial Hermann-Texas Medical Center, Baylor St. Luke's Medical Center, and UT MD Anderson Cancer Center. Across suburban communities including Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Cypress, Pearland, and Clear Lake, Houston Methodist and Memorial Hermann operate extensive regional hospital campuses.

Because individual HealthCare.gov plans in Texas are built almost exclusively on HMO and EPO chasses, carrier sub-networks frequently contract with one major Houston hospital system while excluding another. Even more critically, Medicare Advantage contract disputes with Houston Methodist have prompted thousands of Houston seniors to review whether their 2027 plan still covers their specialists.

3 Steps to Protect Your Houston Doctors Before Enrolling

  1. Check Both Physician Group and Hospital Facility: Verify whether your doctor bills under Houston Methodist Primary Care Group, Houston Methodist Specialty Physician Group, or Memorial Hermann Medical Group (MHMG).
  2. Understand MD Anderson Referral Rules: Specialty oncology centers in the Texas Medical Center are out-of-network on many individual exchange HMOs unless specific carrier contracts or gap exceptions apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Texas ACA Marketplace HMO plans cover Houston Methodist Hospital in 2027?

Many narrow-network Texas Marketplace HMO plans restrict elective inpatient access at Houston Methodist campuses; Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery County enrollees must verify specific carrier sub-networks before selecting a 2027 exchange policy.

Which 2027 coverage option gives Houston seniors guaranteed access to Houston Methodist and MD Anderson?

Original Medicare (Parts A and B) paired with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan provides accepted access to Houston Methodist, Memorial Hermann, and MD Anderson Cancer Center without Medicare Advantage network restrictions.

Are emergency visits at Memorial Hermann or Houston Methodist covered regardless of my ACA network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency room, including Memorial Hermann Level 1 Trauma Center and Houston Methodist ERs, are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates.
