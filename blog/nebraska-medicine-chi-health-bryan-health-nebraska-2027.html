Navigating Omaha, Lincoln & I-80 Corridor Networks in 2027

Across Eastern and Central Nebraska — from the Omaha metro (Douglas, Sarpy, and Washington counties) and Lincoln (Lancaster County) along the I-80 corridor to Grand Island (Hall County) and Kearney (Buffalo County) — individual ACA Marketplace and Medicare Advantage plans divide along two competing hospital alliances: the Nebraska Health Network (NHN) & Bryan Health corridor (comprising Nebraska Medicine at UNMC, Fred & Pamela Buffett Cancer Center, Bellevue Medical Center, Methodist Hospital, Methodist Women's Hospital, and Bryan Health in Lincoln, Grand Island, and Kearney) versus CHI Health (CommonSpirit) (operating CHI Health Creighton University Medical Center – Bergan Mercy, Lakeside, Immanuel, Midlands, CHI Health St. Elizabeth and Nebraska Heart in Lincoln, CHI Health St. Francis in Grand Island, and CHI Health Good Samaritan in Kearney).

On the Nebraska ACA Marketplace, insurers including Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Nebraska (BCBSNE), Medica, Ambetter from Nebraska Total Care, Oscar Health, and UnitedHealthcare frequently price plans around one alliance while placing the competing hospital system out-of-network.

3 Steps for Nebraska Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Distinguish Medica with CHI Health vs. Blueprint Health / NHN Tiers: In Omaha and Lincoln, verify whether your 2027 plan is locked exclusively to CHI Health or aligned with Nebraska Medicine, Methodist, and Bryan Health.
  2. Audit Fred & Pamela Buffett Cancer Center & April Sampson Cancer Center: Confirm both the hospital campus NPI and the academic or employed oncology group (UNMC Physicians in Omaha or Bryan Physician Network in Lincoln).
  3. Check the Grand Island & Kearney Two-Hospital Split: In Hall and Buffalo counties, verify whether your primary care physician and surgeons admit to Grand Island Regional / Kearney Regional (Bryan Health) or CHI Health St. Francis / CHI Health Good Samaritan.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Nebraska Medical Center, CHI Health Bergan Mercy, Methodist Hospital, Bryan Medical Center, and CHI Health St. Elizabeth ERs are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Omaha and Lincoln ACA Marketplace plans split between CHI Health and the Nebraska Health Network (Nebraska Medicine & Methodist) in 2027?

In Douglas, Sarpy, and Lancaster counties, carriers build narrow accountable care networks around competing hospital alliances: either CHI Health (Bergan Mercy, Lakeside, Immanuel, and St. Elizabeth in Lincoln) or the Nebraska Health Network (Nebraska Medicine and Methodist Health System in Omaha, often paired with Bryan Health in Lincoln).

How do I verify the Fred & Pamela Buffett Cancer Center at Nebraska Medicine for 2027?

Because the Fred & Pamela Buffett Cancer Center at Nebraska Medicine in Omaha is Nebraska's NCI-designated cancer center, patients should verify Nebraska Medical Center (facility), UNMC Physicians / Nebraska Medicine faculty specialists, and the outpatient infusion centers.

How do Bryan Health and CHI Health compare in Lincoln, Grand Island, and Kearney?

In Lincoln, Bryan Medical Center (East and West campuses) and CHI Health St. Elizabeth anchor competing network tiers, while along I-80 in Central Nebraska, Bryan Health's Grand Island Regional and Kearney Regional Medical Centers compete directly with CHI Health St. Francis and CHI Health Good Samaritan.
