How to Appeal 2027 Medicare IRMAA Surcharges With Form SSA-44

Because Social Security always looks back two tax years, newly retired seniors routinely get hit with $1,000 to $6,000+ per year in extra Part B and Part D IRMAA surcharges in 2027 based on their 2025 working salary. Filing Form SSA-44 with proof of retirement can remove the surcharge immediately.

Direct answer: If your 2027 Medicare Part B or Part D bill includes an IRMAA surcharge based on your higher 2025 tax return, you can ask Social Security to recalculate using your lower recent income by filing Form SSA-44. Social Security accepts only 8 exclusive life-changing events, most commonly Work Stoppage (retirement), Work Reduction, Marriage, Divorce, or Death of a Spouse.

What 2027 Income Thresholds Trigger IRMAA?

For 2027, standard Part B ($209.40/mo) and $0 Part D IRMAA apply up to $112,000 MAGI (single) or $224,000 MAGI (married filing jointly) on your 2025 return. Crossing by even $1 moves you into the next tier (+$83.70/mo Part B and +$15.40/mo Part D per spouse, escalating up to +$502.40/mo Part B and +$96.40/mo Part D at the top bracket). See the complete 2027 Medicare Part B & IRMAA bracket chart.

Married Filing Separately warning: If you lived with your spouse at any time during 2025 and filed Married Filing Separately, there are only two jump tiers: standard up to $112,000, then straight to +$460.60/mo ($670.00 total Part B) from $112,001 to $387,999, and +$502.40/mo at $388,000+.

Step-by-Step: How to Submit Form SSA-44 in 2027

  1. Step 1 (Confirm Your Qualifying Event & Date): Check the exact event box in Step 1 of Form SSA-44 (most commonly Work Stoppage or Work Reduction) and write the exact month/year it occurred.
  2. Step 2 (Enter Your Lower Tax Year MAGI Estimate): In Step 2, enter your actual or estimated Adjusted Gross Income plus tax-exempt interest for 2026 or 2027 and your filing status.
  3. Step 3 (Attach Clear Supporting Evidence): Include your HR retirement letter, signed tax return (if already filed), marriage/divorce decree, or death certificate.
  4. Step 4 (File Within 60 Days Online, by Fax, Mail, or In Person): Submit through your my Social Security account, fax/mail to your local Social Security office, or call Social Security at 1-800-772-1213 (TTY 1-800-325-0778). Refunds for overpaid months are credited back once approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I file Form SSA-44 online?

Yes. Social Security allows beneficiaries to submit Form SSA-44 digitally through a my Social Security account, or by fax, mail, or in-person appointment at a local Social Security office.

Does selling a house or doing a Roth conversion qualify for Form SSA-44?

No. Voluntary capital gains from selling real estate or stocks and Roth IRA conversions are not on SSA's exclusive list of 8 life-changing events. Those one-year spikes normally drop off automatically two years later.

How long do I have to appeal an IRMAA determination?

Social Security gives you 60 days from the date you receive your Initial IRMAA Determination Notice to request a new initial determination or reconsideration, though SSA-44 can also be filed whenever a qualifying event reduces income during the year.

If my SSA-44 is approved, do I get refunded for higher Part B premiums already deducted?

Yes. Once Social Security updates your IRMAA tier retroactively to January (or the effective month), overpaid Part B and Part D IRMAA deductions are credited back to your Social Security benefit or refunded.
