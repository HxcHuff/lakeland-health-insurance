Direct answer: In Osceola County for 2027, Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter from Sunshine Health offer ACA Marketplace plans covering combinations of AdventHealth Celebration & Kissimmee, Orlando Health St. Cloud Hospital, and HCA Florida Osceola & Poinciana Hospitals, while Cigna Healthcare and Molina Healthcare exited the Florida ACA Marketplace for 2027 (requiring former enrollees to actively select a new carrier).

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance does AdventHealth Celebration accept in 2027?

AdventHealth Celebration accepts Original Medicare with Medigap, contracted Medicare Advantage plans, and 2027 ACA Marketplace plans from Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter (verify UnitedHealthcare network status following the December 31, 2026 contract renewal deadline).

Is Orlando Health St. Cloud Hospital in-network with Oscar, Ambetter, and Florida Blue for 2027?

Orlando Health St. Cloud Hospital participates in select 2027 Florida Blue, Oscar Health, Ambetter, and UnitedHealthcare ACA Marketplace sub-networks in Osceola County, as well as Original Medicare with Medigap and contracted Medicare Advantage plans.

Does HCA Florida Osceola Hospital in Kissimmee accept Ambetter and Florida Blue in 2027?

Yes. HCA Florida Osceola Hospital and HCA Florida Poinciana Hospital participate broadly in 2027 Ambetter from Sunshine Health, Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and UnitedHealthcare ACA plans, plus major Osceola County Medicare Advantage networks.
