How HealthCare.gov Enrollment Works (2027)

  1. Create/login to your HealthCare.gov account.
  2. Complete household and income eligibility accurately.
  3. Upload documents quickly if asked (income, immigration, address).
  4. Compare plans by network, formulary, MOOP, and total annual cost.
  5. Select plan, confirm effective date, and make first payment on time.

Document Prep Checklist

  • ID for all applicants
  • Income proof (recent pay stubs, self-employment records, tax docs)
  • Current doctors and medications list
  • Preferred hospitals (LRH/Watson verification ready)

Universal First-30-Days Checklist

  • Create member portal and mobile app account
  • Select/confirm PCP assignment
  • Verify all chronic prescriptions on formulary tier and quantity limits
  • Schedule preventive annual visit and baseline labs
  • Turn on autopay and paperless billing
  • Save customer-service call logs and reference numbers
