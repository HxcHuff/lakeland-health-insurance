Navigating Metro Atlanta & Statewide Georgia Networks in 2027

Across Fulton, Cobb, Gwinnett, DeKalb, Forsyth, and Cherokee counties — as well as Athens, Augusta, Columbus, and Macon — hospital care is divided among four flagship systems: Piedmont Healthcare (25 hospitals including Piedmont Atlanta, Piedmont Fayette, Piedmont Newnan, Piedmont Henry, Piedmont Athens Regional, Piedmont Augusta, and Piedmont Macon), Wellstar Health System (Wellstar Kennestone Regional Medical Center in Marietta, Wellstar Cobb, Wellstar North Fulton, Wellstar Paulding, and Wellstar MCG Health in Augusta), Emory Healthcare (Emory University Hospital, Emory Saint Joseph's, Emory Midtown, Emory Johns Creek, Emory Decatur, and Winship Cancer Institute), and Northside Hospital (Atlanta, Forsyth, Gwinnett, Cherokee, and Duluth).

Georgia operates its own state-based exchange, Georgia Access (GeorgiaAccess.gov), where insurers design narrow-network HMO and EPO products that frequently partner with only one or two of these health systems per rating area.

3 Steps for Georgia Residents Before Open Enrollment Ends

  1. Verify Both the Hospital System and Employed Medical Group: In Metro Atlanta, Piedmont Clinic, Wellstar Medical Group, and The Emory Clinic bill under specific physician group tax IDs that must be checked alongside the hospital facility.
  2. Check Pediatric Access at Children's Healthcare of Atlanta (CHOA): Families with children using the new Arthur M. Blank Hospital or Scottish Rite campus should confirm CHOA facility and pediatric specialist participation before locking in a 2027 plan.
  3. Review Multi-County Commuter Coverage: If you live in Cobb, Cherokee, or Gwinnett County and commute into Fulton or DeKalb County for specialty care at Emory or Piedmont Atlanta, confirm your HMO covers providers across Metro Atlanta county lines.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at Piedmont, Wellstar, Emory, Northside, and Grady Memorial emergency rooms are covered at in-network cost-sharing regardless of Georgia Access sub-network status; network restrictions apply to non-emergency surgery, scheduled admissions, and clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do 2027 Georgia Access plans cover Piedmont Healthcare, Wellstar, and Emory Healthcare together?

Most Georgia Access HMO and EPO plans in Metro Atlanta align with a specific subset of hospital systems — such as Anthem Pathway, Ambetter Peach State, Oscar, CareSource, or Alliant — so patients must check whether their exact sub-network covers Piedmont, Wellstar, Emory, or Northside.

Are Piedmont Athens Regional, Piedmont Augusta, and Piedmont Macon in the same network as Piedmont Atlanta?

Piedmont Healthcare negotiates system-wide contracts across its Georgia hospitals, but individual plans are sold by county rating area on Georgia Access, meaning a plan available in Clarke, Richmond, or Bibb County may differ from Fulton or Cobb County networks.

How do Medicare beneficiaries in Georgia keep access to Emory Winship Cancer Institute, Piedmont, and Wellstar?

Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) paired with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy is accepted by any hospital and physician in Georgia that accepts Medicare assignment, including Emory Healthcare, Piedmont, Wellstar, and Northside Hospital.
