Why 1.7 Million Seniors Are Losing Their 2027 Medicare Advantage Plans

Heading into the 2027 plan year, major Medicare Advantage carriers sharply contracted county footprints and terminated lower-margin PPO and HMO plans—affecting roughly 1.7 million enrollees nationwide and leaving 193 U.S. counties across 16 states with zero Medicare Advantage prescription drug plans:

  • Humana (~600,000 Members Impacted): Discontinued select plans and reduced its county footprint from roughly 85% down to 80% of U.S. counties for 2027.
  • UnitedHealthcare (~390,000 Members Impacted): Non-renewed select Medicare Advantage plans—especially broader PPO networks carrying higher utilization costs—and exited targeted rural and suburban counties.
  • Molina Healthcare (Exiting Traditional Standalone MAPD Nationwide): Pulling out of traditional standalone Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug (MAPD) products nationally for 2027 while concentrating on Medicaid and Dual Special Needs lines.
  • Aetna, Centene (Wellcare) & Elevance (Anthem): Also pruned county footprints, pared back dental allowances and Part B givebacks, and shifted from broader PPOs toward stricter HMOs.

Do Not Ignore Your Non-Renewal Letter: Original Medicare Fallback & GI Medigap

If your plan sent a formal Notice of Non-Renewal confirming it is ending on December 31, 2026:

  • Automatic Fallback Danger: Doing nothing returns you to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027—leaving you with no Part D prescription drug plan and no annual out-of-pocket cap on the 20% Part B coinsurance.
  • 63-Day Guaranteed Issue Medigap Pass: A plan termination opens a federal Guaranteed Issue window (60 days before through 63 days after December 31, 2026) to enroll in standardized Medigap (such as Plan G or Plan N, depending on Medicare eligibility date) with no medical underwriting.
  • Extended Non-Renewal SEP Through February 28, 2027: In addition to AEP (ending December 7, 2026), displaced beneficiaries can enroll in a new MA plan or Part D plan through February 28, 2027 (note: applying after December 31 delays the new effective date to the first of the next month).

4 Steps If Your 2027 Medicare Advantage Plan Was Discontinued

  1. Keep Your Official Non-Renewal Notice: Store the paper termination notice—you will need it to prove Guaranteed Issue Medigap eligibility without health underwriting.
  2. Act Before December 7 (or December 31) for Zero January Gap: Enrolling before December 7 gives carriers time to issue new cards before January 1; waiting into January or February via the non-renewal SEP pushes your start date to February 1 or March 1.
  3. Run Your Full Prescription List Under the 2027 $2,400 Part D Cap: Formularies and deductibles (up to $700 in 2027) shifted dramatically across standalone PDPs and MA-PD plans—check exact pharmacy pricing first.
  4. Verify Active Hospital Disputes in Your State: Check whether major local systems (such as Sentara/VCU in VA, Vanderbilt in TN, Kettering in OH, Lee Health in FL, or Providence in WA) also changed 2027 carrier contracts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many seniors are affected by 2027 Medicare Advantage plan terminations?

Roughly 1.7 million Medicare Advantage enrollees nationwide are in plans terminating or exiting their counties for 2027, including approximately 600,000 Humana members and 390,000 UnitedHealthcare members alongside Molina's exit from standalone MAPD plans.

What happens on January 1, 2027 if my Medicare Advantage plan ended and I did not pick a new one?

If your plan is discontinued and you do not choose a replacement by December 31, 2026, you automatically revert to Original Medicare (Parts A and B) on January 1, 2027—leaving you without Part D drug coverage and without an annual out-of-pocket limit.

Can I switch to Medigap Plan G without medical underwriting if my Medicare Advantage plan is discontinued?

Yes. A full Medicare Advantage plan termination or county exit triggers a federal 63-day Guaranteed Issue Medigap right allowing eligible beneficiaries to enroll in qualifying Medigap plans without health underwriting when submitting their non-renewal letter.

How long is the Special Enrollment Period if my 2027 Medicare Advantage plan was terminated?

In addition to Annual Enrollment (October 15 through December 7, 2026), federal rules provide a Special Enrollment Period running from December 8, 2026 through February 28, 2027 for beneficiaries whose plans were non-renewed.
