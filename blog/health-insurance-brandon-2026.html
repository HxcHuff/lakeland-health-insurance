Special Enrollment Periods: Who May Qualify

Events that may open a Special Enrollment Period, subject to Marketplace rules, deadlines, and verification, include:

  • Lost job-based coverage (including through reduced hours)
  • Got married
  • Divorce or legal separation that ends your coverage
  • Had or adopted a child
  • Certain permanent moves that change your plan options (prior coverage usually required)
  • Lost CHIP or other minimum essential coverage
  • Aged off a parent's plan at 26
  • COBRA coverage ran out (dropping COBRA or not paying does not qualify)

You generally have 60 days. For most new SEP enrollments, HealthCare.gov verifies eligibility before coverage starts.

For Brandon families, the birth/adoption trigger is particularly relevant — adding a new dependent mid-year is one of the most common SEP triggers, and it lets you re-evaluate your entire family's plan, not just add the child.

HSA-Eligible Bronze Plans: A New Option for Brandon Families

For 2026, all Bronze and Catastrophic Marketplace plans are HSA-eligible. For Brandon families who are relatively healthy and earn above the Silver-tier cost-sharing reduction threshold (roughly 250% of the Federal Poverty Level), a Bronze plan plus an HSA is one option to compare — not a promise of a lower bill. Savings and subsidy eligibility depend on income, household and plan; confirm at HealthCare.gov.

This is worth modeling if your family is in the "subsidy cliff" zone — earning enough that subsidies are minimal but not enough that premiums feel comfortable.
