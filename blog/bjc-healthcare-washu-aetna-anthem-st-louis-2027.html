BJC HealthCare & WashU St. Louis 2027 Network: Patient Guide

How St. Louis, St. Charles, and Jefferson County residents can verify Barnes-Jewish Hospital, St. Louis Children's Hospital, and WashU Medicine network access for 2027.

Academic Medicine and Network Alignment in Greater St. Louis

BJC HealthCare is the largest healthcare provider in the St. Louis metropolitan area, operating flagship academic centers including Barnes-Jewish Hospital and St. Louis Children's Hospital in partnership with Washington University School of Medicine (WashU Medicine) and the Siteman Cancer Center.

Because St. Louis healthcare is divided among four major integrated systems—BJC HealthCare, Mercy, SSM Health, and St. Luke's—individual HealthCare.gov Marketplace plans and Medicare Advantage networks frequently align with only one or two hospital systems. Contract negotiations involving major payers such as Anthem BlueCross BlueShield of Missouri, Aetna, and UnitedHealthcare require St. Louis patients to verify both hospital facility and WashU faculty physician billing before locking in a 2027 plan.

BJC HealthCare and WashU Facilities Across the St. Louis Region

When reviewing a 2027 Missouri health plan directory, confirm how the policy treats both academic downtown campuses and suburban community hospitals:

  • Barnes-Jewish Hospital & Siteman Cancer Center (Central West End): Quaternary referral center, organ transplant institute, and NCI-designated comprehensive cancer center.
  • St. Louis Children's Hospital: Premier pediatric hospital and Level 1 Pediatric Trauma Center staffed by WashU pediatric subspecialists.
  • Missouri Baptist Medical Center (Town and Country): Major West St. Louis County acute-care hospital for cardiology, oncology, and surgical care.
  • Barnes-Jewish West County Hospital (Creve Coeur): Specialized orthopedic, surgical, and outpatient specialty campus.
  • Barnes-Jewish St. Peters Hospital & Progress West Hospital (O'Fallon): Key acute-care hospitals serving St. Charles County families.
  • Christian Hospital (North St. Louis County): Acute-care and emergency hub serving North County.

Emergency Protections Under the Federal No Surprises Act

Under the federal No Surprises Act, St. Louis residents experiencing an emergency medical condition are protected at every hospital emergency department—including Barnes-Jewish Hospital, St. Louis Children's Hospital, Missouri Baptist Medical Center, and Barnes-Jewish St. Peters. Insurers must cover emergency room evaluation and stabilization at in-network cost-sharing rates without requiring prior authorization.

Open Enrollment Checklist for St. Louis & St. Charles Residents

  1. Distinguish BJC Medical Group from WashU Physicians: Community primary care doctors often bill under BJC Medical Group, while specialists at Barnes-Jewish and St. Louis Children's bill under Washington University Physicians. Check both entities in your 2027 directory.
  2. Cross-Check Mercy and SSM Specialists: If your household splits care between BJC and Mercy or SSM Health, verify whether a single Marketplace plan covers both systems or prioritize the system handling your highest-acuity care.
  3. Review Missouri Marketplace Subsidies: Compare Silver CSR and Bronze HSA options on our Missouri Health Insurance Guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Barnes-Jewish Hospital and WashU Medicine physicians in-network for 2027 Missouri ACA plans?

Network participation at Barnes-Jewish Hospital and Washington University Physicians varies significantly by carrier and sub-network tier in the St. Louis rating area; policyholders must check both facility and physician group status before selecting a 2027 plan.

Can St. Louis families use St. Louis Children's Hospital or Barnes-Jewish ER during a medical emergency?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency department, including Barnes-Jewish Hospital and St. Louis Children's Hospital, must be processed at in-network cost-sharing levels.

How can Missouri Medicare beneficiaries keep unrestricted access to BJC HealthCare and WashU specialists?

Original Medicare paired with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policy allows Missouri beneficiaries to see any BJC Medical Group or Washington University physician who accepts Medicare assignment without network referrals or prior authorization.
