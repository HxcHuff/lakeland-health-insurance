Navigating the Southwestern Medical District in Dallas-Fort Worth

In the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex, tertiary academic medicine, trauma care, and pediatric subspecialty care are anchored by three interconnected institutions in the Southwestern Medical District (plus expanding campuses in Plano, Frisco, RedBird, and Fort Worth):

  • UT Southwestern Medical Center: William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital, Zale Lipshy Pavilion, and the Harold C. Simmons Comprehensive Cancer Center (aligned with Texas Health Resources through the Southwestern Health Resources clinically integrated network).
  • Parkland Health: Parkland Memorial Hospital (Dallas County's primary public hospital, Level I Trauma Center, and Regional Burn Center) and Community Oriented Primary Care (COPC) clinics.
  • Children's Health: Children's Medical Center Dallas and Children's Medical Center Plano, staffed primarily by UT Southwestern pediatric faculty specialists.

Texas Continuity of Care & Federal No Surprises Act Protections

  • Federal No Surprises Act Emergency Coverage: Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at Parkland Memorial Hospital ER, Clements University Hospital ER, and Children's Medical Center Dallas ER are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of whether your ACA or Medicare Advantage plan contracts with the hospital for elective care.
  • Texas Continuity of Care (Texas Insurance Code Chapters 843 & 1301): If a UT Southwestern specialist, Simmons Cancer Center oncologist, or Children's Health pediatric specialist leaves your Texas HMO or EPO network while you or your child are undergoing active treatment for a serious or complex condition, Texas law and federal No Surprises Act Section 113 allow eligible patients to request up to 90 days of transitional care at in-network cost-sharing rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans in Dallas-Fort Worth cover UT Southwestern Clements University Hospital?

UT Southwestern Medical Center and the Southwestern Health Resources clinically integrated network participate in select DFW ACA Marketplace sub-networks, while excluding other narrow HMO exchange tiers; Dallas, Tarrant, Collin, and Denton County residents must verify the exact 2027 plan network ID.

How do Children's Health Dallas and UT Southwestern Pediatric Group bill on family ACA plans?

Children's Medical Center Dallas (facility) and UT Southwestern pediatric faculty specialists often bill under separate tax IDs, so DFW families must confirm both the pediatric hospital campus and the attending physician group in their 2027 HealthCare.gov directory.

Are emergency visits to Parkland Memorial Hospital or Clements University Hospital covered out-of-network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at Parkland Level I Trauma Center, Clements University Hospital, and Children's Medical Center Dallas emergency rooms are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates.
