How to check your coverage before scheduled care

  1. Find the exact plan name and plan ID on your insurance card.
  2. Search the hospital by its full name in the plan's provider directory for the year of the visit.
  3. Search each doctor separately. A hospital can be in network while a surgeon, anesthesia group or radiology group is not, and the reverse.
  4. Call the hospital's billing office with the plan name and plan ID. Ask whether they accept that plan for that date of service.
  5. Ask whether your plan requires a referral or prior authorization for the procedure. HMOs often do.
  6. Write down who confirmed each answer, and when.

Marketplace (ACA) plans

Network participation is set plan by plan, and it can differ between plans sold by the same insurer. For 2027 coverage, Open Enrollment starts November 1, 2026. Check the hospital in each plan's directory before you pick. If you plan to deliver a baby there, confirm labor and delivery, the delivering doctor, and newborn and NICU care under the same plan. A birth may qualify you for a Special Enrollment Period, subject to eligibility. Report it to HealthCare.gov within 60 days; the baby's coverage can start on the date of birth. Medicare

  • Original Medicare: covers care at any hospital that accepts Medicare. You do not need a network.
  • Medicare Advantage: plans use networks that can change each January 1. If you want access to this hospital or its doctors in 2027, check the 2027 plan directory during Annual Enrollment, October 15 to December 7, 2026. Your plan's Annual Notice of Change was due by September 30.
  • Medigap: works with Original Medicare, so it follows the same rule.

See Can I keep my doctor if I switch Medicare plans?.

Emergencies

If you have an emergency, go to the nearest emergency room. Your plan must cover emergency services, and the No Surprises Act limits what you pay for out-of-network emergency care to your in-network cost sharing. Once you are stable, admission and follow-up care may fall under your plan's network rules. Call your plan as soon as you can.

What this does not change

A new hospital does not by itself create a Special Enrollment Period. You change Marketplace plans during Open Enrollment or after a qualifying event, and Medicare plans during Medicare enrollment periods. Do not switch plans on the assumption that a network will add the hospital. Check the directory for the plan year you are buying.

Common questions

Is the Orlando Health hospital in Lakeland open?

Yes. Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital opened July 8, 2026, at 4000 Lakeland Highlands Road in Lakeland.

Will my Marketplace plan cover Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital?

Only if the hospital is in network for your exact plan and plan year. Check the plan's provider directory, check each treating doctor separately, and confirm with the hospital's billing office before scheduled care.

Will my Medicare Advantage plan cover the new hospital?

It depends on that plan's network for the year of care. Medicare Advantage networks vary by plan and can change each January 1. Original Medicare covers care at any hospital that accepts Medicare.

What if I go to its emergency room and it is out of network?

Your plan must cover emergency services, and the No Surprises Act limits what you pay for out-of-network emergency care to your in-network cost sharing. Care after you are stable can follow your plan's network rules.

Does the new hospital let me change plans outside Open Enrollment?

No. A new hospital does not create a Special Enrollment Period. Marketplace changes happen during Open Enrollment or after a qualifying event, and Medicare changes happen during Medicare enrollment periods.
