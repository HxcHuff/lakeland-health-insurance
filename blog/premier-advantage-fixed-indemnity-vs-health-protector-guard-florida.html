Why Florida Shoppers Compare Premier Advantage, Health ProtectorGuard & TriTerm

Direct answer: When Under-65 Florida shoppers search for Premier Advantage fixed indemnity, Health ProtectorGuard (HPG), TriTerm Medical, or Short Term Medical Plus Elite, they are typically looking for lower-monthly-premium alternatives outside the ACA Marketplace—or trying to understand what a quote they received over the phone actually covers.

In Florida, non-ACA individual coverage falls into two very different legal product categories that should never be confused:

  • Fixed Indemnity Insurance (such as Premier Advantage or UHOne / Golden Rule Health ProtectorGuard): Pays a fixed, scheduled dollar amount per covered service or per hospital day (for example, $2,000 to $5,000 per inpatient hospital day and $80 to $150 per doctor visit) regardless of what the hospital bills. Fixed indemnity has no deductible, no coinsurance, and no out-of-pocket maximum—meaning you owe any balance above the fixed cash payout.
  • Short-Term / TriTerm Medical Insurance (such as Golden Rule Short Term Medical Plus Elite / Co-Pay Select Max): Medically underwritten temporary health coverage that uses a traditional deductible (such as $2,500 to $12,500), coinsurance (such as 30%), and a defined out-of-pocket cap for covered non-pre-existing illnesses and injuries up to a policy maximum (such as $2 million), subject to federal and state duration rules.

How Golden Rule Health ProtectorGuard & Co-Pay Select Max Stack Together

For healthy Florida self-employed earners or early retirees above the 400% FPL subsidy cliff who pass medical underwriting and want UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus PPO access, fixed indemnity is most effective when used as a supplemental cash layer rather than a standalone substitute for major medical:

  1. Medical Catastrophic Engine: A major medical or medically underwritten policy handles large inpatient hospital bills above a chosen deductible (such as $5,000 or $10,000).
  2. Fixed Indemnity Cash Layer (Health ProtectorGuard Select 2000, Preferred 4000, or Guard 4000): Pays scheduled first-day cash ($2,000 to $4,000 hospital admission, $80–$150 doctor visits, surgical cash) to help offset the underlying deductible.
  3. Dental, Vision & Hearing Layer: A dedicated Golden Rule DentalWise Max or MetLife NCD plan covers preventive cleanings, crowns, implants, and eyewear.

3 Red Flags to Check Before Buying Any Fixed Indemnity Plan in Florida

  • Red Flag #1 — Calling Fixed Indemnity "Major Medical" or "Full PPO Insurance": A legitimate licensed agent will always show you the written daily payout table and state clearly that fixed indemnity pays scheduled dollar amounts—not the full hospital bill.
  • Red Flag #2 — Skipping the 2027 ACA Subsidy Check: During Open Enrollment (November 1 – January 15), always check your 2027 ACA Marketplace subsidy eligibility first. Many households qualify for Silver or Bronze major medical plans at a lower net monthly cost than unsubsidized private packages.
  • Red Flag #3 — Ignoring Surgical & Day Limits: Compare whether surgical benefits pay across unlimited days (like HPG Preferred 4000) or cap surgical payouts at 2 days per calendar year (like HPG Guard 4000).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Premier Advantage or Health ProtectorGuard a major medical health insurance plan?

No. Both Premier Advantage and UHOne (Golden Rule) Health ProtectorGuard are fixed-indemnity insurance plans. They pay a set dollar amount for covered medical services regardless of the actual provider bill and are not Minimum Essential Coverage under the Affordable Care Act.

What is the difference between TriTerm / Short Term Medical Plus Elite and Health ProtectorGuard?

Short Term Medical Plus Elite and TriTerm Medical are medically underwritten temporary medical policies with a deductible, coinsurance, and a policy maximum for unexpected non-pre-existing illnesses and injuries. Health ProtectorGuard is a fixed-indemnity policy with no deductible that pays scheduled cash amounts per day or visit.

Can you use Health ProtectorGuard alongside another health insurance plan?

Yes. Because Health ProtectorGuard pays fixed cash benefits regardless of other coverage, many members pair a tier like Select 2000 or Guard 4000 with a high-deductible medical plan to help offset out-of-pocket deductible expenses.

Should I check Florida ACA Marketplace plans before buying a fixed indemnity policy?

Yes. ACA Marketplace plans on HealthCare.gov cover pre-existing conditions from Day 1, include a true annual out-of-pocket maximum, and offer income-based tax credits for eligible households between 100% and 400% FPL during Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15).
