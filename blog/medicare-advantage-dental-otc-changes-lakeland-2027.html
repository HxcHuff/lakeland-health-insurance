Why 2027 Medicare Advantage Extra Benefits Changed

Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) does not cover routine dental care, eyeglasses, hearing aids, or Over-the-Counter (OTC) pharmacy items. Many Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans in Polk County include these supplemental benefits—often advertised on television as "flex cards," "OTC allowances," or "comprehensive dental."

For the 2027 plan year, industry-wide analyses of CMS Medicare Plan Finder data (including October 2026 findings reported by Healthcare Dive and the Medicare Rights Center) showed that major insurers reduced or restructured supplemental allowances—such as quarterly OTC credits, Part B givebacks, and dental maximums—to offset rising medical utilization and Part D drug liability.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Original Medicare cover routine dental, vision, and hearing in Florida?

No. Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) covers medically necessary inpatient and outpatient care, such as cataract surgery or jaw reconstruction after an injury, but does not cover routine dental cleanings, crowns, dentures, routine eye exams, eyeglasses, or hearing aids. Those benefits are only available through Medicare Advantage plans that include them as supplemental benefits or through standalone dental, vision, and hearing policies.

Why did my Medicare Advantage OTC card or dental allowance shrink for 2027?

Across the country for 2027, many Medicare Advantage insurers reduced supplemental benefits like OTC allowances, flex cards, and Part B givebacks to offset higher medical utilization and new Part D prescription drug liabilities. Your 2027 Annual Notice of Change (ANOC) and Summary of Benefits list the exact 2027 dollar amounts for your specific plan.

Who actually qualifies for Medicare Advantage grocery or utility payment cards in 2027?

Grocery and utility allowances are typically offered only on Dual-Eligible Special Needs Plans (D-SNPs for people with both Medicare and qualifying Florida Medicaid) or Chronic Condition Special Needs Plans (C-SNPs) under CMS SSBCI rules. Standard Medicare Advantage plans generally offer traditional OTC health-item credits rather than grocery or utility cards.

How do I check if my Lakeland dentist accepts a 2027 Medicare Advantage plan?

Medicare Advantage plans contract with specific dental vendor networks (and often specific sub-tiers within that vendor). Before enrolling, check the plan's 2027 dental provider directory by your dentist's NPI and ask the dental office whether they participate in-network with that exact Medicare Advantage dental network for 2027.
