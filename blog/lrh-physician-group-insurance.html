Short answer: check every doctor by name, not the health system

Check each doctor by name and location in your plan's 2027 provider directory, not just "Lakeland Regional Health." Then call the office (LRH primary care: 863.284.5000) with your plan ID.

Original Medicare lets you use any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare, anywhere in the U.S.; Medicare Advantage plans may require network providers for non-emergency care.

How to check your LRH doctors in a 2027 plan

It depends on plan type. HMOs generally don't cover out-of-network care except emergencies; POS plans require a PCP referral for specialists; PPOs allow out-of-network care without a referral at extra cost. Check the plan's rules.

Medicare.gov tells shoppers to ask their doctors and pharmacies whether they are in network with the plan they are considering.

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 (plan must get the request by December 7). The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period runs January 1 to March 31 for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan.

Network status can differ by plan; check the plan's provider directory before enrolling. Use the exact plan ID and coverage year, then call the facility billing office and write down the date and the person you spoke with.

If your doctor isn't in the plan you want

With Original Medicare, any provider who takes Medicare. With Medicare Advantage, confirm each doctor in the plan's directory during AEP (Oct 15–Dec 7).

Medigap (Medicare Supplement Insurance) is extra insurance from a private company that helps pay your share of costs in Original Medicare.

Medicare: keeping your LRH doctor

Medicare marketing on this site is for Florida residents. Confirm hospital, doctor, and pharmacy participation with the plan and the office. Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage follow different network rules.

Common questions

Is My Lakeland Regional Health Doctor in My Plan?

Check each doctor by name and location in your plan's 2027 provider directory, not just "Lakeland Regional Health." Then call the office (LRH primary care: 863.284.5000) with your plan ID. Confirm the exact plan ID and coverage year before you rely on a directory listing.

How many providers are in LRH Physician Group?

Lakeland Regional Health lists more than 400 physicians and providers in more than 40 specialties at more than 35 locations, including 10 or more primary care offices in five cities.

Do I need a referral to see an LRH specialist?

It depends on your plan type. HMOs generally don't cover out-of-network care except in an emergency, POS plans require a referral from your primary care doctor to see a specialist, and PPOs let you use out-of-network providers without a referral for an additional cost. Check your plan's rules before you book.

Can I keep my LRH doctor if I switch Medicare plans?

With Original Medicare, you can see any provider who takes Medicare. With a Medicare Advantage plan, check each doctor in the plan's provider directory before you enroll, ideally during Open Enrollment, October 15 to December 7.
