What insurance does Watson Clinic accept?

Watson Clinic’s published lists cover Traditional Medicare, Medicare supplements, named Medicare Advantage products (some HMO designs are specialty-only), commercial payers, and Florida Blue Marketplace plans such as myBlue, BlueCare, and BlueOptions. Confirm each doctor and location in your plan’s directory and with Patient Relations (863-680-7213).

HealthCare.gov open enrollment for 2027 coverage runs November 1 through January 15, with December 15 as the last day to enroll or change plans for a January 1 start date. Medicare Annual Enrollment for 2027 coverage runs October 15 through December 7.

What changed — and what did not

  • Affiliation, not a merger: Orlando Health’s FAQ describes collaboration with Watson Clinic, not an acquisition.
  • Hospital is open: Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital (4000 Lakeland Highlands Road) began receiving patients July 8, 2026.
  • Insurance is still plan-by-plan: Affiliation does not make every doctor, lab, imaging site, hospital service, or referral path in network for your plan ID.
  • 2027 Humana change: Watson Clinic will not accept Humana plans starting January 1, 2027 — confirm alternatives before enrollment deadlines.
  • Where surprises happen: Exact plan ID, site of service, referral rules, prior authorization, directory lag, and benefit-year network changes.

FAQ

Did Orlando Health buy Watson Clinic?

No. Orlando Health's own FAQ says the relationship is not a merger or acquisition. It is described as an affiliation agreement and collaboration.

Does the hospital opening mean Watson Clinic doctors only use Orlando Health?

No. Orlando Health's Watson Clinic page says Watson Clinic physicians continue to provide care where their patients need care and continue to have privileges at other area hospitals.

Should I rely on a provider directory?

Use the directory as a starting point. For a high-stakes decision, verify the exact plan with the carrier and the provider office because directories can lag after network or facility changes.

What insurance does Watson Clinic accept?

Watson Clinic publishes participation by plan type: Traditional Medicare, Medicare supplements, listed Medicare Advantage products, commercial payers, and Florida Blue Marketplace plans such as myBlue. Accepted at the clinic is not the same as in network for your exact plan ID. Confirm with Patient Relations and your plan directory.

Does Watson Clinic accept Medicare Advantage plans?

It depends on the plan year and plan ID. Check the Medicare Advantage network for doctors and for Orlando Health Watson Clinic Lakeland Highlands Hospital separately before you switch.

Does Watson Clinic take Humana in 2027?

No. Starting January 1, 2027, Watson Clinic will no longer accept Humana plans. Confirm current participation with the clinic and your plan. If you have a Humana plan and see Watson Clinic doctors, talk with David Huff, a local licensed agent, before Medicare Open Enrollment ends December 7.
