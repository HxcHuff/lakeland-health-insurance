Why UnitedHealthcare & Cigna Are Leaving Georgia Access for 2027

Georgia operates its own state-based exchange, Georgia Access (georgiaaccess.gov)—not HealthCare.gov—and two national insurers are leaving the exchange for 2027:

  • UnitedHealthcare Exiting Georgia Access: UHC confirmed it is withdrawing its on-exchange individual ACA plans from Georgia Access effective January 1, 2027 after state regulators pushed back on a proposed 54% rate increase. UHC continues offering off-exchange individual policies, employer group plans, and Medicare plans in Georgia—note that federal premium tax credits only apply to plans bought through Georgia Access, never off-exchange.
  • Cigna Exiting Georgia Completely: Cigna Healthcare is discontinuing all individual and family ACA policies (on- and off-exchange) across Georgia effective December 31, 2026 as part of its 11-state individual exit.

Watch Georgia's 100%–400% FPL Rules & Metro Atlanta Hospital Splits

Because Georgia has not adopted broad ACA Medicaid expansion (instead running limited Georgia Pathways eligibility), federal premium tax credits on Georgia Access generally apply between 100% and 400% FPL ($15,960–$63,840 single / $33,000–$132,000 family of four using 2026 HHS guidelines), with full uncapped repayment at tax time if actual income exceeds your estimate. Enroll by December 15, 2026 for January 1 coverage (Open Enrollment closes January 15, 2027).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UnitedHealthcare leaving Georgia Access for 2027?

Yes. UnitedHealthcare confirmed it is exiting the Georgia Access on-exchange ACA Marketplace effective January 1, 2027 after regulators pushed back on a proposed 54% rate hike, while continuing off-exchange, employer, and Medicare lines.

Can I keep ACA subsidies if I stay with an off-exchange UnitedHealthcare plan in Georgia?

No. Federal advance premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions apply only to qualified health plans purchased through Georgia Access, so subsidy-eligible households must switch to a participating 2027 Georgia Access insurer.

Is Cigna also exiting Georgia for 2027?

Yes. Cigna is exiting individual and family ACA coverage across Georgia (both on- and off-exchange) effective December 31, 2026.

When is the deadline to replace my Georgia Access plan for January 1, 2027?

Complete your new plan selection on Georgia Access by December 15, 2026 for a January 1, 2027 start date. Open Enrollment closes January 15, 2027, with selections after December 15 generally taking effect February 1.
