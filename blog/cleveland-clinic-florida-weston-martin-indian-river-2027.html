Cleveland Clinic Florida's Five-Hospital Corridor in 2027

Along Florida's Treasure Coast and Southeast corridor — spanning Indian River, St. Lucie, Martin, Palm Beach, and Broward counties — Cleveland Clinic Florida operates five major hospitals: Cleveland Clinic Indian River Hospital (Vero Beach), Cleveland Clinic Tradition Hospital (Port St. Lucie), Cleveland Clinic Martin North & Martin South Hospitals (Stuart), and the quaternary transplant and surgical center at Cleveland Clinic Weston Hospital.

In many Treasure Coast ZIP codes (such as 34952, 34953, 34986, 34990, 34994, and 32960), Cleveland Clinic is the dominant hospital system alongside HCA Florida Lawnwood Hospital (Fort Pierce), HCA Florida St. Lucie Hospital, and Jupiter Medical Center. Verifying that your 2027 ACA Marketplace or Medicare plan includes Cleveland Clinic facilities and employed physicians is essential before Open Enrollment ends.

3 Steps for Treasure Coast & Broward Patients

  1. Check Cross-County Access Between Stuart, Port St. Lucie, and West Palm Beach: Residents in southern Martin County (Tequesta, Hobe Sound, Stuart) often use doctors in both Martin County and northern Palm Beach County (Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens). Confirm your 2027 plan covers both counties.
  2. Verify Quaternary Referral Access to Cleveland Clinic Weston: If your specialist in Vero Beach or Port St. Lucie refers complex cardiac, GI, or transplant care to Cleveland Clinic Weston in Broward County, ensure your policy allows multi-county network access.
  3. Review Exiting Carrier Replacements: If your 2026 Cigna or Molina plan is ending, compare active 2027 options directly rather than relying on an automatic Marketplace crosswalk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which 2027 ACA Marketplace plans cover Cleveland Clinic hospitals on the Treasure Coast and in Weston?

Select Florida Blue plans (such as BlueOptions and BlueCare) and specific Oscar Health and Ambetter sub-networks include Cleveland Clinic Martin North, Martin South, Tradition Hospital, Indian River Hospital, and Weston Hospital, though narrow HMO tiers must be verified by county.

Can Treasure Coast patients in Stuart, Port St. Lucie, or Vero Beach be referred to Cleveland Clinic Weston?

Yes, if your plan network spans both the Treasure Coast and Broward County or provides statewide network access (such as Florida Blue BlueOptions or Original Medicare with Medigap); single-county ACA HMOs may require prior authorization for out-of-county referrals to Weston.

Are emergency visits at Cleveland Clinic Tradition, Martin, and Indian River hospitals covered in-network?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical screening and stabilization at any Cleveland Clinic Florida emergency department must be billed at your plan's in-network cost-sharing rate.
