Providence Health Plan's 2027 Insurance Exit Explained

Providence Health Plan confirmed that beginning January 1, 2027, it is transitioning out of its Individual and Family, Medicare Advantage, Individual Medicare, and Employer Group/Commercial health insurance lines. Current 2026 benefits, prescriptions, and claims remain active through December 31, 2026, but policies do not renew into 2027.

Crucial distinction for patients: Only the insurance carrier (Providence Health Plan) is winding down—Providence hospitals, Swedish campuses, PacMed Clinics, and Kadlec medical groups remain open and contract with multiple 2027 insurers. However, because Providence, Swedish, and Kadlec are also leaving UnitedHealthcare Individual Medicare Advantage on January 1, 2027, displaced members must verify 2027 hospital contracts carefully before picking a new carrier.

Medicare Advantage & Medigap Guaranteed Issue Replacement Options

  • Southwest Washington & Devoted Health Partnership: Providence announced a new 2027 Medicare Advantage contracting partnership with Devoted Health in Southwest Washington (Clark County / Vancouver region) and Oregon so displaced Providence Health Plan Medicare Advantage members have a contracted MA option that keeps Providence physicians, clinics, and hospitals in network.
  • Puget Sound, Spokane & Tri-Cities Medicare Advantage Alternatives: Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner (OIC) guidance confirms at least one 2027 Medicare Advantage plan remains available in every Washington county—check exact NPI participation across Premera, Regence, Asuris (Eastern WA), Kaiser Permanente WA, Humana, Aetna, Molina, and Community Health Plan of Washington.
  • Federal Medigap Guaranteed Issue Window: Because your Medicare Advantage plan is leaving your service area rather than voluntarily dropped, terminated Providence Health Plan MA enrollees receive a Special Enrollment Period and a Guaranteed Issue right to return to Original Medicare and buy eligible Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans without health underwriting—keeping your official plan termination notice as proof.
  • Do Not Let Coverage Default Blindly on January 1: If a terminated Medicare Advantage enrollee selects no replacement plan, coverage reverts to Original Medicare alone on January 1, 2027—without standalone Part D prescription drug coverage unless actively enrolled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which insurance lines is Providence Health Plan ending in Washington for 2027?

Providence Health Plan announced it is transitioning out of Individual and Family, Medicare Advantage, Individual Medicare, and Employer Group/Commercial health insurance lines beginning January 1, 2027. Existing 2026 coverage remains active through December 31, 2026.

Are Providence, Swedish, and Kadlec hospitals closing or still accepting other 2027 insurance plans?

Providence hospitals, Swedish campuses, PacMed Clinics, and Kadlec clinics remain open and continue accepting Original Medicare (with or without Medigap) as well as multiple contracted 2027 Medicare Advantage and Washington Healthplanfinder carriers, including a new 2027 Medicare Advantage partnership with Devoted Health in Southwest Washington and Oregon.

Do terminated Providence Health Plan Medicare Advantage members get a Guaranteed Issue right for Medigap?

Yes. Under federal Medicare rules and Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner guidance, when a Medicare Advantage plan leaves your service area and terminates your policy, you receive a Special Enrollment Period and a federal Medigap Guaranteed Issue window to enroll in eligible Medicare Supplement plans without medical underwriting.

What changed on Washington Healthplanfinder for 2027 individual ACA plans?

The Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner approved 12 Exchange insurers for 2027 with a 22.2% average rate increase after Providence Health Plan exited the individual market, while Premera Blue Cross expanded individual coverage into 15 new Washington counties for a total of 24 counties.
