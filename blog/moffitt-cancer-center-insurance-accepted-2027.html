Why Moffitt Network Access Matters in Central Florida

Moffitt Cancer Center in Tampa is the only National Cancer Institute (NCI)-designated Comprehensive Cancer Center based in Florida. Patients from Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, and Polk County rely on Moffitt for cutting-edge clinical trials, advanced surgical oncology, and specialized multidisciplinary care teams.

However, securing coverage at Moffitt requires careful health plan selection. Several insurance companies have narrowed their provider networks or terminated commercial and Medicare Advantage contracts with Moffitt, making network verification essential during Open Enrollment.

Medicare Coverage: Original Medicare vs. Medicare Advantage

Medicare beneficiaries facing complex cancer diagnoses frequently encounter the sharpest divide between coverage models:

  • Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) + Medigap: Moffitt Cancer Center accepts Original Medicare assignment. When paired with a Medicare Supplement plan (such as Plan G or Plan N), beneficiaries can receive care at Moffitt without prior authorization paperwork, referral hurdles, or network restrictions.
  • Medicare Advantage (Part C): In recent years, Moffitt has experienced contract terminations and dispute impasses with major Medicare Advantage payers (notably Humana Medicare Advantage in select service areas). Beneficiaries on private MA HMO plans often face strict prior authorization and out-of-network denials.

Florida Continuity of Care Protections

If your current insurance carrier terminates its agreement with Moffitt while you are actively being treated, Florida law provides statutory protections:

Under Florida Statute § 627.64194 and federal transition rules, active cancer patients (such as individuals undergoing active chemotherapy, radiation, or scheduled surgery) may submit a Continuity of Care request. When approved, the carrier must continue paying for treatment at in-network cost-sharing levels for up to 90 days, ensuring continuity during critical clinical interventions.

Action Steps for Moffitt Patients During Open Enrollment

  1. Identify the Treating Facility & Physician Group: Confirm whether your oncologist bills under Moffitt Medical Group or USF Health.
  2. Avoid Value HMO Traps: Do not select ultra-low premium exchange HMOs without checking hospital participating lists; saving \$40 a month in premium can result in complete exclusion from Moffitt.
  3. Verify Drug Formulary Coverage: Ensure specialized oral oncology medications and IV infusion biologics are properly placed on your prospective plan's drug tier.
  4. Consider Medigap Guaranteed Issue: If your Medicare Advantage plan non-renews or drops your cancer specialists, review whether you qualify for a federal Guaranteed Issue window to transition to Medigap Plan G.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Florida Blue cover Moffitt Cancer Center on all plans?

No. Moffitt Cancer Center is contracted with Florida Blue BlueOptions PPO and select BlueCare HMO plans with referral, but it is excluded from narrow network products like myBlue HMO.

Does Original Medicare with a Medigap supplement cover Moffitt Cancer Center?

Yes. Moffitt Cancer Center accepts Original Medicare (Part A and Part B). Any standard Medigap policy, such as Plan G or Plan N, pays Medicare cost-sharing with no network restrictions or prior authorization delays.

What continuity of care rights protect oncology patients if their plan drops Moffitt?

Under Florida law and federal protections, patients undergoing active cancer treatment can request up to a 90-day continuity of care transition period, allowing them to continue seeing their Moffitt oncologists at in-network cost-sharing levels.
