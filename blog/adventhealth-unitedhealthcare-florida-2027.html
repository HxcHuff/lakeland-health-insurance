The December 31, 2026 Contract Deadline Explained

AdventHealth and UnitedHealthcare are locked in high-stakes contract negotiations across Florida, with their current provider network agreement scheduled to expire on December 31, 2026. Both organizations have published public negotiation updates stating that if a renewed agreement is not reached before the deadline, AdventHealth hospitals, outpatient facilities, urgent care clinics, and employed physician groups in Florida would become out-of-network for UnitedHealthcare plans effective January 1, 2027.

Through December 31, 2026, nothing changes for current patients: all AdventHealth hospitals and physicians remain in-network under existing 2026 contract terms. However, because the December 31 contract expiration date lands immediately after fall enrollment windows close, patients in Polk County, Greater Orlando, Tampa Bay, and Volusia/Flagler must verify their 2027 provider directories carefully before locking in a 2027 plan.

  • Medicare Advantage (Part C): Includes UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage HMO, PPO, Dual Special Needs (D-SNP), and AARP-branded Medicare Advantage plans.
  • Individual & Family ACA Marketplace (Exchange): Includes on-exchange HealthCare.gov and off-exchange individual policies.
  • Commercial Employer-Sponsored Plans: Includes employer group medical plans administered by UnitedHealthcare.
  • Medicaid Plans: Includes UnitedHealthcare Community Plan Medicaid coverage in participating Florida regions.

Emergency Care vs. Non-Emergency Services in 2027

Whether you carry a Medicare Advantage plan, an Individual Marketplace policy, or an employer group plan, federal law separates true medical emergencies from scheduled care:

Call the member services number on the back of your insurance card to request a Continuity of Care form if a contract expires.

Key 2027 Enrollment Windows: Medicare's Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026 for a January 1, 2027 effective date, followed by the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period from January 1 through March 31, 2027. Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Florida contract between AdventHealth and UnitedHealthcare expire?

The current provider network agreement between AdventHealth and UnitedHealthcare in Florida is scheduled to expire on December 31, 2026. Care and in-network coverage continue unchanged through December 31, 2026, while contract negotiations remain underway for the 2027 plan year.

Which health insurance plans are affected if AdventHealth and UnitedHealthcare do not reach a 2027 agreement?

If a renewed contract is not reached before January 1, 2027, the network change would affect UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage plans (including AARP-branded Medicare Advantage plans), Individual and Family ACA Marketplace plans, employer-sponsored commercial group plans, and Medicaid plans across Florida.

Are emergency room visits at AdventHealth hospitals still covered if a contract expires?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act and Medicare emergency coverage rules, emergency room evaluation and stabilization at any AdventHealth hospital emergency department, including AdventHealth Heart of Florida, AdventHealth Lake Wales, AdventHealth Orlando, and AdventHealth Tampa, must be processed at in-network cost-sharing levels regardless of network contract status.

Does this contract dispute affect Original Medicare with a Medigap supplement policy?

No. Original Medicare (Parts A and B) paired with a Medicare Supplement (Medigap Plan G or Plan N) does not rely on private Medicare Advantage carrier networks. Beneficiaries on Original Medicare can continue seeing AdventHealth hospitals and physicians that accept Medicare assignment regardless of UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage contract negotiations.
