Frequently Asked Questions

How does hospital indemnity insurance help with a high-deductible health plan?

Hospital indemnity policies pay a fixed daily cash amount directly to you (or your provider if assigned) when you have a covered inpatient hospital stay, regardless of what your primary health insurance pays. You can use those cash benefits toward your deductible, coinsurance, or household bills.

What is the difference between HospitalWise and Hospital SafeGuard GI?

Both are underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company for ages 18-64. HospitalWise (Form G202605) pays $1,000 per inpatient day (up to 10 days per confinement) across Basic, Bundle 1, and Bundle 2 options. Hospital SafeGuard GI (Form G202602) is guaranteed issue with no medical underwriting and offers Plan 500, 750, 1000, or 2000 ($500 to $2,000/day inpatient plus matching ICU benefits, up to 31 days per confinement).

Does hospital indemnity insurance replace major medical or Medicare?

No. Hospital indemnity and fixed-benefit plans are limited-benefit supplemental policies. They are not ACA Minimum Essential Coverage, not major medical insurance, and not a substitute for Medicare Advantage or Medigap.
