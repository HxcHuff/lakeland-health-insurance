The Space Coast Healthcare Landscape

Health First is the dominant integrated delivery system in Brevard County, operating four acute-care hospitals, comprehensive outpatient clinics, and the county's only Level 2 Trauma Center at Holmes Regional Medical Center in Melbourne.

Contract discussions between Health First and national payers like UnitedHealthcare significantly impact residents across Melbourne, Palm Bay, Cocoa Beach, Rockledge, and Titusville. When contract deadlines approach during fall Open Enrollment, policyholders face uncertainty regarding whether their primary care physicians and chosen hospitals will remain in-network starting January 1.

Health First Facilities Across Brevard County

The Health First network encompasses the primary medical and emergency hubs of the Space Coast:

  • Holmes Regional Medical Center: 514-bed tertiary hospital in Melbourne featuring Level 2 Trauma care, heart center, and comprehensive stroke team.
  • Cape Canaveral Hospital: Serving Cocoa Beach, Cape Canaveral, and the central beachside communities.
  • Viera Hospital: Modern acute-care community hospital serving the master-planned Viera and Suntree areas.
  • Palm Bay Hospital: Serving the rapidly growing South Brevard and Palm Bay population.
  • Health First Medical Group: Multi-specialty physician practices with more than 400 healthcare providers.

Emergency Care Protections Under Federal Law

Brevard County residents worried about emergency medical access should know the federal rules established under the No Surprises Act:

Care Type Network Status Rule Patient Financial Protection
Emergency Room Evaluation Protected by Federal Law Emergency services at Holmes Regional, Viera, Cape Canaveral, or Palm Bay ERs must be covered at in-network cost-sharing.
Trauma Activation & Stabilization Protected by Federal Law Trauma team resuscitation is billed at in-network rates with zero balance billing permitted for emergency stabilization.
Elective Surgeries & Inpatient Care Contract Dependent Out-of-network coinsurance applies, or claims may be denied under narrow HMO exchange policies without prior authorization.

Carrier Alternatives for Space Coast Policyholders

Residents seeking stable, contracted access to Health First facilities have multiple avenues during Open Enrollment:

  • Health First Health Plans: The health system's own commercial and Medicare Advantage health plan offers deeply integrated provider care and coordinated clinical pathways throughout Brevard.
  • Florida Blue: Maintains long-standing provider agreements for BlueOptions PPO and BlueCare HMO tiers across all four Health First hospitals.
  • Oscar Health & Regional Exchange Plans: Evaluate specific provider directory tiers before enrolling on HealthCare.gov.

Open Enrollment Action Steps for Brevard Residents

  1. Audit Current Insurance Plan Code: Locate your 14-digit Plan ID on your insurance portal or policy documentation to determine your exact network type.
  2. Confirm Doctor NPI Affiliation: Ask your physician whether they bill through Health First Medical Group or an independent medical association.
  3. Compare Premiums and Out-of-Pocket Maximums: Balance monthly subsidy savings against potential out-of-network hospital expenses.
  4. Complete Changes Before Deadlines: Enroll by December 7 for Medicare or December 15 for HealthCare.gov coverage effective January 1, 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Health First in-network for UnitedHealthcare in 2027?

Health First hospital facilities and medical group practices maintain contract negotiations with UnitedHealthcare; patients should check their specific Medicare Advantage or commercial plan network prefix during Open Enrollment to confirm current participation.

Can Brevard County patients still use Holmes Regional Medical Center in an emergency?

Yes. Under the federal No Surprises Act, emergency medical evaluation and stabilization at any hospital emergency room, including Holmes Regional Medical Center and Cape Canaveral Hospital, must be billed at your plan's in-network cost-sharing level.

What other insurance carriers cover Health First hospitals in Brevard County?

Health First operates its own regional insurance provider (Health First Health Plans) and maintains active network participation with Florida Blue (BlueOptions and BlueCare) across Space Coast facilities.
