Why Riverview Residents Are Paying More in 2026

The enhanced premium tax credits that cushioned ACA Marketplace costs expired December 31, 2025. On top of that, base premiums climbed 20-26% due to rising hospital costs, specialty drug spend, and healthcare inflation across the Tampa Bay market.

For Riverview's population of young families and mid-career professionals — many with employer coverage that doesn't extend affordably to spouses and dependents — the Marketplace is often the go-to for family coverage. The premium jump changes the math on which tier and plan makes sense.

The Family Glitch Fix remains in effect for 2026: if your employer offers coverage but the family premium exceeds the affordability threshold, your spouse and children can qualify for Marketplace subsidies even if you can't. This is worth re-evaluating given the new cost landscape.

New to Riverview? Your Move Might Qualify You to Enroll Now

Riverview's growth means many residents moved here recently — from another Florida county, another state, or Tampa. Certain moves that change available plan options may open a Special Enrollment Period.

Marketplace rules may include prior-coverage, deadline, and documentation requirements. Confirm the applicable window before relying on a move-related SEP.

Other events that may open an SEP include loss of qualifying coverage, marriage, a new dependent, loss of Medicaid or CHIP, aging off a parent's plan, or COBRA exhaustion. Marketplace rules and documentation apply.

Medicaid Transitions in Hillsborough County

Federal policy changes have accelerated Medicaid redetermination cycles to every six months and introduced work requirements. For Riverview families near the income line between Medicaid and Marketplace eligibility — a common scenario in a community with a mix of salaried and hourly-wage households — this means more frequent eligibility changes.

Loss of Medicaid or CHIP may open a Marketplace Special Enrollment Period, subject to current rules, deadlines, and documentation. The Marketplace determines eligibility using the completed application, including projected household income.

Bronze + HSA Strategy for Riverview Families

For 2026, every Bronze and Catastrophic plan on the Marketplace is HSA-compatible. For Riverview households that are relatively healthy and earn above 250% of the Federal Poverty Level (where cost-sharing reductions phase out), this creates a strategic option: lower premiums with a Health Savings Account that lets you contribute pre-tax, grow tax-free, and withdraw tax-free for qualified medical expenses.

This is especially worth running the numbers on if your family is young and your healthcare utilization is primarily preventive care and the occasional urgent care visit.

Medicare GLP-1 Bridge: Confirm Eligibility Through CMS

CMS says the Medicare GLP-1 Bridge runs from July 1, 2026, through December 31, 2027. Eligible Medicare Part D beneficiaries may receive certain weight-management GLP-1 drugs with a $50 copay after program eligibility screening and prior authorization. Eligibility is not based on BMI alone; check the current CMS criteria or call 1-800-MEDICARE before relying on the program.
