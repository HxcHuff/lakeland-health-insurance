Direct Answer: What Insurance Do Clermont & Leesburg Hospitals Accept in 2027?

Direct answer: In Lake County for 2027, Orlando Health South Lake Hospital (Clermont), AdventHealth Waterman (Tavares), and UF Health Leesburg Hospital participate in select Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter ACA Marketplace networks as well as Original Medicare with Medigap and contracted Medicare Advantage plans—though narrow-network HMO plans often cover only one or two of Lake County's three hospital systems.

Because Southern Lake County residents in Clermont, Minneola, and Groveland routinely commute into West Orange County (Horizon West / Winter Garden) or South into Four Corners (Polk/Osceola), while Northern Lake County residents in Leesburg, Tavares, Eustis, and Mount Dora often see specialists in The Villages or Orlando, checking cross-county network rules is essential before 2027 Open Enrollment closes.

3 Steps for US-27 Commuters (Clermont, Minneola, Groveland & Four Corners)

  1. Check South Lake vs. West Orange County Hospital Sharing: Clermont and Minneola families frequently use Orlando Health Horizon West Hospital or AdventHealth Winter Garden just across the Orange County line. Confirm your 2027 plan covers both Lake and Orange counties.
  2. Verify Four Corners Cross-County Urgent & ER Access: Where US-27 meets US-192 at Four Corners (Lake, Polk, Orange, and Osceola counties), make sure your ACA or Medicare Advantage HMO allows out-of-county urgent care and specialist referrals (see our Four Corners & Davenport 2027 guide).
  3. Review the Dec 31, 2026 AdventHealth / UnitedHealthcare Renewal Status: If your primary care physician is part of AdventHealth Medical Group in Tavares, Mount Dora, Eustis, or Clermont and you carry UnitedHealthcare, review our AdventHealth & UnitedHealthcare 2027 Contract Status Guide.

Emergency Room Protection (No Surprises Act): Under the federal No Surprises Act and CMS Medicare emergency rules, emergency department evaluation and stabilization at any hospital ER are covered at in-network cost-sharing rates regardless of whether the hospital is in your plan's network; network tiers govern scheduled surgeries, non-emergency inpatient admissions, and outpatient clinic visits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What insurance does Orlando Health South Lake Hospital in Clermont accept for 2027?

Orlando Health South Lake Hospital accepts Original Medicare with Medigap, contracted Medicare Advantage plans, and 2027 ACA Marketplace plans from Florida Blue, Oscar Health, Ambetter, and select UnitedHealthcare tiers in Lake County.

Does AdventHealth Waterman in Tavares accept the same insurance as Orlando Health South Lake?

Some broad 2027 Florida Blue, Oscar Health, and Ambetter sub-networks in Lake County include both AdventHealth Waterman and Orlando Health South Lake Hospital, while narrow-network HMO tiers may contract with only one system.

Is UF Health Leesburg Hospital covered by Lake County Medicare Advantage and ACA plans in 2027?

UF Health Leesburg Hospital accepts Original Medicare with any Medigap policy and contracts with major Lake County Medicare Advantage carriers and select Florida Blue and Ambetter ACA Marketplace plans. Always verify your physician group tax ID before enrolling.
