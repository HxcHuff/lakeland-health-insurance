Surprise-bill protections for emergency care

Medicare Part B usually covers emergency department services. You pay a copayment per visit and per hospital service, plus 20% of the Medicare-approved amount for doctor services after the Part B deductible. If admitted to the same hospital for a related condition within 3 days, the ED copayments don't apply. Ask the hospital whether a visit to this freestanding department counts as the same hospital for this rule.

Where will I go if I need to be admitted from HCA Florida Auburndale Emergency?
