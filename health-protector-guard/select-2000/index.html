Who picks Select 2000?

Points to review before selecting Select 2000:

  • Benefit amounts: compare its scheduled hospital, surgical, office-visit, prescription, and imaging benefits with the other Core options.
  • Prescription limits: the brochure lists a combined maximum of 12 covered fills in Year 1, not unlimited prescription coverage.
  • Other coverage: fixed-indemnity insurance is supplemental and should not be treated as comprehensive major medical coverage.
  • Application review: confirm current rate, ZIP-code availability, underwriting, exclusions, and the issued certificate before enrolling.

Frequently asked questions

Is Select 2000 ACA-compliant?

No. It is fixed-indemnity insurance, not ACA major medical. Review it only as supplemental coverage and compare the limitations of every coverage component.

Does Select 2000 have a pre-existing condition exclusion?

Yes — a 12-month lookback applies. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months of the policy.

How do I confirm Select 2000 availability in Florida?

The current carrier brochure lists Florida and policy form HPG3-GRI-09. VERIFICATION REQUIRED: confirm current availability for the applicant's ZIP code through the carrier quote system before applying.

Why is Select 2000 cheaper than Preferred 4000?

Select 2000 lists lower hospital-confinement, ER, specialist, and surgical fixed-benefit amounts than Preferred 4000 in the current brochure. Prescription and imaging schedules also differ by option. Compare the complete benefit tables and current rates.

Review Select 2000 before applying

Review Health ProtectorGuard variants filed in Florida and pairing options with ACA, short-term medical, or Medigap. The goal is to confirm whether the benefit schedule, exclusions, and underwriting fit your situation.
