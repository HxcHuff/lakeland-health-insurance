Who picks Select 2000?

Select 2000 is most commonly chosen by:

  • Floridians on a tight budget who want Core-series Rx and imaging benefits without paying for the higher Preferred or Premier hospital benefits.
  • Self-employed and 1099 enrollees using a high-deductible ACA plan — Select 2000 fills small claims fast.
  • Pre-Medicare enrollees bridging a few months before age 65 who want broader benefits than the Guard series.
  • Anyone who wants Rx covered every fill at the lowest Core premium.

Frequently asked questions

Is Select 2000 ACA-compliant?

No. It's fixed-indemnity insurance, not ACA major medical. Most enrollees pair it with an ACA Bronze plan or use it as a supplement during a coverage gap.

Does Select 2000 have a pre-existing condition exclusion?

Yes — a 12-month lookback applies. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months of the policy.

Is Select 2000 available statewide in Florida?

Yes. It's filed for sale in Florida on policy form HPG3-GRI-09, including in Lakeland, Bartow, Winter Haven, Plant City, and Auburndale.

Why is Select 2000 cheaper than Preferred 4000?

Select 2000 pays lower per-day hospital and surgical benefits, fewer office visits in Year 1, and a lower ER and specialist amount. The Rx and imaging benefits are similar — what you give up is the size of the hospital and surgical payouts.
