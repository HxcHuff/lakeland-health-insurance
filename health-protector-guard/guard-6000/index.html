Frequently asked questions

Is Guard 6000 ACA-compliant?

No. It is fixed-indemnity insurance, not ACA major medical. Review it only as supplemental coverage and compare the limitations of every coverage component.

Why does Guard 6000 cost more than Guard 5000?

Rates are applicant-specific and must be confirmed through a current quote. Compared with Guard 5000, the brochure lists a higher surgical schedule maximum, hospital admission benefit, and outpatient facility benefit for Guard 6000. Compare the premium difference with the complete benefit schedule and limitations.

Does Guard 6000 cover pre-existing conditions?

A 12-month lookback applies. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months.
