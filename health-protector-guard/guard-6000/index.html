Health ProtectorGuard Guard 6000

Guard 6000 pays set amounts for covered hospital, doctor, prescription, imaging, and other listed services. It may supplement other health coverage, but it is not major medical insurance.

What to know first

  • The plan pays a set amount for each covered service, not the full medical bill.
  • It includes limited benefits for certain hospital, doctor, prescription, imaging, and other listed services.
  • Guard 6000 is the higher scheduled-benefit option in the three-option Guard series.
  • Health questions, limits, exclusions, and pre-existing-condition rules apply.

The “6000” refers to a $6,000 fixed benefit for a covered hospital admission. It is not a deductible or a promise to pay your full bill.

How Guard 6000 pays

You do not need to memorize the full benefit schedule to understand the plan.

1

You receive a covered service

The service must be listed as covered in the policy.

2

The plan pays the scheduled amount

The payment is subject to the policy's rules and limits.

3

You handle any remaining balance

If the bill is higher than the benefit, you may owe the difference.

David can show you the exact benefit schedule and current rate when you compare options. The issued policy controls.

When Guard 6000 may be worth comparing

Guard 6000 may be worth comparing if you already have major medical coverage and want supplemental help with certain expenses. It is one of three Guard options with different scheduled-benefit levels.

It should not replace ACA, Medicare, Medicaid, employer, or other comprehensive health coverage. Before you apply, David can confirm the current premium, Florida ZIP-code availability, medical underwriting, benefit limits, and exclusions.

How it differs from the other Guard options

Guard 6000 is the higher scheduled-benefit Guard option. Guard 4000 and Guard 5000 provide lower scheduled benefits for some covered services, and Guard 4000 does not include prescription or outpatient imaging benefits.

The right option depends on the care you expect to use, the coverage you already have, current rates, and what you could still owe.

Frequently asked questions

Is Guard 6000 major medical insurance?

No. Guard 6000 is fixed-indemnity insurance that pays limited, set benefits for covered services. It is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA and should not replace comprehensive health insurance.

Will Guard 6000 pay my full medical bill?

Not necessarily. If the provider's charge is more than the plan's scheduled benefit, you may owe the difference.

Do health questions and pre-existing-condition rules apply?

Yes. The plan is subject to medical underwriting. Current materials describe a 12-month lookback and a pre-existing-condition limitation that can apply for up to 12 months after coverage begins. The issued policy controls.
