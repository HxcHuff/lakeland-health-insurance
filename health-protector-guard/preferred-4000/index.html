Frequently asked questions

Is Health ProtectorGuard Preferred 4000 ACA-compliant?

No. It's fixed-indemnity supplemental insurance, not ACA major medical, and is not minimum essential coverage. Many Floridians pair it with an ACA plan to fill out-of-pocket gaps.

Does Preferred 4000 cover pre-existing conditions?

Golden Rule applies a 12-month pre-existing condition lookback and exclusion. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months.

Can I get Preferred 4000 in Polk County?

Yes — it's filed for sale in Florida on policy form HPG3-GRI-09 and available statewide, including Lakeland, Bartow, Winter Haven, Plant City, and Auburndale.

How does Preferred 4000 pay claims?

Preferred 4000 pays fixed-dollar benefits per covered service directly to you (or assigned to the provider). Benefits are not reduced by what other coverage pays.
