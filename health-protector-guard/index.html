Important Coverage Disclosure

Health Protector Guard is fixed indemnity insurance with limited benefits.

This plan is supplemental coverage only. It is not intended to replace comprehensive major medical insurance.

This plan is NOT Minimum Essential Coverage (ACA).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an ACA-compliant major medical plan?

No. Health Protector Guard is fixed indemnity, limited-benefit supplemental insurance and not Minimum Essential Coverage.

Who underwrites Health Protector Guard?

Golden Rule Insurance Company underwrites the product.

Can this plan be used by itself?

It is intended to supplement other health coverage, not replace comprehensive major medical coverage.

What network and extra features are referenced?

The brochure references UHC Choice Plus, HealthiestYou by Teladoc, and an Optum Perks discount card.

Is there an age limit?

Eligibility is 18–64 with renewability to age 65 under policy and state rules.
