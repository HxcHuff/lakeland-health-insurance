Who picks Premier 5000?

Points to review before selecting Premier 5000:

  • Benefit amounts: compare its scheduled hospital, surgical, office-visit, prescription, and imaging benefits with the other Core options.
  • Prescription limits: the brochure lists a combined maximum of 20 covered fills in Year 1, not unlimited prescription coverage.
  • Other coverage: fixed-indemnity insurance is supplemental and should not be treated as comprehensive major medical coverage.
  • Application review: confirm current rate, ZIP-code availability, underwriting, exclusions, and the issued certificate before enrolling.

Frequently asked questions

Is Premier 5000 ACA-compliant?

No. Premier 5000 is fixed-indemnity supplemental insurance, not ACA major medical. Review it only as supplemental coverage and compare the limitations of every coverage component.

Does Premier 5000 cover pre-existing conditions?

A 12-month lookback applies. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months of the policy.

Which Premier option is listed in the current Florida brochure?

The current carrier brochure that lists Florida identifies Premier 5000. Do not infer current availability from a product name alone; confirm the applicant's ZIP code and current carrier quote before applying.

How does Premier 5000 pay claims?

Premier 5000 pays fixed-dollar benefits per covered service, paid directly to you (or assigned to the provider). Benefits are not reduced by what other coverage pays.
