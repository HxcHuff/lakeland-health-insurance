Health ProtectorGuard — Guard 4000

Guard 4000 is a Health ProtectorGuard Guard option that pays a $4,000 fixed benefit for a covered hospital admission, plus limited scheduled amounts for listed hospital, doctor, surgical, and other services. It does not include prescription drug or outpatient imaging benefits. It is fixed-indemnity insurance, not major medical coverage, and may be worth comparing if you already have comprehensive coverage.

What to know first

  • The plan pays a set amount for each covered service, not the full medical bill.
  • It includes limited benefits for certain hospital, doctor, surgical, and other listed services.
  • It does not include prescription drug or outpatient imaging benefits.
  • Guard 4000 is the lower scheduled-benefit option in the three-option Guard series.
  • Health questions, limits, exclusions, and pre-existing-condition rules apply.

The “4000” refers to a $4,000 fixed benefit for a covered hospital admission. It is not a deductible or a promise to pay your full bill.

How Guard 4000 pays

You do not need to memorize the full benefit schedule to understand the plan.

1

You receive a covered service

The service must be listed as covered in the policy.

2

The plan pays the scheduled amount

The payment is subject to the policy's rules and limits.

3

You handle any remaining balance

If the bill is higher than the benefit, you may owe the difference.

David can show you the exact benefit schedule and current rate when you compare options. The issued policy controls.

When Guard 4000 may be worth comparing

Guard 4000 may be worth comparing if you already have major medical coverage and want supplemental help with certain expenses. It is one of three Guard options with different scheduled-benefit levels.

It should not replace ACA, Medicare, Medicaid, employer, or other comprehensive health coverage. Before you apply, David can confirm the current premium, Florida ZIP-code availability, medical underwriting, benefit limits, and exclusions.

How it differs from the other Guard options

Guard 4000 is the lower scheduled-benefit Guard option. Guard 5000 and Guard 6000 provide higher scheduled benefits for some covered services, and current materials list prescription and outpatient imaging benefits on those two options.

The right option depends on the care you expect to use, the coverage you already have, current rates, and what you could still owe.

Frequently asked questions

Is Health ProtectorGuard real insurance?

Yes. Health ProtectorGuard is fixed-indemnity insurance underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company, a UnitedHealthcare company. It pays limited, scheduled amounts for covered services. It is not major medical insurance and is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA.

Does it cover pre-existing conditions?

Current materials describe a 12-month lookback and a pre-existing-condition limitation that can apply for up to 12 months after coverage begins. The issued policy controls.

Is Guard 4000 major medical insurance?

No. Guard 4000 is fixed-indemnity insurance that pays limited, set benefits for covered services. It is not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA and should not replace comprehensive health insurance.

Will Guard 4000 pay my full medical bill?

Not necessarily. If the provider's charge is more than the plan's scheduled benefit, you may owe the difference.

Do health questions and pre-existing-condition rules apply?

Yes. The plan is subject to medical underwriting. Current materials describe a 12-month lookback and a pre-existing-condition limitation that can apply for up to 12 months after coverage begins. The issued policy controls.
