Frequently asked questions

Is Guard 4000 ACA-compliant?

No. It is fixed-indemnity insurance, not ACA major medical. Review it only as supplemental coverage and compare the limitations of every coverage component.

Why is there no Rx benefit on Guard 4000?

The carrier's benefit schedule lists no prescription drug or outpatient imaging benefit for Guard 4000. Guard 5000, Guard 6000, and the Core-series options list different scheduled benefits; compare current rates and complete terms rather than assuming which option costs less.

Does Guard 4000 cover pre-existing conditions?

A 12-month lookback applies. Conditions you received care for in the 12 months before the policy effective date are generally not covered during the first 12 months.

Is Guard 4000 available in Polk County?

The current carrier brochure lists Florida and policy form HPG3-CSTM-GRI-09. VERIFICATION REQUIRED: confirm current availability for the applicant's ZIP code through the carrier quote system before applying.
