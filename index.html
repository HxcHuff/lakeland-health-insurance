Common Questions

Health Insurance Questions for Lakeland Residents

How do I get health insurance in Lakeland, FL?

Lakeland residents can enroll in health insurance through the ACA Marketplace (Healthcare.gov), Medicare (if eligible), or private plans. A licensed local broker can compare options from multiple carriers at no cost to you. Call 863-640-3102 or visit the help form to start.

What does a health insurance broker cost in Florida?

A licensed health insurance broker in Florida costs nothing to the consumer. Brokers are compensated by insurance carriers, not by clients. You pay the same premium whether you enroll directly or through a broker — but a broker compares plans from multiple carriers to find the best fit for your budget and doctors.

When is open enrollment for health insurance in Florida?

ACA Open Enrollment in Florida typically runs from November 1 through January 15 each year. Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7. Outside these windows, you may still qualify for a Special Enrollment Period if you experience a qualifying life event such as losing job-based coverage, getting married, having a baby, or moving.

What is the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medicare Supplement?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with a bundled plan that often includes prescription drugs, dental, and vision — usually with low or $0 premiums but network restrictions. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) works alongside Original Medicare to cover out-of-pocket costs, with higher premiums but freedom to see any Medicare-accepting provider.

What health insurance plans are available in Polk County?

Polk County residents have access to ACA Marketplace plans from carriers including Ambetter, Molina, Oscar, UnitedHealthcare, and Wellpoint. Medicare beneficiaries can choose from Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement, and Part D plans. Supplemental options include dental, vision, life insurance, and fixed-indemnity Guard plans.
