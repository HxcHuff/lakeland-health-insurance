Questions West Virginia shoppers ask

Where do West Virginia residents sign up for Marketplace coverage in 2027?

West Virginia uses HealthCare.gov, the federal Marketplace. You can apply on HealthCare.gov, with help from a licensed agent who is registered for the plan year, or on an authorized enrollment partner site. Premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions apply only to plans bought through the Marketplace.

What are the 2027 open enrollment dates in West Virginia?

HealthCare.gov Open Enrollment for 2027 coverage starts November 1, 2026. December 15, 2026 is the last day to enroll or change plans for coverage that starts January 1, 2027. Open Enrollment closes January 15, 2027, and selections from December 16 through January 15 generally take effect February 1, 2027.

Does West Virginia have Medicaid expansion, or should I check HealthCare.gov?

West Virginia has adopted ACA Medicaid expansion for eligible adults ages 19 to 64 with household incomes up to 138% of the federal poverty level. If your income is at or below that threshold, check WV Medicaid at bms.wv.gov, as your state Medicaid agency makes the final eligibility decision. Above that level, HealthCare.gov determines Marketplace premium tax credit eligibility.

How do 2027 Marketplace income rules and hospital network checks work in West Virginia?

For 2026 and 2027 coverage, federal premium tax credits are generally limited to households up to 400% of the federal poverty level, and there is no cap on repaying excess advance credits at tax time if your income ends up higher than estimated. Checking WVU Medicine, Vandalia Health (CAMC and Mon Health), and Marshall Health Network against exact 2027 plan IDs is essential before automatic renewal.
