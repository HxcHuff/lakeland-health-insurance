Medicare Advantage

Medicare Advantage is another way to receive Medicare-covered benefits through a private plan. Availability, network, cost sharing, supplemental benefits, and drug coverage vary by exact plan, service area, and year. Continue paying the Part B premium unless an applicable assistance program pays it.

Original Medicare, Medigap, and Part D

Original Medicare can be paired with a separate Part D drug plan and, if eligible, a Medicare Supplement policy. Medigap availability, underwriting, guaranteed-issue rights, premiums, and enrollment timing must be reviewed for the individual situation.

2027 plan-review checklist

  1. Identify the enrollment period and intended effective date.
  2. When 2027 information is available, compare the current plan’s Annual Notice of Change with the new plan documents.
  3. Check each provider and facility by exact location and plan ID.
  4. Check each prescription, tier, restriction, and pharmacy network.
  5. Compare premiums, deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and maximum out-of-pocket exposure.
  6. Review referrals, prior authorization, travel, durable medical equipment, therapy, and other care-specific needs.
  7. Do not disenroll or cancel existing coverage until the new coverage and effective date are confirmed.

Local system names such as Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, BayCare, or Orlando Health do not establish participation. Use the provider and prescription checklist.

Appointment and marketing safeguards

CMS’s contract year 2027 training guidance states that a Scope of Appointment is required before interactions that meet the personal marketing appointment definition. The prior 48-hour waiting period was removed; an in-person marketing appointment requires a written Scope of Appointment. Individual marketing, sales, and enrollment calls between a TPMO and a beneficiary must be recorded in their entirety under the cited guidance.

These requirements govern the sales process; they do not mean that every educational question is an enrollment appointment. Current CMS, carrier, and compliance requirements still apply.

Frequently asked questions

When is Medicare Annual Enrollment for 2027 coverage?

Medicare Annual Enrollment runs October 15 through December 7, 2026 for changes that generally take effect January 1, 2027.

How should I compare Medicare plans in Polk County?

Compare the exact plan available for the applicable ZIP code and year using provider and facility networks, prescription formulary, pharmacy network, premiums, deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and maximum out-of-pocket exposure.

Does Medicare require a Scope of Appointment?

For contract year 2027, CMS training guidance says a Scope of Appointment is required before interactions that meet the personal marketing appointment definition. CMS removed the prior 48-hour waiting period and requires a written Scope of Appointment for in-person marketing appointments.

Does Lakeland Health Insurance offer every Medicare plan?

No. Lakeland Health Insurance does not offer every plan available in every area. Medicare.gov, 1-800-MEDICARE, and SHIP can provide information about all available options.
