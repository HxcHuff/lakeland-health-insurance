What AccidentWise is

AccidentWise is accidental-injury insurance underwritten by Golden Rule Insurance Company, a UnitedHealthcare Individual & Family company. Current materials describe it as fixed-benefit coverage for covered accidents only: if a listed accidental injury occurs and the claim is payable, the policy may pay a set cash amount.

That cash can help with deductibles, copays, follow-up visits, or other costs that remain after an accident. It does not turn the policy into major medical insurance, Medicare coverage, ACA-compliant health insurance, or a substitute for primary coverage.

Current product materials describe scheduled cash benefits that can include hospital admission, emergency-room care, urgent care, major diagnostic exams, follow-up or physical therapy visits subject to visit limits, outpatient surgery, and accidental loss of life, limb, or sight. Amounts scale by plan tier. Sickness and disease are outside this accident benefit.

Current materials do not describe medical underwriting. Incorrect or incomplete application information may still void coverage or affect claims. Current materials also describe the coverage as guaranteed renewable until listed termination triggers, with premiums not increased solely because of claims or a change in health. Florida rate-increase notices are described as at least 45 days. The issued policy controls.

Who it may fit

AccidentWise may be worth comparing if you already have ACA Marketplace, employer, Medicare, Medicare Advantage, or other comprehensive coverage, and want a separate cash benefit if a covered accidental injury happens.

It is a weaker fit if you are looking for major medical insurance, a Medicare replacement, ACA-compliant coverage, Minimum Essential Coverage, or a policy that pays for sickness or disease. Confirm household eligibility, including a spouse or domestic partner and dependent children, on the current Florida application.

How accident insurance differs from major medical, Medigap, and hospital indemnity

Major medical or Medigap

  • Major medical is designed as primary health coverage for a wide range of care, including sickness.
  • Medigap helps with cost sharing on services Medicare already covers.
  • Those products are not limited-benefit accident policies.

AccidentWise accident insurance

  • Pays a stated cash amount for a covered accidental injury or other listed accident event.
  • Does not replace major medical, Medicare, Medigap, Medicare Advantage, or ACA coverage.
  • Does not make the policy comprehensive, and it is not Minimum Essential Coverage.

Hospital indemnity, such as AdvantageGuard, is tied to covered hospital stays rather than accident-only events. Health ProtectorGuard pays scheduled benefits for listed medical services and is a different Golden Rule product. AccidentWise is the accident-only cash-benefit path.

Frequently asked questions

Is AccidentWise major medical or Minimum Essential Coverage?

No. AccidentWise is a limited-benefit accident product. It pays scheduled cash benefits for covered accidental injuries. It is not comprehensive health insurance, not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA, and not a substitute for primary coverage.

Will AccidentWise pay my full medical bill after an accident?

Not necessarily. The policy pays a stated cash amount for a covered accidental injury or listed event. If the medical bill is higher than the benefit, you may still owe the difference.

Who may apply for AccidentWise?

Current product materials list issue ages 18 through 75 for the primary applicant, 16 through 75 for a spouse or domestic partner, and eligible dependent children generally under age 26, subject to state definitions. Florida availability still must be verified.

Is there a waiting period, and how soon must treatment occur?

Current product materials do not describe a waiting period for standard accident benefits. They do describe treatment timing windows, including emergency-room or urgent-care treatment within 72 hours, hospital admission, major diagnostic exams, and outpatient surgery within 30 days, and accidental loss of life, limb, or sight within 90 days. The issued policy controls.

Does AccidentWise cover sickness or disease?

No. Current materials describe accidental-injury coverage. Sickness, disease, and other listed exclusions are outside that accident benefit. The issued policy controls the full exclusion list.
