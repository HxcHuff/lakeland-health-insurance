Government & Healthcare Resources

Official government websites for healthcare enrollment, Medicare information, and public health resources.

🏛
Healthcare.gov

The official Health Insurance Marketplace. Enroll in ACA plans, check if you qualify for subsidies, and compare coverage options during Open Enrollment or a Special Enrollment Period.

Visit Healthcare.gov →
🏥
CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services)

The federal agency that administers Medicare, Medicaid, and the ACA marketplace. Find official program information, coverage rules, and enrollment details.

Visit CMS.gov →
📋
Medicare.gov

The official U.S. government site for Medicare. Compare Medicare Advantage plans, Part D prescription drug plans, find doctors, and learn about your Medicare benefits.

Visit Medicare.gov →
🏢
U.S. Department of Health & Human Services

The principal federal agency protecting the health of all Americans. Access public health information, policy updates, and consumer resources.

Visit HHS.gov →
🌴
Florida Department of Health

Florida's state health department. Find local health resources, immunization information, vital records, and public health programs specific to Florida residents.

Visit FloridaHealth.gov →

Florida Office of Insurance Regulation

File insurance complaints, verify agent licenses, and learn about your rights as a Florida insurance consumer. Your go-to for regulatory questions.
