Where Washington shoppers get tripped up in 2027

  • 22.2% average 2027 Exchange rate hike & Providence Health Plan exit. The Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner approved a 22.2% average 2027 Exchange rate increase across 12 insurers after Providence Health Plan exited the individual market—while Premera Blue Cross expanded individual plans into 15 new Washington counties (24 counties total) and Regence BlueShield filed a +6.7% average adjustment.
  • Standardized Cascade Care benefits do not mean identical hospital networks. Cascade Care and Cascade Select plans standardize deductibles and office copays, but every insurer builds county-locked EPO or HMO networks across King, Pierce, Snohomish, Spokane, Yakima, and Clark counties. Always check facility and physician NPIs for UW Medicine, Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center, Providence Swedish, MultiCare, Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, and Optum clinics before renewing.
  • Regional Blue brands split by Washington county. Depending on where you live, Blue Cross / Blue Shield plans are issued separately by Premera Blue Cross / LifeWise, Regence BlueShield, Asuris Northwest Health (off-exchange in Spokane and Eastern Washington), or Regence BlueCross BlueShield of Oregon (Clark County).
  • Cascade Care Savings & Washington's 3-month short-term medical cap. Eligible households up to 250% FPL only unlock state Cascade Care Savings on qualifying Cascade Care Silver or Gold plans on Washington Healthplanfinder, and Washington OIC rules cap short-term medical policies at 3 months with no renewals.

Questions Washington shoppers ask

Where do Washington residents sign up for Marketplace coverage in 2027?

Washington operates its own state-based exchange, Washington Healthplanfinder (wahealthplanfinder.org), instead of HealthCare.gov. Federal advance premium tax credits, cost-sharing reductions, and Cascade Care Savings state premium assistance apply only to qualified health plans bought through Washington Healthplanfinder.

What are the 2027 open enrollment dates in Washington?

Open Enrollment on Washington Healthplanfinder for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027. Complete your enrollment or plan change by December 15, 2026 for coverage starting January 1, 2027; selections from December 16, 2026 through January 15, 2027 generally begin February 1, 2027.

What are Cascade Care and Cascade Care Savings for 2027?

Cascade Care plans (including Cascade Select public-option plans) standardize deductibles and copays across Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers on Washington Healthplanfinder so you can compare carrier networks directly. Cascade Care Savings provides state-funded monthly premium assistance on top of federal tax credits for eligible Washington households up to 250% of the federal poverty level who enroll in a qualifying Cascade Care Silver or Gold plan.

How do Washington Apple Health Medicaid and short-term medical rules work in Washington?

Washington expanded adult Medicaid through Washington Apple Health, administered by the Washington State Health Care Authority (hca.wa.gov), covering eligible adults ages 19 to 64 up to 138% of the federal poverty level year-round on wahealthplanfinder.org; your state Medicaid agency makes the final eligibility decision. Under Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner rules, short-term limited-duration medical plans in Washington are capped at 3 months and non-renewable.
