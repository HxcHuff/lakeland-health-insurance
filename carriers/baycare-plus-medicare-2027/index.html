BayCarePlus Medicare Advantage in Polk County

BayCarePlus is a provider-sponsored Medicare Advantage plan designed around the BayCare Health System. For beneficiaries who receive care primarily at Winter Haven Hospital, Bartow Regional Medical Center, or through BayCare Medical Group, here is what you need to know for 2027.

Key AEP Takeaway: BayCarePlus provides deep integration with BayCare doctors and hospitals in East Polk. Review your doctor roster if you also see specialists at Watson Clinic or Lakeland Regional Health.

Understanding BayCarePlus Medicare Advantage (2027)

Provider-sponsored plans like BayCarePlus coordinate closely between insurance administration and clinical facilities. Key considerations for 2027 include:

  • Core Hospital Network: Winter Haven Hospital, Winter Haven Women’s Hospital, Bartow Regional Medical Center, and BayCare surgery centers form the hospital foundation in Polk County.
  • Physician Alignment: BayCare Medical Group physicians and allied independent practices participate. Primary care doctor assignment is required on HMO products.
  • $2,400 Part D Drug Cap: Prescription benefits reflect the federal $2,400 annual maximum out-of-pocket limit under the Inflation Reduction Act. Preferred pharmacy networks should be checked against your preferred local pharmacy.

When Does BayCarePlus Fit Best?

BayCarePlus is often a strong candidate for residents in Winter Haven, Bartow, Auburndale, Lake Wales, and Haines City who already have established primary care and specialist relationships within the BayCare medical community. However, if your medical care requires routine visits to specialists tied exclusively to Lakeland Regional Health or Watson Clinic, an independent review can help evaluate whether a broader PPO or Medigap plan provides better overall access without out-of-network surprises.

Common Questions About BayCarePlus

What hospitals are included in the BayCarePlus network in Polk County?

BayCarePlus is offered by BayCare Health Plans and is built around the BayCare Health System. In Polk County, this includes Winter Haven Hospital, Winter Haven Women's Hospital, and Bartow Regional Medical Center, in addition to BayCare hospitals across Hillsborough and Pinellas counties.

Can I see doctors outside of BayCare on a BayCarePlus plan?

BayCarePlus plans in Polk County are primarily HMO designs, which require members to receive non-emergency care from in-network providers and specialists with coordinated referrals. If you regularly use physicians affiliated with Lakeland Regional Health or Watson Clinic, verify whether those doctors participate or consider a plan with broader network access.

How does the $2,400 Part D prescription drug cap work with BayCarePlus?

All 2027 BayCarePlus plans with prescription drug coverage incorporate the federal $2,400 annual out-of-pocket cap on covered Part D medications under the Inflation Reduction Act. Once your out-of-pocket prescription expenses reach $2,400, you pay $0 for covered drugs for the remainder of the calendar year.
