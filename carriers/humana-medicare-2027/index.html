Humana Medicare Plans in Lakeland & Polk County

Humana is one of the most widely held Medicare Advantage and prescription drug carriers across Central Florida. Here is how to evaluate 2027 Humana HMO, PPO, and CarePlus coverage against your specific doctors and medications before the December 7 AEP deadline.

Humana Medicare Advantage Options in Polk County (2027)

For plan year 2027, Humana offers multiple Medicare Advantage (Part C) plan structures to Polk County residents, including Humana Gold Plus HMO plans, Humana Choice PPO plans, and plans coordinated with its subsidiary, CarePlus Health Plans. When reviewing these options, consider the following primary factors:

  • Provider Networks: Humana HMO plans require staying within designated network hospitals, physician groups, and outpatient centers. PPO options allow out-of-network care at higher cost-sharing.
  • Prescription Formularies & $2,400 Cap: All 2027 Humana MAPD plans incorporate the Inflation Reduction Act’s $2,400 out-of-pocket pharmacy cap. Check formulary tiers for your specific maintenance medications and dosage levels.
  • Ancillary Allowances: Dental, vision, hearing, and over-the-counter (OTC) allowances vary substantially across plan designs. Focus on core medical protection, copays, and maximum out-of-pocket (MOOP) limits before supplemental perks.

Common Questions About Humana Medicare in Polk County

Does Watson Clinic accept Humana Medicare Advantage in 2027?

No. Watson Clinic will not be in network with Humana Medicare Advantage plans starting January 1, 2027. If you see Watson Clinic doctors, compare 2027 options before Medicare Open Enrollment ends on December 7, and confirm each doctor by name and NPI in the plan's 2027 provider directory.

How does the 2027 $2,400 Part D cap affect Humana Medicare plans?

Starting in plan year 2027, the Inflation Reduction Act caps out-of-pocket prescription drug costs under Medicare Part D at $2,400 annually. For Humana Medicare Advantage plans with prescription drug coverage (MAPD), covered prescriptions count toward this limit, and members can also enroll in the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan to spread costs across the year.

What is the difference between Humana Gold Plus HMO and Humana Choice PPO?

Humana Gold Plus HMO plans generally require you to select an in-network primary care physician and obtain referrals for specialist care within a designated provider network. Humana Choice PPO plans provide greater flexibility to see out-of-network physicians, though in-network care carries lower copayments and deductibles.

Can I switch to or from a Humana Medicare plan during AEP?

Yes. The Medicare Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs from October 15 through December 7, 2026. Any changes made during AEP take effect on January 1, 2027. Outside of AEP, you may need a qualifying Special Enrollment Period or utilize the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (January 1 – March 31).
