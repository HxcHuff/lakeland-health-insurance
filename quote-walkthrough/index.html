What to know before you apply

  • This page and the video are an outline, not a policy. The issued policy controls.
  • Guard 5000 is Enhanced Health ProtectorGuard fixed-indemnity coverage. It pays limited, set benefits for covered services. It is not comprehensive health insurance, not dental insurance, not major medical insurance, and not Minimum Essential Coverage under the ACA.
  • TriTerm Medical Co-Pay Select Max is short-term medical insurance. It is not Minimum Essential Coverage, is medically underwritten, and applies a 12-month pre-existing condition limitation. It is designed for nearly three years of temporary coverage across three terms when issued and kept in force. Copay Select Max uses the UnitedHealthcare Choice network and generally does not pay non-network benefits except emergency treatment, subject to policy terms.
  • DentalWise Plan 2000 is dental coverage. It does not replace medical insurance.
  • Availability, benefits, and rates vary by ZIP code, underwriting, and plan year.
