Lake Alfred FAQs

What should I have ready before a plan review?

Have your doctors, preferred pharmacy, prescription list, current plan card if you have one, household size, estimated annual income, and target coverage start date.

Can I enroll outside Open Enrollment?

Possibly. ACA coverage outside Open Enrollment usually requires a Special Enrollment Period, such as losing employer coverage, moving, marriage, birth, or aging off a parent's plan. Medicare has separate timing rules.

Does using a broker change my premium?

No. You pay the same plan premium whether you enroll directly or with broker help. The difference is that you get plan review, enrollment guidance, and follow-up support.
