ACA and Medicare Enrollment Timing

Coverage in Lake Alfred is not a year-round open catalog. The window has to match the product:

  • ACA Open Enrollment — For plan year 2027, Florida uses HealthCare.gov. Open Enrollment runs November 1 through December 15, 2026, with January 1, 2027 coverage for completed eligible enrollments.
  • ACA Special Enrollment Periods — Losing employer coverage, moving to or within Florida, marriage, a new dependent, or aging off a parent's plan may open a window. Many SEP periods are 60 days from the event and need documentation.
  • Medicare Open Enrollment — October 15 through December 7 for eligible Medicare Advantage and Part D changes, generally effective January 1.
  • Turning 65 — Initial Medicare enrollment is a separate window around your 65th birthday. Medigap open enrollment is also separate from the fall Medicare window.

If you are not sure which calendar applies, start with the coverage type and the date your current plan ends. I will map the window before we compare networks.
