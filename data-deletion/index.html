Section 02

How to request deletion

To ask us to delete personal data collected through Facebook Messenger, Facebook or Instagram lead ads, or this site's forms, contact us by email at david@lakelandhealthinsurance.com or by phone at (863) 640-3102.

Deleting the data we hold does not delete data Facebook or Instagram keep; manage that in your Meta account settings.

Use the subject line “Data Deletion Request” if you email. Do not include a Social Security number, Medicare ID, or other sensitive identifiers in the first message.

Section 03

What to include

Please include:

  • Your name
  • The phone number or email address you used
  • The source, such as Facebook Messenger, a Facebook or Instagram lead ad, or a website form
Section 04

Response time

We aim to complete a verified deletion request within 30 days. We may need to confirm identity before acting.

Section 05

What we delete

When a request is verified, we delete:

  • Contact details and lead form answers held in our CRM (HubSpot)
  • Message history
  • Marketing lists

Marketing lists are removed. A minimal opt-out record is kept so we do not text, call, or email you again. See what may be retained below.

Section 06

What may be retained

We may keep some records when law, regulation, or a carrier, Medicare, or Marketplace rule requires it — for example, a submitted application or enrollment, Medicare Scope of Appointment and permission-to-contact records, and recorded Medicare sales calls — for the period those rules require. We also keep a minimal opt-out record (your phone number or email and the date you opted out) so we do not text, call, or email you again. We do not use retained records for marketing.

Section 07

How to stop texts and email

To stop texts, reply STOP to any text message.

To stop email, use the unsubscribe link in a marketing email or email david@lakelandhealthinsurance.com.

See also the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
