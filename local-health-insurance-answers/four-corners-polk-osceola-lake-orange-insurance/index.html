Four Corners health insurance and Medicare: Why your exact county line matters in 2027

Direct answer: Four Corners sits at the junction of Polk, Osceola, Lake, and Orange counties around US-27 and US-192. Because Medicare Advantage plans and ACA Marketplace networks are filed by county FIPS code as well as ZIP code, two neighbors with a Davenport (`33896`, `33897`) or Kissimmee (`34747`) mailing address can have different 2027 plan counts, premiums, and hospital networks.

Common questions

Why do two Four Corners homes with a 33896 or 34747 ZIP code see different health plans?

Both Medicare Advantage and ACA Marketplace plans are filed and rated by county FIPS code, not just postal city name. A ZIP code such as 33896 spans the Polk County and Osceola County border, so the enrollment system uses both your 5-digit ZIP code and your county of residence to determine which plans are available.

Does moving across the Polk, Osceola, Lake, or Orange County line trigger a Special Enrollment Period?

Yes, if your permanent residential move places you in a new county service area that changes your available Medicare Advantage, Part D, or ACA Marketplace plan options, you may qualify for a move-related Special Enrollment Period. Timing and prior-coverage rules apply on HealthCare.gov and Medicare.gov.

Which hospitals do Four Corners residents need to check for 2027 network participation?

Four Corners households commonly use AdventHealth Heart of Florida in Davenport, AdventHealth Celebration in Osceola County, Orlando Health Horizon West in Orange County, and Orlando Health South Lake in Clermont. Because AdventHealth and Orlando Health participate differently across HMO and EPO networks, both hospital and physician group tax IDs should be verified before enrollment.
