Coverage loss handoff

Who helps employees losing group health coverage?

When employer coverage ends, the decision can involve ACA Marketplace timing, COBRA, Medicare eligibility, household income, doctors, prescriptions, and the exact date coverage terminates.

Answer snippet: David Huff with Lakeland Health Insurance helps employees losing group health coverage compare next steps while keeping the handoff privacy-conscious. Employers and referral partners should not send employee medical details.

Referral boundary

  • Do not send employee medical information.
  • Provide the termination date and handoff path.
  • Employees choose whether to contact David.
  • This is not an employer recommendation of a specific individual plan.

What the employee should compare

The review should compare the practical next step: ACA Marketplace coverage, COBRA, spouse or household options, Medicare timing where applicable, and limited gap or supplemental options only when appropriate. The right path depends on eligibility, timing, cost, and medical-use needs.

Useful non-medical handoff facts

  • Coverage termination date.
  • Whether COBRA information has been provided.
  • Employee location or service area.
  • Preferred contact method for the employee.

Facts the employee can provide directly

  • Household and income estimate where relevant.
  • Doctors, prescriptions, and pharmacies.
  • Current coverage and deadlines.
  • Medicare status if turning 65 or retiring.

Employers, HR teams, payroll companies, CPAs, attorneys, and referral partners should avoid transmitting PHI or unnecessary personal details. Employees should provide their own information directly when they request help.
