How the 2027 IRS 10.22% Family Affordability Rule Works for Polk County Workers

Across Polk County's largest employers (including retail and distribution centers, public schools, municipal agencies, I-4 logistics warehouses, and medical systems), employers are only required under the ACA employer mandate to make self-only (employee-only) coverage affordable. As a result, an employee might pay $85/month for self-only coverage, but $780/month to add a spouse and two children.

For 2027 plan years, IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-26 sets the affordability threshold at 10.22% of projected household MAGI. HealthCare.gov runs two separate tests:

  • Test 1 (The Employee): Does the lowest-cost self-only plan offered by the employer cost more than 10.22% of annual household income? Usually no, which means the employee should keep the low-cost employer plan.
  • Test 2 (The Spouse & Children): Does the lowest-cost employer plan that covers the entire family cost more than 10.22% of annual household income? If yes (and the family is between 100% and 400% FPL), the spouse and children can decline the employer family tier and apply for subsidized Marketplace coverage on HealthCare.gov.

ACA Family Plans vs. Florida KidCare (MediKids & Healthy Kids)

When a Polk County family applies on HealthCare.gov with children under age 19, the federal application automatically checks whether the children fall within Florida's CHIP / Florida KidCare income band (generally up to roughly 215% of the Federal Poverty Level):

  • Under ~215% FPL: HealthCare.gov may route the children's file to Florida KidCare (MediKids for ages 1 to 4 or Florida Healthy Kids for ages 5 to 18) while issuing an Advance Premium Tax Credit for the adult parent(s) to enroll in a Silver CSR Marketplace plan.
  • Between ~215% and 400% FPL: Both parents and children generally qualify together on the same subsidized HealthCare.gov family policy, sharing one family deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my spouse and children get a 2027 Marketplace subsidy if my employer plan in Polk County is too expensive to add family?

Possibly. Under IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-26, for plan years beginning in 2027, HealthCare.gov tests the employee-only premium and the total family premium separately against 10.22% of projected household income. If employee-only coverage is under 10.22% of household income but the lowest-cost employer plan covering the family exceeds 10.22% of household income, the employee stays on the job plan while the spouse and dependents may qualify for Marketplace tax credits.

How do Florida KidCare and HealthCare.gov interact for Polk County children in 2027?

When a Florida family applies on HealthCare.gov, children in households below the state CHIP / Florida KidCare income threshold (generally around 215% of the Federal Poverty Level) may be referred to Florida KidCare (MediKids or Florida Healthy Kids) rather than receiving an ACA tax credit on the parent's Marketplace policy. Parents between 100% and 400% FPL can still enroll in a subsidized Marketplace plan for themselves.

Which pediatric and maternity hospitals in Polk County should families verify on a 2027 ACA plan?

Polk County families commonly check the Carol Jenkins Barnett Pavilion for Women and Children at Lakeland Regional Health, Johns Hopkins All Children's Outpatient Care in Lakeland, Winter Haven Women's Hospital (BayCare), Orlando Health Lakeland Highlands Hospital, Watson Clinic Pediatrics, Bond Clinic Pediatrics, and Nemours Children's Health. Always verify both the hospital facility and the pediatric or OB-GYN group on the exact 2027 plan ID.

What are the 2027 Florida ACA income limits for a family of 3, 4, or 5 in Polk County?

Using the 2026 HHS Federal Poverty Guidelines for 2027 coverage, the 100% to 400% FPL range is $27,320 to $109,280 for a family of three, $33,000 to $132,000 for a family of four, and $38,680 to $154,720 for a family of five.
