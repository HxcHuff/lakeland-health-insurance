  • Required Medicare permission and scope.

  • County and current coverage.

What a Medicare review should cover

A Medicare review should compare the tradeoffs among Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement, and Part D without assuming one structure is best. Network rules, referral requirements, prior authorization, prescription costs, dental or vision extras, and travel needs can change the answer.

Common timing triggers

  • Turning 65.
  • Retiring or losing employer coverage.
  • Moving counties.
  • Annual Enrollment Period.
  • Medicaid or LIS changes.

Local checks

  • Watson Clinic providers.
  • Lakeland Regional Health access.
  • Orlando Health or BayCare relationships.
  • Preferred pharmacies and drug tiers.
  • Exact plan IDs, not just carrier names.
