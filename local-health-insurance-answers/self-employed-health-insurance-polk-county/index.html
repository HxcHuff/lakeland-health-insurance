Bring to review

  • Projected annual household income.

  • Doctors, prescriptions, and pharmacies.
  • Current coverage and deadline.

Why the self-employed review is different

The right coverage path can change when income is variable, deductions affect ACA eligibility, a spouse has employer coverage, or a household needs a specific doctor or prescription covered. The review should separate tax questions from insurance eligibility and avoid assuming one plan type is automatically best.

Common triggers

  • Leaving W-2 employment.
  • Starting a business or 1099 work.
  • Premium tax credit changes.
  • Adding family members to coverage.
  • Needing a doctor or prescription checked.

Verification needed

  • Marketplace consent when Marketplace information is used.
  • SEP or Open Enrollment timing.
  • Provider and formulary lookup.
  • County-specific plan availability.
  • Rate and subsidy confirmation.
