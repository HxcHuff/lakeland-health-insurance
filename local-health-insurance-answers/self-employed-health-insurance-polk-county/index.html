Common triggers

  • Leaving W-2 employment.
  • Starting a business or 1099 work.
  • Premium tax credit changes.
  • Adding family members to coverage.
  • Needing a doctor or prescription checked.

Verification needed

  • Marketplace consent when Marketplace information is used.
  • Special Enrollment Period eligibility or the current Open Enrollment window.
  • Provider and formulary lookup.
  • County-specific plan availability.
  • Rate and subsidy confirmation.

How to prepare a supportable comparison

Use a good-faith full-year household income estimate rather than a single high or low month. Keep the business records used for the estimate, update the Marketplace when the estimate materially changes, and compare the plan's deductible and maximum out-of-pocket with the household's expected care—not just the monthly premium.
