How to use this hub

This is a directory of Florida-specific answers, not a substitute for a plan review. Start with the life event that created the question. A parent-plan end date, a retirement date, a 1099 income year, a Medicaid notice, and a Medicare birthday are different calendars. Opening every product page at once usually hides the deadline that actually matters.

Local review in Lakeland and Polk County also means checking the care you already use. Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, BayCare, and Orlando Health do not sit in every network the same way. Frequently asked questions

Is Lakeland Health Insurance a government office or an insurance carrier?

No. Lakeland Health Insurance is operated by David Huff, a licensed Florida health insurance broker. It is not a government agency and not an insurance carrier. Plan availability and eligibility must be verified before enrollment.

Which page should I open first?

Open the page that matches the insurance trigger: aging off a parent plan, retiring before 65, self-employment, group coverage ending, Medicare, Medicaid ending, or a local network check. Use this hub to choose the path, then complete a plan-specific review before enrollment.
