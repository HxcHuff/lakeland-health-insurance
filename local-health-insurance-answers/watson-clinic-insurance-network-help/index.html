Why local network checks matter

A familiar brand does not guarantee every part of care is covered the same way. A primary care office, specialist, hospital, lab, imaging center, outpatient surgery location, and pharmacy can each be treated differently by a specific ACA or Medicare plan. Network status can differ by plan; check the plan's provider directory before enrolling.

ACA shoppers

  • Confirm the exact Marketplace or off-exchange plan.
  • Check HMO, EPO, or PPO-style rules.
  • Verify doctors, prescriptions, and pharmacies before enrollment.

Medicare shoppers

  • Confirm county, election period, and plan year. Medicare reviews on this site are for Florida residents.
  • Check primary doctor assignment and specialist access.
  • Compare Medicare Advantage rules against Medigap plus Part D where appropriate.

2027 enrollment dates

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. The plan must receive the request by December 7. The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period runs January 1 to March 31 for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan.

Marketplace Open Enrollment starts November 1. Enroll by December 15 for January 1 coverage. Open Enrollment ends January 15, with February 1 coverage for December 16 to January 15 enrollments.

Common questions

How do I check whether my Watson Clinic doctor is in my plan?

Search for each doctor by name and office location in your plan's provider directory for next year, not just the Watson Clinic name. Then call the office with your plan name and ID to confirm before you enroll.

Does a hospital or clinic name prove every doctor is in network?

No. A clinic, its specialists, its urgent care sites, the hospital, labs, and imaging can each be treated differently by the same plan. Check each provider and location you use in the exact plan and plan year.

Does Watson Clinic urgent care take my insurance?

It depends on your plan. Look up the specific Watson Clinic urgent care or XpressCare location in your plan's directory and call before you go. Your plan sets urgent care cost-sharing separately from emergency room cost-sharing.

When can I change plans to keep my Watson Clinic doctors?

Medicare Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. Marketplace Open Enrollment runs November 1 to January 15, and you should enroll by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1.

Who can help check Watson Clinic networks for a specific plan?

A licensed Florida health agent can check your doctors, locations, and prescriptions against specific ACA or Medicare plans before you enroll. You can also call Watson Clinic and the plan directly to confirm.

Does Watson Clinic take Humana in 2027?

No. Starting January 1, 2027, Watson Clinic will no longer accept Humana plans. Confirm current participation with the clinic and your plan. If you have a Humana plan and see Watson Clinic doctors, talk with David Huff, a local licensed agent, before Medicare Open Enrollment ends December 7.
