Why Entering Income Below $15,960 Triggers a Subsidy Denial in Florida

When the Affordable Care Act was written, Congress assumed states would expand Medicaid up to 138% FPL, so federal Marketplace tax credits were set to begin at 100% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL). Because Florida chose not to expand adult Medicaid, a coverage gap exists below 100% FPL for non-disabled, non-pregnant adults.

However, many Polk County residents (seasonal hospitality workers, part-time retail or warehouse workers, self-employed 1099 contractors, students with part-time jobs, or people between jobs) accidentally enter only a single slow month's pay instead of their full-year 2027 projected household MAGI. If your good-faith full-year 2027 household MAGI reaches at least 100% FPL ($15,960 for 1 person, $21,640 for 2 people), you qualify for both Advance Premium Tax Credits and the 94% Actuarial Value Silver CSR tier.

What Lawfully Counts in 2027 Household MAGI

When projecting your full-year 2027 Modified Adjusted Gross Income on HealthCare.gov, remember to include all countable income for everyone on your federal tax return:

  • Gross W-2 wages, salaries, tips, and seasonal/overtime pay expected across all 12 months of 2027.
  • Net Schedule C self-employment, gig work, lawn/handyman, delivery, or freelance profit.
  • Taxable IRA or 401(k) distributions and pensions.
  • Unemployment compensation and interest/dividends.
  • Both taxable and non-taxable Social Security benefits of tax-household members.

Documentation & Data-Matching Checks (DMI)

If your projected 2027 income is higher than your prior-year tax return (for example, because you are picking up additional hours, starting 1099 work, or taking a planned taxable retirement distribution), HealthCare.gov may request income verification documents within 90 days:

  • Keep pay stubs, employer offer letters, 1099 ledger records, or retirement distribution confirmations.
  • Never fabricate income on a federal application; always base your projection on a good-faith, supportable 2027 estimate and consult a tax professional for tax-filing questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did HealthCare.gov deny a 2027 subsidy in Florida because my income was below $15,960?

Because Florida did not expand adult Medicaid under the Affordable Care Act, adults generally must project annual household income of at least 100% of the Federal Poverty Level to qualify for Advance Premium Tax Credits on HealthCare.gov. For 2027 coverage (using the 2026 HHS poverty guidelines), 100% FPL is $15,960 for 1 person, $21,640 for 2 people, $27,320 for 3 people, and $33,000 for a family of 4.

What income sources count toward reaching the 100% FPL floor on a 2027 Florida Marketplace application?

Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) includes projected full-year W-2 wages, seasonal or part-time earnings, net Schedule C self-employment or gig work profit, unemployment compensation, taxable retirement or IRA withdrawals, interest, dividends, and both taxable and non-taxable Social Security benefits for everyone in the tax household.

How does a 2027 ACA 94% Silver CSR plan compare with the Polk HealthCare Plan?

An ACA 94% Actuarial Value Silver CSR plan (available between 100% and 150% FPL, or $15,960 to $23,940 for a single adult in 2027) is comprehensive commercial health insurance regulated under the ACA with network hospital and specialist access and a federal out-of-pocket cap. The Polk HealthCare Plan is a county tax-funded health program (not health insurance) administered in Bartow for uninsured Polk County residents up to 300% FPL who do not have other affordable coverage available, providing care through participating county partner clinics.

Where can uninsured Polk County residents below 100% FPL get local care?

Polk County residents whose household income remains below 100% FPL and who do not qualify for Florida Medicaid can contact the Polk HealthCare Plan Member Services office in Bartow at (863) 533-1111, Central Florida Health Care (Federally Qualified Health Center clinics across Lakeland, Winter Haven, Mulberry, Dundee, and Frostproof), or Lakeland Volunteers in Medicine.
