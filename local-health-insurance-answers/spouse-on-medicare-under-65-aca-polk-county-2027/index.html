How HealthCare.gov Calculates Tax Credits When One Spouse Has Medicare

Across Lakeland, Winter Haven, Lake Ashton, Solivita, Highland Fairways, Cypress Lakes, and Bartow, one of the most common retirement questions is what happens when an older spouse turns 65 (or retires from Publix, Polk County Public Schools, City of Lakeland, or Lakeland Regional Health) and leaves a 56- to 64-year-old spouse who is not yet eligible for Medicare.

Under IRS Section 36B and HealthCare.gov rules, three mechanics govern your 2027 application:

  1. 2-Person Tax Household Size: Married couples who file a joint federal tax return are counted as a 2-person household for Federal Poverty Level (FPL) thresholds ($21,640 = 100% FPL up to $86,560 = 400% FPL for 2027), even though only one person needs an ACA plan. (Filing Married Filing Separately generally disqualifies a household from Advance Premium Tax Credits outside specific domestic-abuse or abandonment exceptions.)
  2. Combined 2-Person MAGI: The Marketplace totals the projected 2027 Modified Adjusted Gross Income of both spouses. That includes wages, net self-employment income, pensions, taxable IRA/401(k) withdrawals, investment income, and 100% of the Medicare spouse's Social Security benefits (both the taxable portion and the non-taxable portion).
  3. 1-Person Benchmark Silver Rate Minus 2-Person IRS Expected Contribution: Your monthly tax credit is based on the Polk County Second-Lowest-Cost Silver Plan (SLCSP) for the under-65 spouse alone, minus the IRS expected monthly contribution (2.15% to 10.22% of the couple's combined 2027 MAGI under IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-26).

Protecting Against the $86,560 (400% FPL) Cliff in 2027

Because a 58- to 64-year-old faces the steepest age-rated ACA premiums in Florida, crossing $86,560 of combined 2-person MAGI by even $1,000 can eliminate $7,500 to $10,500 per year in Advance Premium Tax Credits.

  • Watch One-Time Retirement Withdrawals: Taking a lump-sum taxable 401(k) or Traditional IRA withdrawal to pay off a car, roof, or move to Polk County increases MAGI dollar for dollar.
  • Consider HSA-Eligible Bronze Plans for the Younger Spouse: Enrolling the under-65 spouse in an HSA-eligible Bronze HDHP allows pre-tax HSA contributions that directly lower household MAGI.
  • Coordinate With Your CPA: Review Roth vs. Traditional withdrawal mixes and capital-gain timing before year-end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are we a 1-person or 2-person household on HealthCare.gov if my spouse is on Medicare?

If you and your spouse file a joint federal income tax return, you are a 2-person tax household on HealthCare.gov even if your spouse is enrolled in Medicare and only you are applying for ACA Marketplace coverage. For 2027 coverage in Florida, the 2-person 100% to 400% Federal Poverty Level window is $21,640 to $86,560.

Does my Medicare spouse's Social Security or pension count toward my 2027 ACA subsidy?

Yes. Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI) on a Marketplace application includes the income of every person in the tax household, including both the taxable and non-taxable portions of your Medicare spouse's Social Security benefits, pensions, and taxable IRA or 401(k) distributions.

Does an older spouse retiring and going on Medicare create a Special Enrollment Period for the younger spouse?

Yes, when the retiring employee's group health plan ends and the younger under-65 spouse loses qualifying employer-sponsored coverage, that coverage loss generally triggers a 60-day Marketplace Special Enrollment Period. Applying before the group plan ends helps prevent a gap in coverage.

Should the under-65 spouse take COBRA or a 2027 Polk County ACA Marketplace plan?

Compare both options before electing COBRA. COBRA requires paying up to 102% of the unsubsidized group premium, whereas a 2-person household with projected 2027 MAGI between $21,640 and $86,560 may qualify for Advance Premium Tax Credits on HealthCare.gov. Also compare whether your doctors at Watson Clinic, Lakeland Regional Health, Orlando Health, or BayCare are in the Marketplace plan network.
