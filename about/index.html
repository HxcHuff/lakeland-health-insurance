Meet David

Your straight-talking, no-BS health insurance agent who actually gives a damn about your coverage.

From Marine Sergeant to Your Insurance Guardian

I'm David, former Marine Sergeant (0811 Cannoneer) turned insurance agent. Why the career change? Because watching my mother navigate the healthcare system during her disability taught me that families need someone who won't back down when insurance companies start playing games.

In the Marines, we had a saying: "Leave no one behind." That applies to insurance too. When my teenage kids got hurt playing sports, the policies I recommend covered those unexpected expenses without breaking our budget. That's when I knew I was in the right business - protecting families from financial disasters they never saw coming.

I hate seeing good people get hurt by outside forces. Nobody asks for cancer, car accidents, or medical emergencies. But when life throws you a curveball, you need someone in your corner who knows how to fight back. If insurance companies have games, I'm Milton Bradley.

Based in Central Florida but serving families nationwide. Marine-trained discipline meets real-world family experience.
