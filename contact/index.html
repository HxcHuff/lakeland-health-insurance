Keep sensitive information out of email

Do not email Social Security numbers, Medicare numbers, payment information, full medical records, policy passwords, or carrier-portal credentials. David can direct you to an appropriate secure enrollment or servicing channel when needed.

Before requesting a plan review

  • Have your Florida ZIP code, county, household size, and expected coverage date available.
  • For ACA eligibility, household income and current coverage information may be needed through an authorized secure workflow.
  • For provider or prescription review, use the exact provider location, exact plan name and ID, medication details, pharmacy, and plan year.
  • For Medicare, do not place your Medicare number in the general website form or email.
