Questions Maryland shoppers ask

Where do Maryland residents sign up for Marketplace coverage in 2027?

Maryland operates its own state-based exchange, Maryland Health Connection (marylandhealthconnection.gov), instead of HealthCare.gov. Federal advance premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions apply only to qualified health plans bought through Maryland Health Connection.

Open Enrollment on Maryland Health Connection for 2027 coverage runs November 1, 2026 through January 15, 2027. How does Maryland Medicaid expansion work with Maryland Health Connection?
