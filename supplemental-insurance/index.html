Clean distinction

Supplemental is not the same thing as fixed-benefit.

The terms get mixed together in marketing. For decision-making, keep them separate.

Traditional supplemental add-ons

  • Accident coverage
  • Critical illness or cancer coverage
  • Hospital indemnity
  • Dental, vision, and life as separate benefit categories
  • Usually layered beside a primary medical plan

Fixed-benefit coverage like HPG

  • Pays set benefits according to the schedule
  • Can be reviewed beside ACA, STM, TriTerm, or employer coverage
  • May use networks or negotiated rates depending on product design
  • Can be useful, but must be judged by the contract
  • Not the same as ACA-compliant major medical
